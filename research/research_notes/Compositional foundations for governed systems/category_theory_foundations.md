## 1. Polynomial functors / Poly (Spivak, Niu): interfaces, mode-dependence, comonoids, Org, learners' languages

### Takeaway
Poly is the single most compact candidate kernel: one category whose objects are interfaces, whose morphisms are bidirectional interaction patterns (dependent lenses), and whose four monoidal structures give parallel composition, sequencing, wiring, and (via comonoids) categories and databases themselves. Its account of self-rewiring collectives (Org, 2022; OrgTr, 2026) is real mathematics but young, example-driven, deterministic/Set-based, and has no native notion of goals, norms or authority; one paper in the hierarchical-agents series was withdrawn for an error.

### Cited Findings
- Primitives. A polynomial p = sum over positions i of y^(p[i]) (positions = outputs/states of an interface, directions = inputs accepted at that position). A morphism p -> q is a dependent lens: forward on positions, backward on directions. The book-length reference is Niu & Spivak, *Polynomial Functors: A Mathematical Theory of Interaction* (arXiv Dec 2023, rev. Aug 2024; Cambridge University Press 2025), covering limits, adjunctions, monoidal products, closures, comonoids, comodules and bicomodules. CONSTRUCTION + THEOREMS. [V] — [arXiv:2312.00990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00990); [CUP chapter list](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/polynomial-functors/polynomial-comonoids-and-retrofunctors/352E5607109BDEA94B5FC98C6E5EFDAF)
- Mode-dependent dynamics. A dynamical system with state set S and interface p is a lens S y^S -> p (equivalently a p-coalgebra S -> p(S)); because directions depend on positions, the set of admissible inputs can change with the system's current output ("mode-dependence"). A wiring diagram is itself a lens p1 (x) ... (x) pk -> q, and it too may depend on the current positions, so the wiring pattern can change with state. CONSTRUCTION. [R] — Spivak, "Poly: An abundant categorical setting for mode-dependent dynamics" (2020), [arXiv:2005.01894](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01894)
- Comonoids are categories. The category of comonoids for the composition (substitution) product in Poly is equivalent to the category of small categories with retrofunctors (cofunctors) as morphisms. THEOREM (originally Ahman & Uustalu 2016; Chapter 7 of the book). [V for the statement via CUP summary; R for the Ahman-Uustalu attribution] — [CUP ch. 7](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/polynomial-functors/polynomial-comonoids-and-retrofunctors/352E5607109BDEA94B5FC98C6E5EFDAF); Ahman & Uustalu, "Directed containers as categories", [arXiv:1604.01187](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01187)
- Further structure catalogued in one place: +, x, the parallel (Dirichlet) product with closure [p,q], the composition product, free monads and cofree comonoids, bicomodules between comonoids (which correspond to parametric right adjoints between copresheaf categories, i.e. data-migration functors), giving a double category "Cat#". THEOREMS/CONSTRUCTIONS. [R] — Spivak, "A reference for categorical structures on Poly" (2022), [arXiv:2202.00534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00534)
- Learners' languages. The Fong-Spivak-Tuyeras "learners" (morphisms A -> B that update themselves) are coalgebras for the internal-hom polynomial [A y^A, B y^B]; the category of p-coalgebras is a topos, so each interface has an internal logic in which dynamic properties (e.g. "this learner does gradient descent") can be stated. THEOREM (topos) + CONSTRUCTION. [R] — Spivak, "Learners' languages" (ACT 2021, EPTCS 372), [arXiv:2103.01189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01189)
- Org. Shapiro & Spivak define "the monoidal double category Org of dynamic organizations" and use enrichment in it to define dynamic categories, dynamic operads and dynamic monoidal categories; the two worked examples are prediction markets (a dynamic operad) and deep learning (a dynamic monoidal category). Opening sentence: "Natural organized systems adapt to internal and external pressures." CONSTRUCTION with two examples; published EPTCS 380 (ACT 2022), pp. 183-202. [V] — [arXiv:2205.03906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03906)
- 2026 extension: interfaces that themselves change. Spivak, "Interactions that reshape the interfaces of the interacting parties" (20 Feb 2026) introduces "polynomial trees" (coinductive structures where each round of interaction determines the next interface), a monoidal closed category PolyTr and a bicategory OrgTr generalising Org; constant trees embed Org fully faithfully on hom-categories; the motivating example is progressive GANs. CONSTRUCTION, single-author preprint. [V] — [arXiv:2602.17917](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917)
- Hierarchical agents series. Libkind & Spivak, "Dynamic task delegation for hierarchical agents" (Oct 2024, "fourth in a series" on hierarchy via polynomial functors) claimed that the free-monad and cofree-comonad constructions extend to (co)monads on Org, yielding Cat-enriched operads for programmatic and behavioural views. **The arXiv record shows v2 (26 Nov 2024) as withdrawn, with a comment that the relevant Kleisli category is pre-monoidal rather than monoidal, undermining a definition.** Treat its results as not established. [V] — [arXiv:2410.08373](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08373)
- Related in the same series: "Pattern runs on matter: the free monad monad as a module over the cofree comonad comonad" (Libkind & Spivak, 2024) formalises programs/decision-trees (free monads) executing against environments/behaviours (cofree comonads). THEOREM-level structural result. [R] — [arXiv:2404.16321](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16321)
- Collectives. Niu & Spivak model a "collective" as a monoid for the parallel product in Poly, a protocol for aggregating contributions and distributing returns. CONSTRUCTION. [R] — [arXiv:2112.11518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.11518)
- Use in AI-agent theory. Smithe's "Structured Active Inference" (2024, extended abstract) uses polynomial-style interfaces so that agents can have mode-dependent interfaces, manage other agents, and be "meta-agents" that change their own structure, with typed policies and goals as logical predicates. PROGRAMME (extended abstract, no theorems of scale or experiments). [V] — [arXiv:2406.07577](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07577)

### Inferences
- Poly gives, in one object, most of what other frameworks assemble from parts: lenses (morphisms), coalgebras (systems), operadic wiring (parallel product + lenses), categories/databases (comonoids/bicomodules), internal logic (coalgebra toposes), and self-modifying wiring (coalgebras of internal homs, i.e. Org). This is the strongest argument for "Poly alone" as a minimal kernel.
- Nested, self-rewiring collectives: nesting is handled (a wired composite again has a polynomial interface); state-dependent rewiring is handled within a fixed "arena" of possible wirings (Org); change of the interface itself only since the 2026 PolyTr preprint. What is missing is any theorem about what such rewiring systems do (stability, invariants, safety), and any native vocabulary for goals, obligations, permissions or authority. Those must be added (e.g. as predicates in the coalgebra topos, or as costs in a categorical-cybernetics layer).
- Poly over Set is deterministic and discrete-time by default; probabilistic, continuous-time and nondeterministic variants require changing the base or moving to the more general doctrines of categorical systems theory (section 3).

### Gaps
- No source found giving a compositionality theorem (behaviour of the whole computed from parts) for Org/OrgTr comparable to those in categorical systems theory.
- No evidence of software tooling that executes Org-style dynamic organisations at scale; no empirical study of Poly models with more than toy numbers of components.
- The withdrawn 2024 paper means the "hierarchical agents" strand has an unresolved technical hole as of the latest record found; whether a corrected version exists was not determined.

## 2. Operads and wiring-diagram algebras: is "a system of systems is a system" exactly an operad algebra?

### Takeaway
Yes, for static architectures this is an exact, proven formalisation: an operad (or symmetric monoidal category) of wiring diagrams supplies the syntax of nesting, and an algebra supplies, for each interface, the set (or category) of systems with that interface plus a composition rule that is associative under substitution. What the plain operad-algebra picture lacks is (a) maps between systems, (b) changing wiring, and (c) behaviour; these are what double-categorical/DOTS and Org generalisations add.

### Cited Findings
- Spivak (2013) defines the operad of wiring diagrams: objects are boxes with typed ports, operations are ways of wiring boxes inside an outer box, composition is substitution (nesting). CONSTRUCTION. [R] — [arXiv:1305.0297](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0297)
- Vagner, Spivak & Lerman (2015, Theory and Applications of Categories) prove that open dynamical systems (ODE-based) form an algebra on the operad of wiring diagrams, with a sub-algebra of linear systems; i.e. wiring open systems yields an open system, coherently under nesting. THEOREM. [R] — [arXiv:1408.1598](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1598)
- Classification results linking operad algebras to familiar categorical structure: algebras on wiring-diagram/cobordism operads correspond to traced and compact closed categories (Spivak, Schultz, Rupel), and hypergraph categories are equivalent to algebras for cospans (Fong & Spivak). THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1508.01069](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.01069); [arXiv:1806.08304](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08304)
- Network operads. Baez, Foley, Moeller & Pollard, "Network models" (TAC 2020): a network model is a lax symmetric monoidal functor into Cat; each yields a typed operad whose operations are ways of assembling networks of a given kind (overlay and disjoint union), developed for designing networked systems of boats/planes under a DARPA programme with Metron. THEOREM (construction of operads from network models). [R] — [arXiv:1711.00037](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00037)
- Systems-engineering use: Foley, Breiner, Subrahmanian & Dusel, "Operads for complex system design specification, analysis and synthesis" (Proc. R. Soc. A, 2021) argue operads separate syntax (architecture) from semantics (models at several fidelities) and survey applications. Expository + CONSTRUCTION. [R] — [arXiv:2101.11115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.11115)
- Computational realisation: Libkind, Baas, Patterson & Fairbanks, "Operadic modeling of dynamical systems: mathematics and computation" implement directed (machines, input/output) and undirected (resource-sharing) composition in AlgebraicDynamics.jl. CONSTRUCTION + software. [R] — [arXiv:2105.12282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.12282)
- The 2025 successor makes the operadic reading explicit and upgrades it: a "double operadic theory of systems" is "an algebra over a double operad", consisting of three categories (systems, interfaces, interactions), a boundary functor from systems to interfaces, and a composition functor; "DOTS continues earlier work on operadic composition for systems theories". [V] — [Topos blog, "DOTS from double theories", 7 Nov 2025](https://topos.institute/blog/2025-11-07-dots-from-double-theories); [arXiv:2505.18329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329)
- Dynamic operads (operads enriched in Org) let the composition pattern itself change in response to what flows through it. CONSTRUCTION. [V] — [arXiv:2205.03906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03906)

### Inferences
- "System of systems is a system" = existence of an algebra map F(phi): F(X1) x ... x F(Xn) -> F(Y) for each wiring phi, with the operad-algebra associativity law being exactly "nesting in two steps equals nesting in one". This is the cleanest known formal statement of fractal/recursive composition, and it is scale-free by construction (the same law holds at depth 1 or depth 30).
- It is necessary but not sufficient for governance: an operad algebra says composites exist and are well-typed; it says nothing about whether composite behaviour is predictable from parts (that is a separate compositionality theorem, section 3/7), nor about who may rewire (authority).
- Interfaces must be chosen up front; "everything is an operad algebra" pushes the modelling burden into choosing the operad (directed vs undirected vs mode-dependent wiring).

### Gaps
- No source found that proves properties of very deep or very wide operadic nestings (e.g. complexity bounds for analysis as a function of depth/width).
- Operad-level treatment of permissions/authority over rewiring: none found.

## 3. Open systems: decorated/structured cospans, open Petri nets and Markov processes, double categories, categorical systems theory, DOTS

### Takeaway
This is the most theorem-rich strand. Structured/decorated cospans give symmetric monoidal double categories of open networks with functorial (sometimes only lax) semantics; Myers' categorical systems theory and the Libkind-Myers DOTS framework (2025) unify cospan-style (variable sharing), lens-style (port plugging) and Moore-machine doctrines under one double-categorical definition. DOTS is explicitly titled "Towards" and is the backbone of the UK ARIA Safeguarded AI theory effort; integration of all formalisms is ongoing, not finished.

### Cited Findings
- Decorated cospans (Fong 2015) and structured cospans (Baez & Courser 2020): an open system is a cospan whose apex carries extra structure and whose feet are interfaces; composition is by pushout (gluing along shared interface). Given suitable finite-colimit hypotheses one obtains a symmetric monoidal (double) category. THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1502.00872](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00872); [arXiv:1911.04630](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.04630)
- Baez, Courser & Vasilakopoulou, "Structured versus Decorated Cospans" (Compositionality vol. 4, 1 Sept 2022) compare the two and give conditions under which they are equivalent, with applications to circuits, Petri nets, dynamical systems and epidemiological modelling. THEOREM. [V] — [Compositionality](https://compositionality.episciences.org/13517); [arXiv:2101.09363](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.09363)
- Open Petri nets (Baez & Master, MSCS 30, 2020): a symmetric monoidal double category of open Petri nets via structured cospans; the reachability semantics is a double functor to relations that is only lax (reachability of a composite can exceed what is derivable from the parts' reachability relations). THEOREM. [V for the double category and publication data; R for the laxness statement] — [arXiv:1808.05415](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05415)
- Black-boxing functors for open Markov processes and open reaction networks (Baez, Fong, Pollard; Baez & Pollard): functors from open networks to relations between boundary quantities (steady states), i.e. compositional functorial semantics. THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1508.06448](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06448); [arXiv:1704.02051](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.02051)
- Baez's 2025 overview "Double Categories of Open Systems: the Cospan Approach" compares the virtues of structured and decorated cospan double categories with open Petri nets as running example. Survey. [V] — [arXiv:2509.22584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22584)
- Categorical systems theory (Myers). Core idea: a "systems theory" (doctrine) specifies what a system is, what an interface is, and how systems compose; systems, interfaces, interaction patterns and maps of systems organise into double categories / doubly indexed categories; behaviours are (co)representable, and a "compositionality theorem" lets one derive a composite's behaviour from its components and the wiring pattern, "though this isn't always possible". Book draft: *Categorical Systems Theory*. CONSTRUCTION + THEOREMS in the deterministic/Moore setting. [V for the description via Libkind-Myers and Myers' slides; R for book URL] — [arXiv:2505.18329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329); [Myers, "Paradigms of Composition" slides 2020](https://www.davidjaz.com/Talks/Paradigms%20of%20Composition%20Oct2020.pdf); [book draft](http://davidjaz.com/Papers/DynamicalBook.pdf); Myers, "Double categories of open dynamical systems" [arXiv:2005.05956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.05956)
- DOTS. Libkind & Myers, "Towards a double operadic theory of systems" (23 May 2025, v2 28 May 2025): a systems theory is a symmetric monoidal loose right module of systems over a symmetric monoidal double category of interfaces and interactions. Detailed examples: open Petri nets over undirected wiring diagrams; deterministic Moore machines over lenses. Doctrines (pseudo-functorial constructions) are given for port-plugging systems, variable-sharing systems and generalised Moore machines, each generalising existing work; diagrammatic interaction patterns are shown to be free processes in particular doctrines. CONSTRUCTION + THEOREMS; title signals PROGRAMME. [V] — [arXiv:2505.18329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329)
- Stated limits/observations in the Topos exposition: double theories (the logic behind CatColab) struggle with systems that do not "look like categories" since "the simplest double theory — the point — already has categories as its models"; DOTS is more flexible; the double-theory-to-DOTS construction is "one way of many (i.e. at least two)"; the post omits coherence checks ("Everything just works I promise"). [V] — [Topos blog 2025-11-07](https://topos.institute/blog/2025-11-07-dots-from-double-theories)
- Behaviour beyond determinism was open until 2026: Lynch, Myers, Rischel & Staton, "Clock systems for stochastic and non-deterministic categorical systems theories" (31 Mar 2026) show stochastic and non-deterministic systems theories admit clock systems (representing objects for behaviour), answering a previously open question; behaviours form a representable double functor to Span. THEOREM. [V] — [arXiv:2603.29573](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29573). Earlier related: P. Wang, nondeterministic behaviours in double categorical systems theory, [arXiv:2502.02517](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02517) [V, existence only]
- Double theories: Lambert & Patterson, "Cartesian double theories" give a double-categorical framework for "doctrines" (kinds of categorical structure), the basis of CatColab's logics; Carlson & Patterson (2025, substantially rewritten March 2026) treat instances of models of double theories. THEOREMS/CONSTRUCTIONS. [R for Lambert-Patterson; V for Carlson-Patterson existence] — [arXiv:2310.05384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05384); [arXiv:2510.08861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08861)
- Institutional uptake: ARIA's Safeguarded AI programme (backed by GBP 59m) funds "Double Categorical Systems Theory for Safeguarded AI", led by David Jaz Myers (Topos Research UK) with Lynch, Libkind, Spivak and Fairbanks, aiming to use DCST for explainable, auditable models of an AI system's deployment environment; a sibling project on "Doubly Categorical Systems Logic" is led by Alexandra Silva. PROGRAMME. [V] — [ARIA TA1.1 creators](https://www.aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces/mathematics-for-safe-ai/safeguarded-ai/meet-the-creators); [ARIA funded projects](https://aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces/mathematics-for-safe-ai/safeguarded-ai/funded-projects)
- John Baez (blog, Aug and Nov 2025) reports roughly 50 people at a Bristol meeting and about 60 at an Edinburgh meeting of the programme; describes a "categorical core" software layer plus DOTS as the framework for building systems from parts, and says Myers was to integrate the many formalisms, "a huge job". A repost notes the programme director (David Dalrymple) "has changed the plan recently" without details. Second-hand blog commentary, not an official status report. [V via search snippet; the page itself returned 503 when fetched] — [Baez, "Safeguarded AI (Part 2)" mirror](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/3917/feed_items/16936216/content); [n-Category Cafe, "Safeguarded AI meeting", Aug 2025](https://classes.golem.ph.utexas.edu/category/2025/08/safeguarded_ai_meeting.html)

### Inferences
- The field has converged on the shape "symmetric monoidal double category of interfaces/interactions + module (or doubly indexed category) of systems + (lax) behaviour functor". That shape subsumes cospans, lenses/Poly-style arenas, and operad algebras, and is the best current candidate for a framework-of-frameworks.
- The double-categorical dimension is what makes this a governance-relevant foundation: the second direction (maps between systems/interfaces) is where abstraction, refinement, simulation and "this implementation satisfies that specification" live.
- The kernel is larger and heavier than Poly (requires fluency with double categories, loose modules, pseudo-functors), and its generality is bought with limited worked semantics beyond Moore machines, ODEs, Petri nets and, since 2026, stochastic/nondeterministic behaviours.

### Gaps
- The Myers book is still a draft (not confirmed as published); exact theorem statements were not re-read this session.
- Official ARIA technical documents describing the status or redirection of the categorical effort were not found; the "change of plan" is unverified hearsay.
- No DOTS treatment found of dynamically changing interaction structure (the Org direction) or of agents with goals; integration with categorical cybernetics is not documented in the sources found.

## 4. Categorical cybernetics: open games, lenses/optics, Para, Bayesian lenses, compositional active inference

### Takeaway
This is the only strand that models agency directly: an agent is a parametrised bidirectional process (a parameter space of strategies/weights, a forward "act" pass and a backward "feedback/utility/gradient" pass), and games, learners and Bayesian inference all share the lens/optic composition law. Proven results cover composition of games and Bayesian updates; the programme admits it cannot compute equilibria in general, does not address equilibrium selection, and has had a hard road to adoption.

### Cited Findings
- Open games. Ghani, Hedges, Winschel & Zahn, "Compositional game theory" (LICS 2018): games as morphisms with strategies, play, coplay and a best-response/equilibrium component, composed sequentially and in parallel in a symmetric monoidal category, so that equilibria of a composite are defined from the components. The authors stress this is non-trivial: emergent effects make compositionality very hard to achieve in game theory. THEOREM. [V] — [arXiv:1603.04641](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04641)
- Structure in one slide (Hedges, ACT@UCR, 15 Apr 2020): an open game is a set of strategy profiles, a family of optics indexed by them, and for every context a best-response subset; the strategy set of a composite is the product of the components' strategy sets. [V] — [Hedges slides](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/mathematical/ACTUCR/Hedges_Open_Games.pdf)
- Early limitation fixed later: the original framework was deterministic with no chance elements; Bayesian open games (Bolt, Hedges, Zahn) add stochastic environments and Bayesian Nash equilibria. THEOREM/CONSTRUCTION. [V via search summary] — [arXiv:1910.03656](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.03656)
- Self-assessed limits (Hedges 2020 slides): open games are "inflexible in many ways with a very steep learning curve"; "we can't compute equilibria"; equilibrium selection is not addressed. "General conclusions for ACT": "Realising the promised benefits of ACT is still hard"; "Interdisciplinary work is very costly"; "Designing good abstractions will always be an art form"; "String diagrams may not even be the best representation!". Under "Economics as worst case scenario": "This is not publishable in economics venues"; "Project has not paid off for my collaborators". [V: the "General conclusions"/"Economics" quotes were read from the PDF text; the first three phrases come from a search-engine summary of the same deck] — [Hedges slides](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/mathematical/ACTUCR/Hedges_Open_Games.pdf)
- Lenses and optics. Lens composition is the chain rule for backward passes ("destructive updates compose by a chain rule"); optics generalise lenses to any monoidal category via a coend, reducing to lenses in the cartesian case. THEOREMS. [V via slides; R for papers] — [Hedges slides](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/mathematical/ACTUCR/Hedges_Open_Games.pdf); Riley, "Categories of optics" [arXiv:1809.00738](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.00738)
- Para and the cybernetics synthesis. Capucci, Gavranovic, Hedges & Rischel, "Towards Foundations of Categorical Cybernetics" (ACT 2021, EPTCS 372, pp. 235-248): Para models parameters (agents' controllable choices) hidden from a morphism's boundary; Optic models bidirectional flow (action forward, feedback backward); parametrised optics form a (monoidal) bicategory proposed as the common setting for open games, supervised learning and policy optimisation. CONSTRUCTION; self-described "Towards". [V] — [arXiv:2105.06332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06332)
- Learning as the same pattern: "Backprop as Functor" (Fong, Spivak, Tuyeras; LICS 2019) proves gradient-descent learners compose functorially; Cruttwell et al. (ESOP 2022) recast gradient-based learning with parametrised lenses over reverse-derivative categories; Gavranovic et al. (ICML 2024 position paper) argue categorical deep learning is "an algebraic theory of all architectures". THEOREMS for the first two; PROGRAMME for the third. [R] — [arXiv:1711.10455](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10455); [arXiv:2103.01931](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01931); [arXiv:2402.15332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15332)
- Bayesian lenses and active inference. Smithe's Oxford DPhil thesis (arXiv Dec 2022, v3 Dec 2023) defines Bayesian lenses, shows Bayesian updating composes according to the lens pattern, defines fibrations of statistical games whose sections are inference problems, formalises open dynamical systems as coalgebras of polynomial functors, and constructs functors explaining the bidirectional structure of predictive-coding circuits under the free-energy principle. THEOREMS + CONSTRUCTIONS. [V] — [arXiv:2212.12538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12538); earlier paper [arXiv:2109.04461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04461)
- "Structured Active Inference" (Smithe 2024): interface as compositional Markov blanket; agents as controllers dual to generative models; agents managing agents; meta-agents altering their own structure; typed, verifiable policies; goals as context-dependent logical predicates; aimed at "compositionally constraining and controlling self-organizing groups of agents". PROGRAMME (extended abstract). [V] — [arXiv:2406.07577](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07577)
- Tooling: the open-game-engine (Haskell DSL) maintained by the CyberCat Institute. [R] — [github.com/CyberCat-Institute/open-game-engine](https://github.com/CyberCat-Institute/open-game-engine)

### Inferences
- Categorical cybernetics supplies exactly what Poly/DOTS lack: a place for goals (utility/loss/free energy in the backward pass) and agency (the Para parameter chosen by an optimiser/selection function). Conversely it lacks rich hierarchical nesting and changing structure, which Poly/Org supply. The two meet technically because lenses are morphisms in Poly and parametrised lenses are close to [p,q]-coalgebras.
- "Compositional" here means the definition of equilibrium/update composes; it does not mean equilibria are cheaply computable or unique. For governance at scale this is a hard limit: one can type-check an institution's incentive wiring but not, in general, solve it.
- Norms and authority still appear only indirectly (as payoff structure, or as typed policies in Smithe's proposal); no deontic layer exists.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed full paper found delivering the "agents managing agents / meta-agents" theorems of structured active inference; only the extended abstract.
- No independent (non-author) evaluation of open games in economics or mechanism design practice was found.
- Current activity level and industrial users of the open-game-engine were not verified this session.

## 5. Coalgebra and bisimulation; monads/comonads; algebraic effects; relation to Poly

### Takeaway
Universal coalgebra is the mature (25+ years), fully rigorous theory of state-based behaviour: systems are coalgebras X -> F(X), behavioural equivalence is bisimulation, and the final coalgebra is the universe of behaviours. It is the semantic bedrock under Poly and categorical systems theory but by itself gives little structure for open, multi-interface composition; Poly can be read as "coalgebra plus interfaces plus wiring".

### Cited Findings
- Rutten, "Universal coalgebra: a theory of systems" (Theoretical Computer Science 249, 2000): coalgebras of a functor as a general theory of systems, dual to universal algebra; homomorphisms, bisimulation, final coalgebras and coinduction. THEOREMS. [R] — [doi:10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00056-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00056-6)
- Jacobs, *Introduction to Coalgebra: Towards Mathematics of States and Observation* (Cambridge, 2016): textbook treatment including polynomial functors as the standard source of system types, invariants, and coalgebraic modal logic. [R] — [doi:10.1017/CBO9781316823187](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781316823187)
- A genuine compositionality theorem from this tradition: Turi & Plotkin (LICS 1997) show that operational rules given by a distributive law of syntax (monad) over behaviour (comonad) yield bialgebras in which bisimilarity is a congruence, i.e. behaviour of a composite term is determined by behaviours of its parts. THEOREM. [R] — [doi:10.1109/LICS.1997.614955](https://doi.org/10.1109/LICS.1997.614955)
- Algebraic effects: Plotkin & Power characterise computational effects by operations and equations (monads from algebraic theories). THEOREM. [R] — [doi:10.1023/A:1023064908962](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1023064908962)
- Relation to Poly: dynamical systems in Poly are p-coalgebras; the cofree comonoid on p is the category of p-behaviour trees; categories themselves are polynomial comonoids; free monads on polynomials are terminating programs/decision trees, and Libkind-Spivak relate the free-monad and cofree-comonad sides ("pattern runs on matter"). Smithe likewise formalises open dynamical systems as coalgebras of polynomial functors. [V for Smithe and CUP chapter summary; R for the rest] — [CUP ch. 7](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/polynomial-functors/polynomial-comonoids-and-retrofunctors/352E5607109BDEA94B5FC98C6E5EFDAF); [arXiv:2404.16321](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16321); [arXiv:2212.12538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12538)
- In categorical systems theory, behaviours are captured representably (maps out of "clock" systems), generalising the final-coalgebra view to stochastic and nondeterministic settings as of 2026. THEOREM. [V] — [arXiv:2603.29573](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29573)

### Inferences
- Coalgebra answers "what is a behaviour and when are two systems the same?" for any scale; it is the natural notion of equivalence/refinement a governance layer needs ("this department can be replaced by that one without the rest noticing" = bisimilar at the interface).
- Alone it is not a kernel for composition: a coalgebra has one functor type; open composition, multiple interfaces and wiring need the Poly/operad/double-category layer. Bialgebraic (distributive-law) compositionality is the classical bridge.
- Monads (free: syntax, plans, effects) and comonads (cofree: contexts, behaviours) are the two halves of the "agent program vs environment" duality that recurs in Poly and in active-inference formulations.

### Gaps
- No source found applying coalgebraic bisimulation to organisational or institutional systems at scale; applications are overwhelmingly in programming-language semantics and automata theory.

## 6. Sheaf theory: dynamical systems and sheaves, temporal type theory, local-to-global obstruction as emergence/inconsistency

### Takeaway
Sheaves give the rigorous language for "local data that must agree on overlaps" and thus for integration, consistency and time-varying behaviour; cohomology and consistency radius quantify failure to glue. This yields proven tools (behaviour toposes with internal temporal logic; sheaf Laplacians whose stable states are exactly global sections), but "emergence = cohomology" is a partial, case-specific formalisation, not a general theory.

### Cited Findings
- Schultz, Spivak & Vasilakopoulou, "Dynamical systems and sheaves" (Applied Categorical Structures, 2020): sheaves on categories of time intervals model discrete, continuous and hybrid behaviours uniformly; "machines" built from such sheaves form algebras on a wiring-diagram operad, so heterogeneous systems (discrete + continuous) compose in one framework. THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1609.08086](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.08086)
- Schultz & Spivak, *Temporal Type Theory* (Birkhauser 2019): a topos of behaviour types (sheaves on an interval domain) whose internal higher-order logic serves as a temporal logic for proving properties of systems of interacting components; motivated by a National Airspace System safety case. THEOREMS (soundness of the logic in the topos) + case study. [R] — [arXiv:1710.10258](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10258)
- Behavioural mereology (Fong, Myers, Spivak): parts of a system as quotients of its behaviour type, with a logic of constraint passing between parts ("compatibility" and "ensurance" modalities). CONSTRUCTION + THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1811.00420](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.00420)
- Robinson: sheaves as the canonical structure for heterogeneous sensor/data integration; the "consistency radius" measures agreement between overlapping local observations and yields a consistency filtration identifying regions where data are consistent to a given level; a 2025 MITRE paper (Robinson & Szulczewski) applies minimal consistency radius to composite dynamical systems as a measure of consistency between observations and a hypothesis. THEOREMS (continuity/stability of the radius) + applications. [V for consistency radius and 2025 paper; R for the 2017 paper] — [arXiv:1805.08927](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08927); [arXiv:2511.04603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04603); [arXiv:1603.01446](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.01446)
- Hansen & Ghrist, "Opinion Dynamics on Discourse Sheaves": agents on a social network with private opinion spaces and edge-wise discourse spaces (what C says to B need not match what C says to A); under sheaf-Laplacian diffusion "the only stable opinion distributions are global sections of the discourse sheaf"; includes nonlinear and bounded-confidence variants. THEOREM. [V] — [arXiv:2005.12798](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12798)
- Local consistency without global consistency as a cohomological obstruction: Abramsky & Brandenburger show contextuality/non-locality is exactly the non-existence of a global section of a presheaf of local data; Abramsky, Mansfield & Barbosa give Cech-cohomology witnesses, which are sufficient but not complete detectors (some contextual models have vanishing obstruction). THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1102.0264](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264); [arXiv:1111.3620](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.3620)
- Adam's MIT thesis (2017) frames cascade-like interactional effects as "a loss of exactness" and as "(co)homological intuition waiting to be formalized"; the companion overview (Adam & Dahleh) says one can compute (co)homology objects capturing systems' potential to produce effects. Partially formalised. [V] — [MIT DSpace](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/109012); [arXiv:1911.10406](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.10406)

### Inferences
- For governance, sheaves offer the cleanest formal definition of "institutional inconsistency": local rule-sets/observations that pairwise agree but admit no global section; H^1-type invariants localise where the conflict lives. This is complementary to, not a replacement for, the compositional frameworks: operads/double categories build systems, sheaves audit whether their local views cohere.
- "Emergence as cohomology" is rigorous only where (i) the system is cast as a sheaf over a fixed cover and (ii) the relevant structure is abelian enough for homological algebra; and cohomological tests can miss obstructions. It should be presented as a tool, not a theory of emergence.
- Temporal type theory is the most developed candidate for the "logic" slot of a kernel (specifying norms/safety over time), but its tooling is essentially absent.

### Gaps
- No implemented proof assistant or model checker for temporal type theory was found.
- No source found applying sheaf cohomology to organisational/legal norm conflicts with empirical data.

## 7. Generative effects and failure of compositionality; compositionality as laxness

### Takeaway
The field has a precise, modest definition of emergence: an observation/semantics map that fails to preserve composition (joins, pushouts, wiring), i.e. is only lax. Strictly functorial semantics = no surprises; lax semantics = the composite can do more than the parts predict, and the gap is measurable in specific settings.

### Cited Findings
- Fong & Spivak, *Seven Sketches in Compositionality* (2018; CUP 2019), ch. 1: a generative effect is the failure of a monotone map Phi to preserve joins, Phi(a) v Phi(b) < Phi(a v b); maps that are left adjoints (Galois connections) preserve joins and therefore exhibit no generative effects. THEOREM (adjoint functor theorem for preorders) + definition, following Adam. [R] — [arXiv:1803.05316](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05316)
- Adam (MIT PhD 2017, advisors Dahleh and Ozdaglar; 288 pp.): studies "interactional effects" arising when systems interact and proposes extracting from each system its potential to generate such effects, with cascade effects exposed as loss of exactness. [V] — [MIT DSpace](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/109012); overview [arXiv:1911.10406](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.10406)
- Concrete instance: reachability semantics of open Petri nets is a lax, not strict, double functor (Baez & Master). [R for laxness] — [arXiv:1808.05415](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05415)
- Categorical systems theory treats this as the general situation: a compositionality theorem derives composite behaviour from components and wiring, and Libkind & Myers note this is not always possible. [V] — [arXiv:2505.18329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329)
- Game theory: Ghani et al. note there is a priori no reason equilibria of a composite should be definable from components, and that emergent effects make compositionality very hard to achieve. [V] — [arXiv:1603.04641](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04641)
- Puca, Hadzihasanovic, Genovese & Coecke, "Obstructions to Compositionality" (ACT 2023): model compositionality failure via lax functors and define homotopy/homology-style invariants that measure the obstruction. CONSTRUCTION + THEOREMS in the stated setting. [R] — [arXiv:2307.14461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14461)

### Inferences
- A governance foundation should treat each property of interest (safety, reachability, equilibrium, cost) as a semantics functor and ask of each: strict, lax, or oplax? Strict properties can be verified locally and scale to arbitrary size; lax ones require global analysis or bounding the laxator. This is probably the most practically useful conceptual export of ACT for "staying controlled at 10^10 actors".
- No framework removes emergence; they locate it.

### Gaps
- No general quantitative theory bounding laxators (how much emergent behaviour) across frameworks was found; results are example-specific.

## 8. String diagrams, monoidal categories, resource theories, Markov categories, process theories

### Takeaway
Symmetric monoidal categories are the common substrate of everything above (processes with sequential and parallel composition, with a sound and complete graphical calculus). Resource theories and Markov categories are well-established, theorem-backed specialisations for convertibility of resources and for probability/causality respectively.

### Cited Findings
- Soundness of string diagrams: Joyal & Street, "The geometry of tensor calculus I" (1991); survey of graphical languages by Selinger. THEOREMS. [R] — [doi:10.1016/0001-8708(91)90003-P](https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(91)90003-P); [arXiv:0908.3347](https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.3347)
- Coecke, Fritz & Spekkens, "A mathematical theory of resources" (Information and Computation, 2016): a resource theory is a symmetric monoidal category in which objects are resources and morphisms are freely implementable conversions; yields a convertibility preorder, monotones, catalysis; unifies quantum resource theories, thermodynamics, etc. CONSTRUCTION + THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1409.5531](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.5531)
- Fritz, "A synthetic approach to Markov kernels, conditional independence and theorems on sufficient statistics" (Advances in Mathematics, 2020): Markov categories (symmetric monoidal categories with copy/delete on every object, deletion natural) support synthetic proofs of classical statistics theorems. THEOREMS. [R] — [arXiv:1908.07021](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.07021)
- Co-design: Censi's "A mathematical theory of co-design" treats design problems as monotone relations between posets of functionality and resources, composable in series, parallel and feedback; presented in Seven Sketches ch. 4 as enriched profunctors in a compact closed category. THEOREMS (existence/computation of minimal resource antichains by fixed points). [R] — [arXiv:1512.08055](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.08055); [arXiv:1803.05316](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05316)
- Control/signal-flow: Baez & Erbele, "Categories in control" give a presentation of signal-flow diagrams as a symmetric monoidal category of linear relations. THEOREM. [R] — [arXiv:1405.6881](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.6881)
- Bayes as a functor into lenses (Hedges/Smithe): composition of conditional distributions inverts by the lens rule. [V] — [Hedges slides](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/mathematical/ACTUCR/Hedges_Open_Games.pdf); [arXiv:2212.12538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12538)

### Inferences
- "Symmetric monoidal category" is the true minimal primitive: every other framework is an SMC with extra structure (hypergraph/compact for undirected sharing, lenses/optics for bidirectionality, copy-delete for probability, double-categorical direction for maps between systems).
- Resource theories give the cleanest scale-free account of the lowest level in the brief ("resource") and of budgets/feasibility constraints; co-design adds requirement/provision matching, which is close to a formal notion of contract between levels.
- SMCs alone express no state, no agency and no nesting discipline; they are necessary, not sufficient.

### Gaps
- No verified source found applying resource theories or co-design to organisational/governmental resource allocation beyond engineering design examples.

## 9. Institution theory, ologs, functorial data migration, categorical databases, sketches

### Takeaway
For knowledge, schemas and specifications, the category-theoretic foundations are old, proved and tooled: institutions make logic itself a composable parameter; schemas-as-categories with the Delta/Sigma/Pi adjoint triple make data integration functorial. They address "what is known and how vocabularies align", not dynamics or agency.

### Cited Findings
- Goguen & Burstall, "Institutions: abstract model theory for specification and programming" (JACM 39(1), 1992): an institution is a category of signatures with sentence and model functors and a satisfaction relation invariant under signature change ("truth is invariant under change of notation"); theories can be combined by colimits independently of the underlying logic. THEOREMS. [R] — [doi:10.1145/147508.147524](https://doi.org/10.1145/147508.147524)
- Spivak & Kent, "Ologs: a categorical framework for knowledge representation" (PLoS ONE 2012): ontology logs as categories with functional relationships, alignable by functors. CONSTRUCTION. [R] — [arXiv:1102.1889](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.1889)
- Spivak, "Functorial data migration" (Information and Computation, 2012): a schema is a (finitely presented) category, an instance a set-valued functor; any schema mapping F induces three adjoint data-migration functors (pullback Delta, left pushforward Sigma, right pushforward Pi). THEOREM. [R] — [arXiv:1009.1166](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.1166)
- Schultz, Spivak, Vasilakopoulou & Wisnesky, "Algebraic databases" (TAC 2017): schemas with attributes via algebraic theories; basis of the CQL language commercialised by Conexus. THEOREMS + software. [R] — [arXiv:1602.03501](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03501); [categoricaldata.net](https://categoricaldata.net)
- The same structure reappears inside Poly: bicomodules between polynomial comonoids are data-migration (parametric right adjoint) functors. [R] — [arXiv:2202.00534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00534)
- Acsets (attributed C-sets): Patterson, Lynch & Fairbanks, "Categorical data structures for technical computing" (Compositionality 2022) generalise graphs and data frames as functors from a schema, with generic limits/colimits implemented in Catlab. THEOREMS + software. [R] — [arXiv:2106.04703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04703)
- Double theories (Lambert & Patterson) play the institution-like role in CatColab: each "logic" (causal loop diagrams, stock-flow, Petri nets, schemas) is a double theory; models can be given semantics afterwards (e.g. causal loop diagrams interpreted as linear or Lotka-Volterra ODEs). [V] — [Topos blog 2025-11-07](https://topos.institute/blog/2025-11-07-dots-from-double-theories)
- NIST engagement: Breiner, Jones, Spivak, Subrahmanian & Wisnesky used categorical data migration for manufacturing-service database integration (J. Comput. Inf. Sci. Eng., 2017); NIST hosted an applied category theory workshop in 2018. [R] — [doi:10.1115/1.4034268](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4034268); [NIST SP 1249](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1249)

### Inferences
- Institutions are the right abstraction for a multi-logic governance stack (different levels speak different logics yet specifications must compose); double theories are a newer, more structural analogue with active tooling.
- Functorial data migration is the most "shippable" piece of ACT: narrow, proven, and implemented; it is a component (shared vocabulary/records layer) rather than a system kernel.

### Gaps
- Independent, quantified evidence of CQL/Conexus production deployments was not found this session; claims of industrial use originate from the vendor and collaborators.
- Whether institution theory and double theories have been formally related was not determined.

## 11. Smallest kernel; comparison of candidates; what remains unsolved (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
No single published framework is both minimal and sufficient. The most minimal expressive candidate is Poly (one category; interfaces, lenses, coalgebras, wiring, categories, and self-rewiring via Org all internal to it). The most general and best-supported-by-theorems candidate is the double-categorical systems theory / DOTS shape, of which Poly-style lens systems and cospan systems are doctrines. Neither natively contains goals, norms or authority; the nearest rigorous supplement is categorical cybernetics (parametrised optics) for goals/agency and a behaviour-topos or sheaf logic for specifications. Experts' own framing ("Towards ...", open questions closed only in 2026, a withdrawn paper, a "huge job" of integration) shows the unified theory is still under construction.

### Inferences
- Candidate kernels ranked by minimality vs expressiveness:
  1. **Poly alone** — most minimal. Sufficient for: interfaces, state machines, wiring/nesting, mode-dependent and self-rewiring structure, categories/data, and an internal logic. Insufficient for: probability/continuous time without changing base; goals; norms; maps-between-systems theory as rich as DCST; proven compositionality theorems for dynamic organisations.
  2. **"Symmetric monoidal double category + module of systems" (DOTS)** — most general and the direction institutional money is following. It is a meta-framework (a definition of "systems theory") rather than a kernel one can compute with directly; each use requires choosing a doctrine. Poly-style lens systems and cospan systems are instances.
  3. **"SMC + lens/optic + operad algebra"** — a reasonable description of the common denominator, and it is essentially what (1) packages internally and (2) axiomatises externally. It is not a third independent option.
- A defensible minimal stack for governed systems of systems, assembled from proven parts: (a) interfaces and wiring: Poly or a DOTS doctrine; (b) behaviour and equivalence: coalgebra/representable behaviours; (c) agency and goals: parametrised lenses/optics layered on the same interfaces; (d) specification and consistency: topos/sheaf internal logic over behaviours, with laxness/cohomology diagnostics for emergence; (e) shared vocabulary/data: schemas as categories with functorial migration. Items (a), (b), (e) are theorem-grade; (c) is theorem-grade for composition but not for solution; (d) is theorem-grade but untooled; the glue between (a)-(e) is the unsolved part.
- What category theory currently cannot express without importing from other fields (flag for the other research strands): authority/delegation/permission as first-class structure; obligations and sanctions (deontic content); identity and accountability of actors across restructurings; and any quantitative guarantee of controllability at 10^10 scale. Self-reflection is partially available (Poly internal homs: systems whose states are wirings of systems; coalgebra toposes: a logic internal to each interface) but no result addresses a system reasoning about its own governance rules.
- Fractality is the one requirement that is fully and rigorously met: operad algebras / double operads make "a system of systems is a system" a theorem-backed law at every depth.

### Gaps
- No expert survey paper (2025-2026) explicitly ranking these frameworks as foundations was found; the ranking above is this researcher's inference.
- No published treatment found of norms/authority/delegation within Poly, DOTS or categorical cybernetics; absence of evidence after a limited search, not proof of absence. Assume-guarantee contract algebras and deontic logics (other strands) were not searched here.
- No scalability results (computational or analytical) at large actor counts were found for any framework.
