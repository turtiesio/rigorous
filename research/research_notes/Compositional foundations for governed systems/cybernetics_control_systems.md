# Cybernetics, control theory and general systems theory as foundations for recursive governance

Source-status convention. **[V]** = the URL and the claim were confirmed in this session's search results (abstract or snippet level; full texts were generally not opened). **[M]** = bibliographic detail, URL/DOI and content are from the researcher's prior knowledge of the primary source and were not re-fetched; the theorem statements are standard, but DOIs should be spot-checked before publication. Where the status of a claim (theorem vs heuristic) matters, it is tagged **THEOREM**, **CONDITIONAL THEOREM** (true under restrictive assumptions that are often dropped when it is quoted), **HEURISTIC**, or **FRAMEWORK** (not falsifiable as stated).

## 1. Ashby: requisite variety, good regulator, internal models, ultrastability

### Takeaway
The law of requisite variety is a genuine but elementary information-theoretic inequality, and it is the single most reusable limit in this literature. The good regulator "theorem" is much weaker than its title; modern repairs (Wentworth 2021, Richens & Everitt 2024, Richens et al. 2025) and the internal model principle (Francis & Wonham 1976) are the rigorous versions, each with narrower hypotheses.

### Cited Findings
- **Law of requisite variety (THEOREM, elementary).** Setup: disturbance D, regulator response R, outcome E = f(D,R), with the table condition that for each fixed r the map d -> f(d,r) is injective. Counting form: V(E) >= V(D)/V(R). Entropy form: H(E) >= H(D) + H(R|D) - H(R) >= H(D) - H(R). "Only variety can destroy variety." It bounds outcome variety from below by regulator capacity; it says nothing about how to build the regulator. — [Ashby 1956, An Introduction to Cybernetics, ch. 11](http://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/books/IntroCyb.pdf) [M]
- Ashby also shows the regulator is bounded by the channel capacity through which it learns about D (the regulator as a channel), linking requisite variety to Shannon's theorem 10. — [Ashby 1956, ch. 11](http://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/books/IntroCyb.pdf) [M]
- **Modern form (THEOREM).** Touchette & Lloyd: the maximum entropy reduction achievable by closed-loop control exceeds that of open-loop control by at most the mutual information the controller acquires about the system: ΔH_closed <= ΔH_open(max) + I(X;C). This is requisite variety restated as a second-law-like bound. — [Touchette & Lloyd 2000, Phys. Rev. Lett. 84:1156, arXiv chao-dyn/9905039](https://arxiv.org/abs/chao-dyn/9905039) [M]; [Touchette & Lloyd 2004, Physica A, arXiv physics/0104007](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0104007) [M]
- **Good regulator theorem (CONDITIONAL THEOREM, weak).** Conant & Ashby 1970: among regulators that minimise the entropy of the outcome Z = ψ(S,R), the simplest optimal ones choose R as a deterministic function of the system state S. "Model" means only that such a mapping h: S -> R exists. Assumptions: the regulator observes S perfectly, finite sets, entropy (not utility) as objective. — [Conant & Ashby 1970, Int. J. Systems Science 1(2):89-97](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207727008920220) [M]
- **Critique.** Wentworth: the theorem's notion of "model" is nearly vacuous (an identity or constant function can qualify), it uses entropy minimisation rather than expected utility, and assumes full observation. His repair: give the regulator only partial information X about S and require it to perform well across a family of tasks; then any optimal minimal regulator must internally compute something equivalent to the posterior P(S|X). — [Wentworth 2021, "Fixing the Good Regulator Theorem", Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/Dx9LoqsEh3gHNJMDk/fixing-the-good-regulator-theorem) [V]
- Follow-ups extend this to imperfect-knowledge regulators minimising output entropy and give plain-language reconstructions. — [A Generalization of the Good Regulator Theorem, LessWrong](https://lesswrong.com/posts/YEnTGLBu2crDw5GFo/a-generalization-of-the-good-regulator-theorem) [V]; [A Straightforward Explanation of the Good Regulator Theorem, LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/JQefBJDHG6Wgffw6T/a-straightforward-explanation-of-the-good-regulator-theorem) [V]
- **Causal repair (THEOREM, ICLR 2024).** Richens & Everitt: the environment is a causal Bayesian network with decision and utility nodes; the agent is a policy oracle mapping interventions to policies. Any agent satisfying a regret bound across a sufficiently large set of distributional shifts (local interventions) must have learned an approximate causal model of the data-generating process, recoverable from its policy, converging to the true model as regret goes to zero. Theorem 2 covers approximately optimal agents. — [Richens & Everitt 2024, ICLR proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/44a2b9f7bf9aec3f1fa333ad875b0ee0-Abstract-Conference.html) [V]; [illustrated summary, Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/Rwjrrmn6LBzHrfen7/summary-of-robust-agents-learn-causal-world-model) [V]
- **Sequential repair (THEOREM, ICML 2025).** Richens, Abel, Bellot & Everitt: any agent able to generalise to multi-step goal-directed tasks must have learned a predictive model of its environment, extractable from its policy; the model must be more accurate the more capable the agent or the more complex the goals. The arXiv title later changed to "General agents contain world models" (v5, Oct 2025). — [arXiv 2506.01622](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01622) [V]; [PMLR 267:51659-51687](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/richens25a.html) [V]
- **Internal model principle (THEOREM, linear finite-dimensional case).** Francis & Wonham: a controller achieving asymptotic regulation/tracking that is structurally stable (robust to small plant perturbations) must incorporate in the feedback path a suitably reduplicated copy of the dynamics of the exogenous signal generator (the "exosystem"). Note the model is of the disturbance/reference generator, not of the plant. Integral action for step disturbances is the simplest instance. — [Francis & Wonham 1976, Automatica 12(5):457-465](https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-1098(76)90006-6) [M]
- Nonlinear and biological extensions exist (e.g. Sontag 2003: adaptation implies an internal model under signal-detection assumptions; a 2022 Annual Review survey by Bin, Huang, Isidori, Marconi, Mischiati & Sontag). — [Sontag 2003, Systems & Control Letters 50:119-126](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-6911(03)00136-1) [M]
- **Ultrastability and the homeostat (constructive demonstration + HEURISTIC).** Ashby's homeostat (1948) had four coupled units; when "essential variables" left their bounds, step-function parameters were re-drawn at random until a stable field was found. This is double-loop adaptation: a fast loop regulates, a slow loop re-parameterises the fast loop. Ashby himself calculated that random re-selection scales hopelessly for large fully joined systems and concluded that adaptation in reasonable time requires the system to be only partly joined ("iterated" or "multistable" systems with weakly interacting subsystems). — [Ashby 1952/1960, Design for a Brain](https://archive.org/details/designforbrainor00ashb) [M]

### Inferences
- For governance, requisite variety gives a hard necessary condition: a governor with channel/decision capacity C bits per unit time cannot hold outcome entropy below H(D) - C. It is necessary, never sufficient.
- The 2024-2025 Richens results are the first versions of "good regulators need models" strong enough to bear weight: competence over a rich task/shift family implies an extractable world model. The converse governance reading: an overseer of a general agent can in principle read a world model out of its policy; and any governor that is robust across many regimes must itself carry a causal model of the governed.
- Ashby's ultrastability scaling argument is an early statement of the same conclusion Simon reaches: large adaptive systems must be nearly decomposable.

### Gaps
- No single theorem unifies requisite variety, internal model principle and the Richens results; they use different formalisms (entropy, linear geometry, causal Bayesian networks).
- Exact quantitative bounds in Richens et al. 2025 (error vs goal depth) were not re-read in full this session.

## 2. Beer's Viable System Model, recursion, variety engineering, Cybersyn

### Takeaway
The VSM is the most direct prior art for "recursive governance at every scale", but it is a structured heuristic derived by analogy and variety accounting, not a theorem. Its "Recursive System Theorem" is an axiom/definition. Evidence is case-study-heavy with one large survey from a proponent.

### Cited Findings
- **Structure (FRAMEWORK).** Five functions necessary and sufficient (claimed) for viability: S1 operations (themselves viable systems), S2 coordination/anti-oscillation, S3 operational control with the resource bargain and synergy, S3* sporadic audit, S4 intelligence/adaptation ("outside and then"), S5 policy/identity providing closure and balancing S3 vs S4. Algedonic signals are pain/pleasure alerts that bypass intermediate levels to reach the level able to act. — [Beer 1984, "The Viable System Model: its provenance, development, methodology and pathology", J. Operational Research Society 35(1):7-25](https://doi.org/10.1057/jors.1984.2) [M]
- **Recursive System Theorem (AXIOM, not proven).** "In a recursive organizational structure, any viable system contains, and is contained in, a viable system." Stated in Brain of the Firm (1972) and Heart of Enterprise (1979). Supported by Beer's four principles of organisation (variety equalisation across the operation/management/environment loops; channel capacity; transducer capacity; no time lags) and three axioms of management (horizontal variety absorbed by S1s equals vertical variety on the six vertical channels; S3 variety equals S4 variety; S5 absorbs the residual). — [Beer 1984, JORS](https://doi.org/10.1057/jors.1984.2) [M]
- **Variety engineering (HEURISTIC resting on a theorem).** Management cannot match operational variety directly, so it must attenuate incoming variety (aggregation, exception reporting, autonomy of S1) and amplify its own (delegation, standards). The requisite-variety inequality is real; the specific variety balances are never measured in bits in practice. — [Beer 1984, JORS](https://doi.org/10.1057/jors.1984.2) [M]
- **Cybersyn (Chile 1971-73).** Components: Cybernet (telex network), Cyberstride (statistical filtering of production indices for exception reports), CHECO (economic simulator), and the Opsroom. The telex network was used to coordinate distribution during the October 1972 truckers' strike; the full system was never completed and ended with the 1973 coup. The standard history is Medina. — [Medina 2011, Cybernetic Revolutionaries, MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262525961/cybernetic-revolutionaries/) [M]
- **Empirical evidence.** Schwaninger & Scheef (2016), "A Test of the Viable System Model: Theoretical Claim vs. Empirical Evidence", Cybernetics and Systems 47(7):544-569: a broad survey with quantitative analysis; the authors report the data support the hypotheses and corroborate the VSM. Prior evidence was mostly case studies. — [University of St. Gallen repository](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/249134) [V]; [Vlerick repository record](https://repository.vlerick.com/items/4906c7bd-a438-4ce6-81bd-361c57c030b4) [V]
- Schwaninger (2018, Kybernetes) describes the VSM as corroborated by case studies and that large-scale study; this is self-assessment by the same author, not independent replication. — [Schwaninger 2018, Kybernetes](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/K-01-2018-0019/full/ris) [V]
- **Criticisms.** Jackson (1988) and Ulrich (1981) argue the VSM under-treats purpose, power and culture, can be used autocratically, and rests on organismic analogy; Checkland makes similar points from soft-systems methodology. — [Jackson 1988, J. Management Studies 25(6):557-573](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6486.1988.tb00047.x) [M]
- **Team Syntegrity.** Beer's 1994 protocol for non-hierarchical deliberation: 30 participants mapped to the edges of an icosahedron, 12 topics at the vertices, each person a member of two teams and critic of two others, designed for maximal information reverberation. — [Beer 1994, Beyond Dispute: The Invention of Team Syntegrity, Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Beyond+Dispute%3A+The+Invention+of+Team+Syntegrity-p-9780471944515) [M]

### Inferences
- The VSM's durable contributions to a kernel are (a) the same five-function schema at every level, (b) autonomy of operational units bounded by a resource bargain, (c) separate channels for coordination, audit and alarm, (d) an explicit present-vs-future balance (S3/S4) closed by identity (S5). None is derived; all are consistent with theorems elsewhere (requisite variety, time-scale separation, internal model).
- "Necessary and sufficient for viability" is unproven: there is no formal definition of viability inside the VSM against which sufficiency could be proved. Aubin's viability theory (section 8) supplies such a definition but has not been joined to the VSM.

### Gaps
- I found no accepted mathematical formalisation of the VSM (a formal semantics in which the recursion theorem is provable). Attempts exist in the Kybernetes literature but none was verified this session.
- No independent (non-proponent) quantitative replication of Schwaninger & Scheef was found.
- Team Syntegrity: no controlled evidence of superiority over other deliberation formats was found.

## 3. Control theory proper: limits, hierarchy, decentralisation, layered architecture, supervisory control

### Takeaway
Control theory supplies the hard theorems: conservation laws on robustness, minimum information rates, intractability of decentralised and modular synthesis, and scaling limits of local feedback. Its prescriptions (layering, time-scale separation, structured information patterns) are partly theorems and partly Doyle-style architectural principles.

### Cited Findings
- **Bode sensitivity integral (THEOREM).** For a stable closed loop with loop gain of relative degree >= 2, the integral over frequency of ln|S(jω)| equals π times the sum of real parts of open-loop unstable poles (zero if none). Sensitivity reduction in one band must be paid for by amplification elsewhere: the "waterbed effect", popularised as conservation of fragility. — [Stein 2003, "Respect the Unstable", IEEE Control Systems Magazine 23(4):12-25](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCS.2003.1213600) [M]
- Applied to biology: glycolytic oscillations explained as a necessary consequence of the robustness/efficiency trade-off under autocatalysis, via a Bode-type integral. — [Chandra, Buzi & Doyle 2011, Science 333:187-192](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1200705) [M]
- **Data-rate theorem (THEOREM).** A discrete-time linear plant can be stabilised over a rate-limited feedback channel if and only if the rate R exceeds the sum over unstable eigenvalues of log2|λ_i| bits per sample. Nair, Fagnani, Zampieri & Evans (Proc. IEEE 95(1):108-137, 2007) review the field; for nonlinear plants the threshold is the topological feedback entropy. — [Nair et al. 2007 overview record](https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/scholarlywork/294877-feedback%20control%20under%20data%20rate%20constraints-%20an%20overview) [V]; [Nair, Evans, Mareels & Moran 2004, IEEE TAC 49(9)](https://people.eng.unimelb.edu.au/gnair/TAC04.pdf) [V]; [Tatikonda & Mitter 2004, IEEE TAC 49(7):1056-1068](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2004.831187) [M]
- **Witsenhausen's counterexample (THEOREM + open problem).** A two-stage LQG problem with a non-classical information pattern (the second controller does not know what the first knew) in which nonlinear strategies strictly outperform every linear one; the optimal strategy remains unknown. — [Witsenhausen 1968, SIAM J. Control 6(1):131-147](https://doi.org/10.1137/0306011) [M]; [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witsenhausen%27s_counterexample) [V]
- **Complexity of decentralised control (THEOREMS).** The discrete version of Witsenhausen's problem is NP-complete. — [Papadimitriou & Tsitsiklis 1986, "Intractable problems in control theory", SIAM J. Control Optim. 24(4)](https://www.mit.edu/people/jnt/Papers/J012-86-intractable.pdf) [V]. Finite-horizon decentralised POMDPs and decentralised MDPs with as few as two agents are NEXP-hard (and in NEXP for short horizons), i.e. provably not polynomial, in contrast to centralised MDPs (P) and POMDPs (PSPACE). — [Bernstein, Givan, Immerman & Zilberstein 2002](https://people.cs.umass.edu/%7Eimmerman/pub/bgiz.pdf) [V]. Broader survey: [Blondel & Tsitsiklis 2000, Automatica 36(9):1249-1274](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0005-1098(00)00050-9) [M]
- **When decentralisation is tractable (THEOREMS).** Partially nested information structures make linear strategies optimal for LQG teams (Ho & Chu 1972); quadratic invariance characterises when optimal decentralised synthesis is convex (Rotkowitz & Lall 2006); system level synthesis makes locality and delay constraints convex and gives controllers whose synthesis cost scales with local neighbourhood size rather than global size. — [Rotkowitz & Lall 2006, IEEE TAC 51(2):274-286](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2005.860365) [M]; [Wang, Matni & Doyle, "A System Level Approach to Controller Synthesis", arXiv 1610.04815](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.04815) [M]
- **Consensus and scaling limits of local feedback (THEOREMS).** Linear consensus converges iff the (union) communication graph has a spanning tree; rate is set by the algebraic connectivity λ2. — [Olfati-Saber, Fax & Murray 2007, Proc. IEEE 95(1):215-233](https://doi.org/10.1109/JPROC.2006.887293) [M]. With purely local feedback on d-dimensional lattices, per-node deviation from coherence grows with network size in low dimensions (unbounded in 1D and 2D for consensus-type problems) and stays bounded only in higher dimensions. — [Bamieh, Jovanović, Mitra & Patterson 2012, IEEE TAC 57(9):2235-2249](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2012.2202052) [M]
- **Hierarchical multilevel systems (formal framework with theorems).** Mesarović, Macko & Takahara (1970) distinguish strata (levels of description), layers (levels of decision complexity) and echelons (organisational levels), and prove coordinability results for the interaction-balance and interaction-prediction principles in a set-theoretic and optimisation setting (closely related to Lagrangian/price decomposition, so subject to duality gaps without convexity). — [Mesarović, Macko & Takahara 1970, Theory of Hierarchical, Multilevel, Systems, Academic Press](https://www.sciencedirect.com/bookseries/mathematics-in-science-and-engineering/vol/68) [M]
- **Model predictive control (THEOREMS).** Stability and recursive feasibility follow from terminal cost and terminal invariant-set conditions; hierarchical and distributed MPC architectures are surveyed by Scattolini. — [Mayne, Rawlings, Rao & Scokaert 2000, Automatica 36(6):789-814](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0005-1098(99)00214-9) [M]; [Scattolini 2009, J. Process Control 19(5):723-731](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jprocont.2009.02.003) [M]
- **Doyle: universal laws and architectures (mixed).** Doyle & Csete, "Architecture, constraints, and behavior", PNAS 108 (suppl. 3):15624-15630 (2011), stresses robustness/efficiency trade-offs, the control systems that balance them, their highly organised layered architecture and the resulting fragilities. — [PMC3176601](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/3176601) [V]. "Constraints that deconstrain" (after Gerhart & Kirschner) and layered architecture as definition of architecture appear in Doyle's lectures. — [Doyle lecture slides 2012](https://doyle.caltech.edu/images/doyle/1/16/2_DoyleSageLec2_May14_2012.pdf) [V]. Bow-tie architecture (many inputs, a thin conserved core of carriers/protocols, many outputs) is described for metabolism and transcription/translation in Csete & Doyle 2004. — [Csete & Doyle 2004](https://Www.Gwern.net/doc/biology/2004-csete.pdf) [V]
- Robust-yet-fragile / highly optimised tolerance: systems optimised for robustness to anticipated perturbations are hypersensitive to unanticipated ones (model-based result, not a universal theorem). — [Carlson & Doyle 2002, PNAS 99 (suppl. 1):2538-2545](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.012582499) [M]
- Diversity-enabled sweet spots: layering heterogeneous components (fast-inaccurate with slow-accurate) achieves speed and accuracy that no homogeneous design can; shown theoretically and in sensorimotor experiments. — [Nakahira, Liu, Sarma & Doyle 2021, PNAS 118(22):e1916367118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1916367118) [M]. A quantitative layered multi-rate control framework is proposed in [Matni, Ames & Doyle 2024, arXiv 2401.15185](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15185) [M].
- **Supervisory control theory (THEOREMS).** Ramadge & Wonham: plant G is an automaton over events split into controllable and uncontrollable; a supervisor may only disable controllable events. A specification language K is achievable iff it is controllable (prefix-closure of K followed by an uncontrollable event that the plant allows stays in the prefix-closure of K); the supremal controllable sublanguage exists and is computable in time polynomial in the product state space, giving a minimally restrictive supervisor. Under partial observation, observability is required and no supremal observable sublanguage exists in general (normality is used instead). — [Ramadge & Wonham 1987, SIAM J. Control Optim. 25(1):206-230](https://doi.org/10.1137/0325013) [M]; [Ramadge & Wonham 1989, Proc. IEEE 77(1):81-98](https://doi.org/10.1109/5.21072) [M]; [Wonham's notes page](https://www.control.utoronto.ca/~wonham/) [M]
- **Complexity of SCT (THEOREMS).** Gohari & Wonham (2000) show several controller existence problems in the Ramadge-Wonham framework are NP-hard; Rohloff & Lafortune show deciding existence of a supervisor for a modular system against a global specification, and many modular verification problems, are PSPACE-complete, even for prefix-closed cases. — [Rohloff & Lafortune CDC 2002](https://web.njit.edu/~rohloff/papers/2002/rohloff_lafortune_CDC02.pdf) [V]; [Rohloff & Lafortune 2005 journal record](https://researchwith.njit.edu/en/publications/pspace-completeness-of-modular-supervisory-control-problems/) [V]
- Modular supervision (conjunction of supervisors is valid when the languages are non-conflicting) and hierarchical supervision (hierarchical consistency, observer property of the abstraction) reduce cost when their structural conditions hold; Feng & Wonham combine abstraction and modularity. — [Wonham & Ramadge 1988, modular supervisory control](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/modular-supervisory-control-of-discrete-event-systems/) [V]; [Feng & Wonham, "Computationally efficient supervisor design: abstraction and modularity"](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Walter-Wonham/publication/224644242_Computationally_efficient_supervisor_design_Abstraction_and_modularity/links/547c92eb0cf27ed97861deeb/Computationally-efficient-supervisor-design-Abstraction-and-modularity.pdf) [V]
- Decentralised supervisory control with nonblocking requirements is undecidable in general. — [Tripakis 2004, Information Processing Letters 90(1):21-28](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipl.2004.01.004) [M]

### Inferences
- Three independent hard limits apply to any central controller: (i) information rate must exceed the plant's instability rate (data-rate theorem); (ii) robustness is conserved (Bode); (iii) synthesis for modular/decentralised plants is PSPACE-complete, NEXP-hard or undecidable in the worst case. Tractability returns only under structural conditions: partial nesting, quadratic invariance, locality, non-conflict, hierarchical consistency.
- Those structural conditions are the mathematical content of "good hierarchy": information flows that are nested, interfaces whose abstractions preserve controllability (observer property), and layers separated in time scale.
- SCT is the closest thing in this strand to a compositional, algebraic theory of "permission to act": the supervisor only disables, never forces, and maximal permissiveness is a lattice-theoretic fixpoint. It maps directly onto policy enforcement for software agents.

### Gaps
- Doyle's "universal laws" are a research programme; apart from the specific theorems cited, no general theorem of layered architecture was verified. Whether the 2011 PNAS paper itself uses the terms "hourglass" and "constraints that deconstrain" was not confirmed from full text.
- The exact theorem statements of Gohari & Wonham 2000 were seen only via secondary citation.

## 4. Hierarchy theory: Simon, Koestler, Pattee, Salthe, Ahl & Allen, scaling laws

### Takeaway
Near-decomposability has a rigorous core (Simon-Ando aggregation); the rest of hierarchy theory is conceptual vocabulary. Urban and organisational scaling laws are robust empirical regularities with contested exponents and no agreed derivation.

### Cited Findings
- **Simon 1962 (HEURISTIC with a rigorous core).** Complex systems that evolve are hierarchic and nearly decomposable: intra-component interactions are much stronger and faster than inter-component ones. The watchmaker parable (Hora builds from stable subassemblies of 10, Tempus does not; with interruption probability 0.01 per part on a 1000-part watch Tempus takes about 4000 times longer) argues that stable intermediate forms make evolution of complexity feasible. — [Simon 1962, "The Architecture of Complexity", Proc. American Philosophical Society 106(6):467-482](https://www.jstor.org/stable/985254) [M]
- **Simon-Ando theorem (THEOREM, linear systems).** In a nearly completely decomposable linear dynamic system, in the short run each block approaches its own internal equilibrium nearly independently; in the long run blocks interact only through aggregate variables. This justifies coarse-graining and time-scale separation. — [Simon & Ando 1961, "Aggregation of Variables in Dynamic Systems", Econometrica 29(2):111-138](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1909285) [M]
- **Koestler (CONCEPT).** A holon is simultaneously a whole to its parts and a part of a larger whole ("Janus-faced"), with self-assertive and integrative tendencies; a holarchy is a hierarchy of holons. No formal theory. — [Koestler 1967, The Ghost in the Machine](https://archive.org/details/ghostinmachine00koes) [M]
- **Pattee, Salthe, Ahl & Allen (CONCEPTS).** Pattee: levels are separated by constraints; higher levels are slower rate-independent (symbolic) constraints on lower rate-dependent dynamics (the "epistemic cut"). Salthe: a triadic view (focal level, lower level giving initiating conditions, upper level giving boundary conditions) and the distinction between scalar (compositional) and specification hierarchies. Ahl & Allen: levels are observer-dependent, defined by grain and extent. — [Pattee (ed.) 1973, Hierarchy Theory: The Challenge of Complex Systems](https://archive.org/details/hierarchytheoryc0000unse) [M]; [Ahl & Allen 1996, Hierarchy Theory: A Vision, Vocabulary, and Epistemology, Columbia UP](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/hierarchy-theory/9780231084819) [M]
- **Scaling laws (EMPIRICAL).** Across cities, socio-economic outputs (wages, patents, crime) scale superlinearly with population (exponent about 1.15) and infrastructure sublinearly (about 0.85). — [Bettencourt, Lobo, Helbing, Kühnert & West 2007, PNAS 104(17):7301-7306](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0610172104) [M]; proposed derivation in [Bettencourt 2013, Science 340:1438-1441](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1235823) [M]. Companies scale sublinearly and have approximately constant mortality hazard (half-life about 10 years for US public firms). — [Daepp, Hamilton, West & Bettencourt 2015, J. R. Soc. Interface 12:20150120](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2015.0120) [M]
- **Criticism of scaling.** Exponents depend on how city boundaries are defined, and statistical tests often cannot distinguish nonlinear from linear scaling. — [Arcaute et al. 2015, J. R. Soc. Interface 12:20140745](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2014.0745) [M]; [Leitão, Miotto, Gerlach & Altmann 2016, R. Soc. Open Science 3:150649](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.150649) [M]

### Inferences
- Near-decomposability is the common structural precondition behind tractable hierarchical control (section 3), valid coarse-graining (section 7) and feasible adaptation (Ashby). It is the best candidate for a kernel axiom about structure.
- Scaling laws suggest that organisations with tree-like, centrally controlled resource distribution face economies of scale but bounded growth, while open network-like systems (cities) show increasing returns; this is suggestive for governance design but not a theorem.

### Gaps
- No rigorous nonlinear, stochastic generalisation of Simon-Ando that covers adaptive agents was identified in this session (singular perturbation theory covers smooth cases).

## 5. Second-order cybernetics and self-reference

### Takeaway
This cluster supplies the concepts of closure, self-production and observer-inclusion that a self-reflective foundation needs, but little that is both rigorous and operational. The rigorous fragments are the RAF theory of autocatalytic sets and the algebraic content of Laws of Form (which is Boolean algebra).

### Cited Findings
- **Von Foerster (CONCEPT with a mathematical image).** Second-order cybernetics is the cybernetics of observing systems; stable objects are "eigenbehaviours", fixed points of recursive operations of an observer. — [von Foerster 2003, Understanding Understanding, Springer](https://doi.org/10.1007/b97451) [M]
- **Autopoiesis (DEFINITION + toy model).** A system is autopoietic if it is a network of processes that produces the components which regenerate that network and constitute it as a bounded unity. A minimal computational model was given in 1974. — [Varela, Maturana & Uribe 1974, BioSystems 5(4):187-196](https://doi.org/10.1016/0303-2647(74)90031-8) [M]
- **Luhmann (FRAMEWORK).** Social systems consist of communications (not persons), are operationally closed and autopoietic, and relate to other systems only through structural coupling; function systems operate with binary codes. Descriptive sociology; not formalised, not falsifiable as stated. — [Luhmann 1995 (German 1984), Social Systems, Stanford UP](https://www.sup.org/books/title/?id=2225) [M]
- **Laws of Form (formal, but not new mathematics).** Spencer-Brown's calculus of indications rests on a single primitive, the distinction (mark), with two arithmetic axioms (calling, crossing). Its primary algebra is equivalent to Boolean algebra. The "re-entry" of a form into itself (imaginary Boolean values, oscillation) inspired Varela's calculus for self-reference but has no agreed semantics. — [Banaschewski 1977, "On G. Spencer Brown's Laws of Form", Notre Dame J. Formal Logic 18(3):507-509](https://doi.org/10.1305/ndjfl/1093888028) [M]
- **Rosen's (M,R)-systems (formal, contested).** Metabolism f: A -> B, repair Φ: B -> H(A,B), replication β: H(A,B) -> H(B,H(A,B)); "closure to efficient causation" means every efficient cause (mapping) is produced inside the system. Rosen claimed such systems have non-computable models, hence life is not mechanism. — [Rosen 1991, Life Itself, Columbia UP](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/life-itself/9780231075657) [M]
- **Critiques of Rosen.** The non-computability proof is not valid as given; closure to efficient causation can be expressed in λ-calculus, i.e. computably. — [Chu & Ho 2006, Artificial Life 12(1):117-134](https://doi.org/10.1162/106454606775186392) [M]; [Mossio, Longo & Stewart 2009, J. Theoretical Biology 257(3):489-498](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2008.12.012) [M]
- **Autocatalytic sets (THEOREMS + algorithms).** Kauffman's collectively autocatalytic sets are formalised by Hordijk & Steel as RAF sets (reflexively autocatalytic and food-generated). Deciding whether a chemical reaction system contains a RAF is polynomial-time (worst case O(|R|^2 log|R|)); finding a smallest RAF is NP-hard; the theory relates to Rosen's (M,R)-systems. — [Steel, Hordijk & Xavier 2019, J. R. Soc. Interface, PMC6408349](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6408349) [V]; [Hordijk 2023, concise formal definition, arXiv 2303.01809](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2303.01809) [V]; [Steel & Hordijk 2018, arXiv 1801.03953](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.03953v1) [V]
- **Pask and Bateson (CONCEPTS).** Conversation theory models learning and agreement as conversations between "P-individuals" (coherent conceptual systems) that may be distributed over "M-individuals" (bodies/machines), so one participant can span several substrates and vice versa. Bateson's logical levels of learning (Learning 0-III, after Russell's type theory) describe change, change in the process of change, and so on. — [Pask 1976, Conversation Theory, Elsevier](https://pangaro.com/pask/ConversationTheory.zip) [M]; [Bateson 1972, Steps to an Ecology of Mind](https://archive.org/details/stepstoecologyof0000bate) [M]

### Inferences
- "Organisational closure" (autopoiesis, (M,R), RAF) is the biological analogue of a system that produces its own governors. RAF theory shows closure can be defined compositionally and checked efficiently, which makes it the only member of this family ready for use as a kernel primitive.
- Pask's P/M-individual distinction anticipates the modern need to separate roles/agents-as-coherent-processes from the humans or models that host them.

### Gaps
- No rigorous theory of "structural coupling" or of re-entry was found; Luhmann's and von Foerster's notions remain interpretive.
- Self-reference limits proper (Gödel, Löb, reflective oracles) are another researcher's scope.

## 6. Free energy principle, Markov blankets, Bayesian mechanics, and Perceptual Control Theory

### Takeaway
The FEP is the most ambitious scale-free account of "agents within agents", but its key step (any persisting thing with a Markov blanket can be read as inferring its environment) is established only under restrictive conditions and has drawn specific technical refutations of earlier derivations. Perceptual Control Theory is a simpler hierarchical-control account with good low-level evidence and little formal theory of recursion.

### Cited Findings
- **Core claim (FRAMEWORK with a mathematical core).** Variational free energy upper-bounds surprise; systems that persist at a non-equilibrium steady state with a Markov blanket partition (internal, external, sensory, active states) can be described as if internal states parametrise beliefs about external states and minimise free energy. — [Friston 2010, Nature Reviews Neuroscience 11:127-138](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2787) [M]; [Friston 2013, "Life as we know it", J. R. Soc. Interface 10:20130475](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2013.0475) [M]
- **Nested blankets.** Kirchhoff et al. argue living systems are Markov blankets of Markov blankets, giving a recursive account of autonomy from cells to organisms to collectives; Palacios et al. simulate hierarchical blanket formation. — [Kirchhoff, Parr, Palacios, Friston & Kiverstein 2018, J. R. Soc. Interface 15:20170792](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2017.0792) [M]; [Palacios, Razi, Parr, Kirchhoff & Friston 2020, J. Theoretical Biology 486:110089](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2019.110089) [M]
- **Bayesian mechanics.** Ramstead et al. present the FEP as a "physics of and by beliefs", with path-tracking, mode-tracking and mode-matching formulations and a claimed duality with the constrained maximum-entropy principle. — [Ramstead et al. 2023, Interface Focus 13:20220029, arXiv 2205.11543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11543) [M]
- **Path-integral reformulation.** Friston et al., "Path integrals, particular kinds, and strange things", Physics of Life Reviews 47:35-62 (2023), recasts the FEP as a least-action principle over paths and classifies particles (dissipative/conservative, inert/active, ordinary/strange); "strange" particles' internal states do not directly sense their own actions and so appear to infer them. — [arXiv 2210.12761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.12761) [V]
- **Collective extensions.** Proposals for shared generative models and belief-sharing protocols among many agents. — [Friston et al. 2024, "Designing ecosystems of intelligence from first principles", arXiv 2212.01354](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.01354) [M]; [Kaufmann, Gupta & Taylor 2021, "An active inference model of collective intelligence", Entropy 23(7):830](https://doi.org/10.3390/e23070830) [M]
- **Critique: Pearl vs Friston blankets.** Bruineberg et al. (BBS; online 22 Oct 2021) distinguish "Pearl blankets" (an epistemic tool for inference on a model) from "Friston blankets" (a claimed physical boundary), and argue the literature slides from the former to the latter without justification. — [Cambridge BBS article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/emperors-new-markov-blankets/715C589A73DDF861DCF8997271DE0B8C) [V]; [PhilSci preprint](https://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/18467/1/The%20Emperor's%20New%20Markov%20Blankets.pdf) [V]
- **Critique: narrowness.** Aguilera, Millidge, Tschantz & Buckley (Physics of Life Reviews 40:24-50, 2022) analyse non-equilibrium linear systems with recurrent couplings analytically and find the FEP's conditions (Markov blanket at steady state plus the required flow structure) hold only in a narrow part of parameter space, excluding typical sensorimotor-loop asymmetries. — [arXiv 2105.11203](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.11203) [M]
- **Critique: derivation errors.** Biehl, Pollock & Kanai (Entropy 23(3):293, 2021) show that several equations in "Life as we know it" and the 2019 "particular physics" monograph do not hold in general; Friston, Da Costa & Parr replied with additional assumptions. — [Biehl et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/e23030293) [M]; [Friston, Da Costa & Parr 2021, Entropy 23(8):1076](https://doi.org/10.3390/e23081076) [M]
- A BBS commentary by Seth, Korbak & Tschantz and others continue the debate. — [arXiv 2201.06900](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2201.06900) [V]
- **Perceptual Control Theory (model with evidence at low levels).** Organisms control perceptual inputs, not outputs; a hierarchy of negative-feedback loops in which each level's output sets reference values for the level below; "reorganisation" (random parameter change driven by intrinsic error, after Ashby) accounts for learning; conflict arises when two systems control the same variable to different references. Tracking experiments fit with very high correlations; the higher levels of the proposed hierarchy are much less tested. — [Powers 1973, Behavior: The Control of Perception](https://archive.org/details/behaviorcontrolo0000powe) [M]; [Marken & Mansell 2013, Review of General Psychology 17(2):190-195](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0032933) [M]

### Inferences
- What is solid in FEP for a governance kernel: the conditional-independence (blanket) definition of a boundary/interface, and variational inference as a normative model of a regulator. What is not solid: the claim that every persisting system at every scale has such a blanket and therefore is an agent.
- PCT and VSM converge on the same schema: higher levels set reference signals (goals), not actions, for lower levels; lower levels have autonomy in how to meet them. This "control by setting references" is consistent with MPC/layered control theory and is a strong kernel candidate.

### Gaps
- No proof that blankets compose (that a blanket of blankets satisfies the FEP's conditions given that the parts do) was found.
- Status of the FEP debate after 2023 (whether the path-integral formulation answers Aguilera et al.) was not resolved from sources this session.

## 7. Multi-scale and collective-agency theory

### Takeaway
There are now several quantitative, information-theoretic definitions of "individual", "macro-level" and "emergent", but they disagree with each other and none yields a control theorem except Bar-Yam's multiscale requisite variety, which has been formalised only for a restricted class.

### Cited Findings
- **Multiscale requisite variety (CONDITIONAL THEOREM).** Bar-Yam (2004, Complexity 9(4)) extends Ashby's law by the scale at which responses are needed and whether components act in coordination, and argues that hierarchical control and central planning are limited for complex social problems (the controller's own complexity bounds the collective complexity it can direct at the largest scale). — [NECSI page for the paper](https://necsi.edu/multiscale-variety-in-complex-systems) [V]
- Siegenfeld & Bar-Yam (Entropy 27(8):835, 2025) give a formal definition of scale-dependent complexity (complexity profile); a multi-scale law of requisite variety holds for block-independent systems, Ashby's law does not extend to the profile in general, profiles depend on the chosen partition, and a sum rule expresses a trade-off between fine-scale and large-scale degrees of freedom. — [Entropy 27(8):835](https://mdpi.com/1099-4300/27/8/835) [V]; [arXiv 2206.04896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04896v2) [V]
- **Information theory of individuality (DEFINITIONS).** Krakauer, Bertschinger, Olbrich, Flack & Ay (Theory in Biosciences 139:209-223, 2020): individuals are aggregates that propagate information from their past to their future; decomposing predictive information yields organismal, colonial and environmentally driven forms; individuality is graded, nested and possible at any level. — [PMC7244620](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7244620) [V]
- **TAME (FRAMEWORK).** Levin (Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience 16:768201, 2022): cognition is continuous and substrate-independent; agents are characterised by the spatio-temporal scale of the goals they can pursue (the "cognitive light cone"); collectives of cells scale up goals via bioelectric coupling; claims about agency are engineering claims about which control protocols work. — [Levin 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201/text) [V]; [arXiv 2201.10346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.10346) [V]
- **Causal emergence (MEASURE, contested).** Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi (2013): a coarse-grained macro-model can have higher effective information than the micro-model. — [PNAS 110(49):19790-19795](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314922110) [M]. Hoel's "Causal Emergence 2.0" (arXiv 2503.13395, March 2025) grounds the theory in an axiomatic notion of causation, apportions causal contribution across scales, and defines emergent complexity as how widely causal workings are spread across scales. — [arXiv 2503.13395](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13395v1) [V]
- **Rosas et al.** "Reconciling emergences" (arXiv 2004.08220) gives a partial-information-decomposition criterion for causal emergence in multivariate data. — [arXiv 2004.08220](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08220) [M]. "Software in the natural world" (arXiv 2402.09090; Rosas, Geiger, Luppi, Seth, Polani, Gastpar, Mediano) defines three ways a macro-process can be self-contained (informational, causal, computational closure), with case studies in diffusion, spin models, random walks, agent-based models and neural systems. — [arXiv 2402.09090](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.09090) [V]
- **Flack (CONCEPT).** Slow, coarse-grained collective variables (e.g. power structures) computed by components are fed back as constraints on components; coarse-graining is how downward causation is implemented. — [Flack 2017, Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 375:20160338](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2016.0338) [M]
- **Wolpert: COIN and semantic information.** Collective intelligence design studies how to set private utilities so that selfish learners maximise a world utility; "wonderful life"/difference utilities have high "factoredness" (alignment) and "learnability" (signal-to-noise). — [Wolpert & Tumer 1999, arXiv cs/9908014](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/9908014) [M]. Semantic information is the subset of system-environment correlation that is causally necessary for the system to maintain its own existence (measured by counterfactual scrambling). — [Kolchinsky & Wolpert 2018, Interface Focus 8:20180041, arXiv 1806.08053](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08053) [M]

### Inferences
- The "computational closure" of Rosas et al. and the observer property in hierarchical supervisory control are the same idea in different formalisms: a macro-level is a legitimate level of governance exactly when its coarse-graining commutes with the dynamics (the macro model predicts/controls itself without micro detail).
- Bar-Yam's sum rule gives a precise form to the autonomy/coordination trade-off: large-scale coordinated action is bought by removing fine-scale independent variety from the parts. A hierarchy in which each unit is controlled by a single superior cannot exhibit collective complexity above that superior's.
- Wolpert's difference utilities are a concrete, tested mechanism for aligning 10^n learners with a global objective without central control, complementing the structural prescriptions.

### Gaps
- Causal emergence measures are disputed (dependence on intervention distribution and normalisation); the critique literature was not re-verified this session.
- No theorem links cognitive light cones to any measurable control quantity.

## 8. Viability, resilience, safety: Aubin, Holling, HRO, Perrow, Leveson, Rasmussen

### Takeaway
Viability theory is the rigorous mathematics of "staying within bounds" and is computationally limited by the curse of dimensionality. The safety-science literature (Perrow, Rasmussen, Leveson, HRO) is empirical and qualitative but converges on a control-theoretic reading: accidents are loss of control over constraints across a hierarchy.

### Cited Findings
- **Viability theory (THEOREMS).** For a controlled differential inclusion x' in F(x) and constraint set K, the viability kernel Viab(K) is the largest set of initial states from which at least one evolution stays in K forever; the viability theorem characterises viable sets by a tangential condition (F(x) meets the contingent cone to K at every x in K; a generalisation of Nagumo's 1942 theorem). Regulation maps select viable controls; "heavy" evolutions change controls only when the boundary of viability forces it (inertia principle). — [Aubin, Bayen & Saint-Pierre 2011, Viability Theory: New Directions, Springer](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-16684-6) [M]
- Kernel computation (grid-based, Hamilton-Jacobi reachability) scales exponentially with state dimension; control barrier functions give scalable sufficient conditions for forward invariance (safety filters). — [Bansal, Chen, Herbert & Tomlin 2017, "Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability: A Brief Overview and Recent Advances", arXiv 1709.07523](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07523) [M]; [Ames et al. 2019, "Control Barrier Functions: Theory and Applications", arXiv 1903.11199](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11199) [M]
- **Resilience and panarchy (CONCEPTS).** Holling distinguishes engineering stability (return rate to equilibrium) from ecological resilience (size of disturbance absorbed before regime shift). Panarchy: nested adaptive cycles (growth, conservation, release, reorganisation) at different scales, coupled by "revolt" (fast small-scale collapse cascading up) and "remember" (slow large scales stabilising renewal). — [Holling 1973, Annual Review of Ecology and Systematics 4:1-23](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.es.04.110173.000245) [M]; [Gunderson & Holling (eds.) 2002, Panarchy, Island Press](https://islandpress.org/books/panarchy) [M]
- **Normal Accidents (HEURISTIC, empirical).** Systems with both interactive complexity and tight coupling will have unavoidable system accidents; the organisational dilemma is that interactive complexity calls for decentralisation while tight coupling calls for centralisation. — [Perrow 1984/1999, Normal Accidents, Princeton UP](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691004129/normal-accidents) [M]. Criticised as imprecise and hard to falsify. — [Hopkins 1999, "The limits of normal accident theory", Safety Science 32(2-3):93-102](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0925-7535(99)00015-6) [M]
- **High Reliability Organisations (EMPIRICAL case studies).** Carrier flight decks, air traffic control and nuclear plants sustain reliability by switching from hierarchical to expertise-based decision structures under tempo, redundancy and a preoccupation with failure. — [La Porte & Consolini 1991, J. Public Administration Research and Theory 1(1):19-48](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.jpart.a037070) [M]
- **Rasmussen (MODEL).** Work systems migrate under gradients of cost-efficiency and least effort toward the boundary of acceptable performance ("drift"); safety requires making boundaries visible and controlling across the whole socio-technical hierarchy (government, regulators, company, management, staff, work). — [Rasmussen 1997, Safety Science 27(2-3):183-213](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0925-7535(97)00052-0) [M]
- **STAMP/STPA (METHOD).** Leveson: safety is a control problem; accidents result from inadequate enforcement of safety constraints by a hierarchical control structure. Each control loop has controller, process model, control actions and feedback; four kinds of unsafe control action (not provided, provided when unsafe, wrong timing/order, wrong duration); many accidents trace to a process model inconsistent with the process. — [Leveson 2004, Safety Science 42(4):237-270](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0925-7535(03)00047-X) [M]; [Leveson 2011, Engineering a Safer World, MIT Press (open access)](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262533690/engineering-a-safer-world/) [M]; [Leveson & Thomas 2018, STPA Handbook](https://psas.scripts.mit.edu/home/get_file.php?name=STPA_handbook.pdf) [M]

### Inferences
- Viability theory gives the formal definition the VSM lacks: governance at each level = a regulation map that keeps state in a constraint set; the "safe operating envelope" is the viability kernel. The inertia principle formalises management by exception.
- Leveson's "process model inconsistent with process" is the operational face of the good-regulator results; STPA is effectively a manual audit of requisite variety and model adequacy for every control loop in a hierarchy.
- Perrow's dilemma is resolved in HRO practice by mode-switching (centralised norms, decentralised action), which is again control by constraints/references rather than commands.

### Gaps
- No compositional viability theorem (viability kernel of a coupled system from the kernels of its parts) was verified; assume-guarantee/contract methods for invariance exist in the control literature but were not sourced here.

## 9. Constructor theory, assembly theory, General Systems Theory, mathematical systems theory, DEVS

### Takeaway
DEVS and its ancestors (Wymore, Mesarović-Takahara) are the only items here that actually deliver a proved "system of systems is a system" property (closure under coupling). Constructor theory and assembly theory are young and contested, and neither yet supports engineering of governance.

### Cited Findings
- **General System Theory (PROGRAMME).** Von Bertalanffy proposed isomorphic laws across disciplines and the open-system concept (steady state, equifinality); it is a research orientation rather than a theory. — [von Bertalanffy 1968, General System Theory, Braziller](https://archive.org/details/generalsystemthe0000bert) [M]
- **Klir (FRAMEWORK, formal taxonomy).** An epistemological hierarchy of systems: source, data, generative, structure and meta-systems, each level adding knowledge about the one below. — [Klir 1985, Architecture of Systems Problem Solving, Plenum](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-1168-4) [M]
- **Wymore (FORMAL).** Wymore's 1967 mathematical systems theory defines systems as input/state/output structures; composing systems under a coupling specification gives a resultant that is again a well-defined system (closure under coupling). — [summary in a systems-of-systems paper, SciTePress 2014](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2014/68138/68138.pdf) [V]
- **DEVS (THEOREMS).** Zeigler's Discrete Event System Specification: atomic models (state set, input/output events, internal and external transition functions, output function, time advance) and coupled models. The class is closed under coupling, which justifies hierarchical, modular construction and an abstract simulator; closure has been proved for extensions such as DEV&DESS (Praehofer), and absence of closure for a subclass motivates finding the smallest closed class containing it. — [Zeigler 2018, "Closure under coupling: concept, proofs, DEVS recent examples", University of Arizona repository](https://repository.arizona.edu/handle/10150/634636) [V]; [Zeigler, Praehofer & Kim 2000, ch. 1 (TU Delft copy)](https://simulation.tudelft.nl/SEN9110/lectures/04%20ZeiglerEtAl_2000_Ch1_Introduction%20to%20Systems%20Modeling%20Concepts.pdf) [V]. A 2025 preprint (Zeigler, Kewley & Wainer) treats closure, universality and uniqueness; not peer reviewed. — [preprints.org 202510.1207](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202510.1207) [V]
- **Behavioural approach (FORMAL).** Willems: a system is a set of allowed trajectories (behaviour); interconnection is variable sharing (intersection of behaviours), with no a priori input/output distinction; control is interconnection. — [Willems 2007, IEEE Control Systems Magazine 27(6):46-99](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCS.2007.906923) [M]
- **Constructor theory (PROGRAMME).** Laws of physics are expressed as statements about which transformations (tasks) are possible or impossible and why; a constructor is an entity that can cause a task and retain the ability to do so again. Information is characterised by which copying/permutation tasks are possible; life is addressed via self-reproducing constructors under "no-design" laws. No distinctive confirmed predictions so far. — [Deutsch 2013, Synthese 190:4331-4359, arXiv 1210.7439](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.7439) [M]; [Deutsch & Marletto 2015, Proc. R. Soc. A 471:20140540, arXiv 1405.5563](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5563) [M]; [Marletto 2015, J. R. Soc. Interface 12:20141226, arXiv 1407.0681](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.0681) [M]
- **Assembly theory (MEASURE, disputed).** The assembly index is the minimal number of joining steps to build an object from basic parts with reuse; combined with copy number it is claimed to quantify selection. — [Sharma et al. 2023, Nature 622:321-328](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06600-9) [M]. Critics argue it is an approximation to algorithmic complexity based on LZ-type compression that does not explain selection, and that copy-number-based assembly is equivalent to Shannon entropy. — [Abrahão et al. 2024, arXiv 2403.06629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06629v3) [V]; [Ozelim et al., arXiv 2408.15108](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15108v7) [V]. Proponents reply that the assembly index is in a different computational complexity class from Shannon entropy, Huffman coding and LZW and answers a different question. — [Kempes et al. 2024, arXiv 2406.12176](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12176) [V]

### Inferences
- "Closure under coupling" is the precise, provable form of "a system of systems is a system". Any candidate foundation should be required to state and prove it for its own primitives; DEVS shows how (define the resultant, prove it is in the class) and that the property can fail for natural-looking subclasses.
- DEVS closure guarantees well-defined composite behaviour, not good behaviour: it carries no notion of goal, control or viability. It is a substrate, to be combined with supervisory control or viability constraints.
- Constructor theory's primitive (a task that is possible/impossible; a constructor that retains its capability) is conceptually close to capability/permission-based governance, but there is no calculus yet at the engineering level.

### Gaps
- Marletto & Deutsch sources were not re-fetched; no 2024-2026 constructor-theory results relevant to systems governance were found.
- The dispute over assembly theory is unresolved; peer-review status of the critical preprints was not checked.

## 10. Formal limits on centralised control of 10^10 actors, prescribed structure, and synthesis (theorems vs heuristics; minimal kernel)

### Takeaway
Four independent families of results (variety/information, bandwidth/delay, robustness conservation, computational complexity) each rule out flat central control at 10^10 scale, and the theories agree on the remedy: nearly decomposable recursive structure, local autonomy inside constraints set from above, thin standard interfaces, and aggregated exception-based upward information. Only part of that remedy is theorem; the rest is convergent heuristic.

### Cited Findings
- **Variety limit.** Outcome entropy cannot be reduced below disturbance entropy minus regulator capacity; closed-loop gain is bounded by acquired mutual information. With N roughly independent actors, H(D) grows linearly in N while a centre's channel capacity is fixed. — [Ashby 1956](http://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/books/IntroCyb.pdf) [M]; [Touchette & Lloyd 2000](https://arxiv.org/abs/chao-dyn/9905039) [M]
- **Multiscale limit.** A single controller's complexity bounds the large-scale complexity of what it directly controls; coordination at large scale trades against fine-scale variety (sum rule; proved for block-independent systems). — [Siegenfeld & Bar-Yam 2025](https://mdpi.com/1099-4300/27/8/835) [V]; [Bar-Yam 2004](https://necsi.edu/multiscale-variety-in-complex-systems) [V]
- **Bandwidth limit.** Stabilisation needs feedback rate above the sum of log unstable eigenvalue magnitudes; unstable modes across many subsystems add. — [Nair et al. 2007](https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/scholarlywork/294877-feedback%20control%20under%20data%20rate%20constraints-%20an%20overview) [V]
- **Robustness limit.** Sensitivity is conserved (Bode); delay and unstable dynamics worsen the trade-off; optimisation for known disturbances creates fragility to unknown ones. — [Stein 2003](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCS.2003.1213600) [M]; [Doyle & Csete 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/3176601) [V]
- **Scaling limit of purely local control.** Local feedback alone cannot maintain global coherence in low-dimensional interaction topologies as N grows. — [Bamieh et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2012.2202052) [M]
- **Computational limit.** Decentralised optimal control is NP-complete (discrete Witsenhausen) to NEXP-hard (DEC-POMDP); modular supervisor existence is PSPACE-complete; decentralised nonblocking supervision is undecidable. — [Papadimitriou & Tsitsiklis 1986](https://www.mit.edu/people/jnt/Papers/J012-86-intractable.pdf) [V]; [Bernstein et al. 2002](https://people.cs.umass.edu/%7Eimmerman/pub/bgiz.pdf) [V]; [Rohloff & Lafortune 2005](https://researchwith.njit.edu/en/publications/pspace-completeness-of-modular-supervisory-control-problems/) [V]
- **Requisite hierarchy.** Aulin's law of requisite hierarchy: the weaker the average regulatory ability and the larger the uncertainty, the more hierarchy is needed to achieve the same regulation; conversely better local regulation permits flatter structure. — [Aulin-Ahmavaara 1979, Kybernetes 8(4):259-266](https://doi.org/10.1108/eb005528) [M]
- **Prescribed structure.** Recursion with autonomous operational units and a resource bargain (Beer); near-decomposability with time-scale separation (Simon & Ando); coordination by prices/predictions rather than commands (Mesarović et al.); layered architectures with thin conserved interfaces (Doyle); higher levels set references not actions (Powers); maximally permissive supervision that only disables (Ramadge & Wonham); constraints enforced at every level of a control hierarchy with feedback (Leveson). — [Beer 1984](https://doi.org/10.1057/jors.1984.2) [M]; [Simon & Ando 1961](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1909285) [M]; [Ramadge & Wonham 1989](https://doi.org/10.1109/5.21072) [M]; [Leveson 2004](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0925-7535(03)00047-X) [M]

### Inferences

**Back-of-envelope consequence for 10^10 actors (inference, not sourced).** With span of control b per level, a recursive structure needs about log_b(10^10) levels: 10 levels at b = 10, 5 at b = 100, about 3.3 at b = 1000. Each level must attenuate variety by roughly a factor of b (in the number of units it reports on) to keep every node within fixed capacity, so upward channels must carry aggregates and exceptions only. Delay accumulates per level, so stability requires each level to act on a slower time scale than the one below (time-scale separation), with an algedonic-style bypass for fast alarms.

**Candidate minimal kernel (inference).** The smallest set of ideas from this strand that is both rigorous and jointly sufficient to state a recursive governance scheme:
1. *System and composition:* an input/state/output (or behavioural) system class that is closed under coupling (Wymore/DEVS/Willems). This is the recursion axiom made provable.
2. *Boundary/interface:* a conditional-independence or variable-sharing interface between inside and outside (Markov blanket in the Pearl sense; Willems' shared variables), without the FEP's metaphysics.
3. *Viability:* a constraint set and its viability kernel as the definition of "under control" (Aubin); governance = a regulation map keeping state in the kernel.
4. *The control loop with model:* controller, process model, actuation, feedback (Leveson's loop), with three necessary conditions: requisite variety/information rate (Ashby, data-rate), adequate model (Richens et al., internal model principle), conserved robustness (Bode).
5. *Supervision as constraint:* higher levels restrict (disable, set references/budgets), lower levels choose within what is left; maximal permissiveness as the formal content of autonomy and subsidiarity (Ramadge-Wonham; Beer's resource bargain; Powers' reference signals).
6. *Level-forming condition:* a coarse-graining is a legitimate higher level only when it is dynamically closed and preserves controllability (Simon-Ando aggregation; observer property/hierarchical consistency; computational closure of Rosas et al.).
7. *Time-scale separation and exception channel:* slower dynamics at higher levels, plus a fast bypass for viability threats (Ashby's ultrastability, Beer's algedonics, panarchy's revolt/remember).

Items 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 each have theorems; item 7 and the specific five-function decomposition of the VSM remain heuristic. What the VSM adds beyond the kernel is a checklist of which loops must exist (coordination, audit, intelligence, identity), which is plausibly derivable but has not been derived.

**Self-reflection.** Within this strand, self-reference is handled by double-loop structures (ultrastability, Bateson's learning levels, S4/S5 modelling the organisation itself, second-order cybernetics) rather than by any theorem; the rigorous statement nearest to "a governor must model itself-in-the-world" is Richens et al.'s world-model results, which do not address self-models.

### Gaps
- No source was found that proves the combined scheme (kernel items 1-7) composes: for example, that viability kernels or supervisory controllers of parts yield a viable/controllable whole under stated interface conditions. This is the main open theoretical question and the likely point of contact with the category-theory strand.
- No quantitative study measuring variety, bandwidth or delay limits in real organisations at 10^9-10^10 scale was found; the scaling argument above is my own inference.
- Formal statements of Aulin's law of requisite hierarchy and its proof conditions were not re-read.
