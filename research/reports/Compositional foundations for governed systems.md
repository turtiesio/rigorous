# Small rigorous substrates exist; the integrated foundation is unbuilt

Several small, rigorous, compositional substrates already exist, and none of them, alone or combined, has been shown to deliver the whole of what the question asks; this report also does not show that the full combination is impossible. Seven bodies of prior art (applied category theory, logic and formal methods, concurrency and distributed systems, cybernetics and control, organisation and institutional theory, the mathematics of self-reference, and the 2022-2026 AI-agent governance frontier) each have a theorem-grade core covering a different part of the problem. Closure under composition ("a system of systems is a system") is available by construction in operad algebras, in process calculi and, for the general class, in DEVS coupling. Substitutivity, meaning a part can be replaced by an equivalent part without rechecking the whole, holds outright in some settings (strong bisimilarity in CCS, contract refinement) and under side conditions in others (weak equivalences, name passing, bigraphs). At least one formal result on governance across levels exists (a compliance checker proved sound and complete against its authors' formal semantics for institutional models), alongside an enforcement architecture in which subordinate laws conform to superior laws by construction. What no reviewed work shows is authority, membership, liability and control carried faithfully through every level from resource to nation. Guarantees do not come free with expressiveness: Rice and Apt-Kozen rule out a complete automatic verifier for arbitrary programs and for the general problem of unboundedly many actors (restricted families remain decidable), Gödel and Löb rule out unconditional self-certification by a consistent, effectively axiomatised theory containing arithmetic, and worst-case decentralised optimal control is NEXP-hard or undecidable. These results do not rule out a universal foundation. They mean that guarantees over a universal language are paid for with proof effort, conservatism, runtime enforcement, a guarantee that holds only with stated probability or under computational assumptions, or accountability after the fact, and the last gives deterrence and not a guarantee. The evidence at scale is for two patterns. One is runtime enforcement of restricted policy languages, with proof effort spent on the enforcing engine: in one case a formally verified engine is reported at about a billion authorization decisions per second. The other is static admission of general-purpose code by a small type checker for a fixed safety invariant, as in WebAssembly validation†. Admission with actor-supplied proofs of richer invariants is at research stage. Properties compose conditionally and one by one. Safety invariants, capability confinement, monotone state and refinement-ordered contracts have the best-developed composition theorems; liveness and controlled invariance have conditional ones; some incentive impossibilities soften in large populations of interchangeable actors, and the aggregation and agreement impossibilities do not. Formal coverage is strong for specified components and thin for autonomous principals (humans, learned agents, third-party services) and for authority that is constituted by recognition instead of delegated. The prior art suggests a recurring checklist of nine architectural elements, which is this report's inference and is neither minimal nor complete: it omits identity, time, information, goals and probability. No published formalism integrates these elements with a proved composition law, and this survey found no demonstration of the full combination for anything near 10^10 independently acting principals. Coordination among 10^9 people exists in at least four shapes: thin-protocol federation, centrally rooted identity with a narrow vocabulary, price-mediated exchange, and polycentric legal hierarchy.

## Seven strands each solve one piece, and none solves the whole

The comparison below comes before any synthesis, because the central finding is negative: every strand has a theorem-grade core, each core covers a different part of the problem, and no strand's own experts claim sufficiency. The comparison is organised by field and not by single candidate formalism, and it does not test any single candidate against each level from resource to nation. A later section walks the ten levels once across all the fields; a candidate-by-candidate test on a common worked example remains to be done.

| Candidate foundation | Primitives | What it proves about composition | What it does not express | Largest evidence of use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poly, operads, double-categorical systems theory | Interfaces, dependent lenses, wiring, modules of systems | Nesting is an exact operad law; behaviour composes strictly or laxly per doctrine | Goals, norms, authority, identity; no theorem for self-rewiring systems | Hundreds of hand-wired components (epidemiology, ODE models) |
| Contract algebra (assume-guarantee, hypercontracts) | Contract = (environments, implementations); refinement, composition, conjunction, quotient | Independent implementability; quotient is right adjoint to composition | Authority, obligation with violation, knowledge | Automotive and aerospace design flows |
| Proof assistants with a small trusted kernel (Isabelle/HOL, Rocq, Lean; Iris as resource layer) | Types, proofs, resource algebras, invariants | Small trusted checker; frame rule for local reasoning | Norms and time only by encoding; proof cost is high | seL4 (Isabelle/HOL), CompCert (Rocq), Cedar's model (Lean) |
| Process calculi, bigraphs, actors with supervision | Units, asynchronous messages, names, nested places | Strong bisimilarity is a congruence in CCS and, where relative pushouts exist, in bigraphs; pi-calculus bisimilarity is not preserved by input prefix; supervision recursion in production | Authority (except via names), obligation, data consistency | 10^6 connections per node, 10^8-10^9 users per service |

### Category theory gives the cleanest closure law and admits it lacks agency, norms and scale

Applied category theory supplies the cleanest statement of recursion. An operad of wiring diagrams gives the syntax of boxes nested in boxes ([Spivak 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0297))†, and open dynamical systems form an algebra over it, so wiring open systems yields an open system coherently at every depth ([Vagner, Spivak & Lerman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1598))†. The most compact single candidate is the category of polynomial functors: one category whose objects are interfaces and whose morphisms are bidirectional interaction patterns, with book-length treatment of its monoidal structures, comonoids and bicomodules ([Niu & Spivak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00990)). It reaches self-rewiring structure through the double category Org of "dynamic organizations", whose worked examples are prediction markets and deep learning ([Shapiro & Spivak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03906)), and since February 2026 through interfaces that themselves change, in a single-author preprint ([Spivak 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917)). The most general candidate is the double operadic theory of systems, which defines a systems theory as a module of systems over a double category of interfaces and interactions and recovers Petri nets over undirected wiring and Moore machines over lenses as doctrines ([Libkind & Myers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329)).

The field's own record marks the limits. **The paper claiming task delegation for hierarchical agents on Org was withdrawn** after its authors found the relevant Kleisli category was pre-monoidal rather than monoidal ([arXiv 2410.08373](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08373)). The double operadic paper is titled "Towards", and its authors state that it is "not always possible" to characterise a composite's behaviour in terms of its parts ([Libkind & Myers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329)). Whether the stochastic and non-deterministic systems theories of this framework supported clock systems, which represent behaviours, was open until March 2026 ([Lynch, Myers, Rischel & Staton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29573)). The result restricts stochasticity to the systems themselves, handles the non-deterministic case by definition, and is not the origin of stochastic compositional semantics generally. The only strand that models agency, categorical cybernetics, composes the definition of equilibrium and of Bayesian update through parametrised optics ([Ghani et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04641); [Capucci et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06332)), yet its originator's own slides say "we can't compute equilibria" and "realising the promised benefits of ACT is still hard" ([Hedges 2020](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/mathematical/ACTUCR/Hedges_Open_Games.pdf)). The proposal for agents managing agents and meta-agents altering their own structure exists only as an extended abstract ([Smithe 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07577)).

The strand's most useful export for governance is a precise, modest definition of emergence. A generative effect is the failure of an observation map to preserve composition ([Fong & Spivak](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05316))†; the reachability semantics of open Petri nets is the standard example of a semantics that is lax rather than strict ([Baez & Master](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05415), publication data checked, the laxness statement cited from memory). The researchers infer the practical rule: treat each property of interest as a semantics functor and ask whether it is strict (the composite's value is fixed exactly by the parts' values) or lax (it is only bounded by them). Strictness is an algebraic fact and not a complexity bound: computing a strictly compositional property can still be intractable, and a lax abstraction can still give a sound local sufficient condition. No framework removes emergence; each locates it. Sheaf theory supplies the complementary audit tool, the formal meaning of "locally consistent, globally inconsistent": under sheaf-Laplacian diffusion the only stable opinion distributions are global sections of the discourse sheaf ([Hansen & Ghrist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12798)).

On adoption, the documented successes are compositional epidemiological modelling and scientific model management ([Baez et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08373)), and the largest institutional bet is the UK ARIA Safeguarded AI programme, which funds double categorical systems theory under David Jaz Myers ([ARIA](https://www.aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces/mathematics-for-safe-ai/safeguarded-ai/meet-the-creators)). The researchers found no category-theoretic framework governing a large organisation or multi-agent deployment, no test above hundreds of components, and no treatment of permissions, obligations, delegation or actor identity; they flag that last absence as the result of a limited search rather than proof.

### Logic supplies an algebra of contracts and small trusted checkers

Formal methods contribute two kernels at opposite ends. The most minimal is the algebra of contracts. In the meta-theory of Benveniste and colleagues a contract is a pair of sets, admissible environments and admissible implementations, and assume-guarantee contracts, interface automata, modal interfaces and rely-guarantee reasoning are all instances ([Benveniste et al.](https://nowpublishers.com/article/Details/EDA-053)). Hypercontracts extend the algebra to hyperproperties and show the quotient, the most permissive missing component, is the right adjoint of composition ([Incer et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02449)). The recurring signature is a refinement preorder, a monotone parallel product, a meet and a residual. The researchers observe that this is the same residuated shape as linear and separation logic, and that it is recursive by construction: a composed contract is a contract. Its limit is that it is a shell. Circular assume-guarantee reasoning is sound for safety assumptions and not for liveness in general ([Abadi & Lamport](https://doi.org/10.1145/203095.201069))†, so circular mutual promises of eventual action are not sound on their own. Liveness still composes under added conditions such as fairness, scheduling assumptions or well-founded ranking arguments, and the same authors' composition theorem covers liveness guarantees with separate premises for the safety part ([Abadi & Lamport, full paper](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/abadi-conjoining.pdf)).

The most general kernel is a proof assistant with a small trusted checker, in higher-order logic or dependent type theory, used as a meta-language. Such systems have carried large verified artefacts (seL4 in Isabelle/HOL, CompCert in Rocq), which establishes feasibility and not a unique foundation, and the cost is documented: **about 200,000 lines of Isabelle proof for roughly 7,500 lines of C in seL4, about 25 person-years for design, implementation and verification together** ([Trustworthy Systems](https://trustworthy.systems/projects/OLD/l4.verified/numbers)). A study of three verified distributed systems found 16 bugs, none in verified code and all in specifications, unverified shims and tools ([Fonseca et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3064176.3064183))†. Verification relocates risk to the specification and to the boundary with the unverified world, which for organisations, where the specification is contested social intent, is the dominant problem. The architecture that matters for governing untrusted AI agents is the split between arbitrary proof search and a tiny checker: Cedar's authorization semantics is modelled in Lean and tied to production Rust code by differential testing, a process that found 4 soundness bugs by proof and 21 by differential and property-based testing ([Disselkoen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01688v1)). At larger scale, AWS replaced its authorization engine with one proved correct in Dafny against a formal specification, reported as invoked a billion times per second ([Chakarov et al.](https://conf.researchr.org/details/icse-2025/icse-2025-research-track/119/Formally-Verified-Cloud-Scale-Authorization)). That is formal verification of one property, authorization decisions, under one operator and over a restricted policy language.

For resources and interaction, separation logic's frame rule gives local reasoning that survives any larger context ([Reynolds](https://doi.org/10.1109/LICS.2002.1029817))†, and Iris generalises ownership to user-defined resource algebras with invariants inside a proof assistant ([Jung et al.](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-functional-programming/article/iris-from-the-ground-up-a-modular-foundation-for-higherorder-concurrent-separation-logic/26301B518CE2C52796BFA12B8BAB5B5F)). Multiparty session types give correctness by construction for closed, pre-agreed protocols, with a soundness history that needed correction in 2019 and repair in 2024-2026 ([Scalas & Yoshida](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290343); [Hou, Yoshida & Kuhn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16741v3)).

Normative logic teaches a negative lesson. Standard deontic logic cannot represent contrary-to-duty obligations consistently ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-deontic/))†; the working treatments make norms non-truth-bearing conditional pairs ([Makinson & van der Torre](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/DagSemProc.07122.32)) or prioritised defaults mirroring statutory drafting, as in Catala, which encoded a section of the US tax code and French family benefits and exposed a bug in the official French implementation ([Merigoux et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03198v2)). The researchers' reading is that a norm is typed data with a monitor semantics, not a modal formula. Authority is the one normative area with proven planetary scale, and it reached that scale by being decidable, monotone or stratified, default-deny and locally evaluable; the "says" and "speaks for" calculus, introduced by Abadi, Burrows, Lampson and Plotkin†, makes delegation chains proofs ([Garg & Abadi](https://rd.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-540-78499-9_16.pdf)). Experts expect no single universal object logic: the Stanford Encyclopedia entry on combining logics treats "why are there so many logics instead of just one?" as a central question of the field ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2014/entries/logic-combining)), and institution theory answers structurally by making the logic itself a composable parameter under the slogan that truth is invariant under change of notation ([Goguen & Burstall](https://doi.org/10.1145/147508.147524)). The researchers found no implemented kernel integrating dependent types, a separation-style resource layer and modalities for time, knowledge, obligation and authority. Partial integrations exist: a linear logic with per-principal affirmation and knowledge modalities, with cut elimination proved, dates from 2006 ([Garg et al.](https://www.mpi-sws.org/~dg/papers/aff-know.pdf)), and proof-carrying authorization gives delegation a checked logical semantics ([Bauer et al.](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~lbauer/papers/pcaprototr.pdf)).

### Concurrency theory and planetary systems agree on federation, thin interfaces and local coordination

Process calculi share a tiny kernel of parallel composition, name scoping and one interaction rule, and their payoff is a behavioural equivalence that is a congruence, so an equivalent part can replace a part in any context without re-verifying the whole ([Milner](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-10235-3))†. That holds for strong bisimilarity in CCS; weak bisimilarity is not preserved by sum, which is why Milner defined observation congruence. In the pi-calculus, early and late bisimilarity are not preserved by input prefix, and congruence has to be recovered by closing under substitution ([Bengtson & Parrow](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.3960)). Bigraphs are the smallest framework that makes nesting and linking independent first-class structures, a place graph and a link graph over the same nodes, and recover CCS, the pi-calculus, ambients and Petri nets as instances ([Milner 2009](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511626661))†. The choice of primitives is not neutral: no reasonable encoding takes synchronous mixed choice into the asynchronous pi-calculus, because mixed choice solves leader election in symmetric networks ([Palamidessi](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0960129503004043))†. The researchers prefer an asynchronous kernel with receiver-local choice for very large systems; that is an engineering judgement and not a consequence of the separation result.

The actor model has the strongest production record, and Erlang/OTP adds the one deployed mechanism that is literally recursive governance: the supervision tree, in which each supervisor restarts its children and escalates to its own supervisor when restart intensity is exceeded ([Erlang/OTP](https://www.erlang.org/doc/system/sup_princ.html))†. A 2014 account of WhatsApp reports roughly 465 million monthly users on about 11,000 cores with more than 70 million Erlang messages per second ([High Scalability](https://highscalability.com/how-whatsapp-grew-to-nearly-500-million-users-11000-cores-an/), search excerpt). Authority composes through object capabilities, where a reference is the permission and is acquired only by creation, endowment or introduction ([Miller](http://www.erights.org/talks/thesis/markm-thesis.pdf))†; this is the same rule as the actor locality laws ([Hewitt & Baker](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/41962))†. Attenuating bearer credentials are deployed: a macaroon holder can add caveats offline and pass the narrowed token on ([Birgisson et al.](https://research.google/pubs/macaroons-cookies-with-contextual-caveats-for-decentralized-authorization-in-the-cloud/)), and services use them for access grants. These are credentials checked by a verifier and not object-capability confinement. Open capability standards remain drafts: the UCAN specification lists a 1.0 release candidate with no final version ([ucan-wg](https://github.com/ucan-wg/spec)), and OCapN's documents are not frozen ([OCapN](https://github.com/ocapn/ocapn)).

Distributed computing draws the coordination boundary exactly. A program has a consistent coordination-free implementation if and only if it is monotonic ([Hellerstein & Alvaro](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01930)), and a set of transactions preserves an invariant without coordination if and only if it is invariant-confluent; **10 of 12 TPC-C invariants pass that test** ([Bailis et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2237)). The notes caution that CALM's formal proof lives in the relational-transducer model and should not be quoted as model-independent. Preventing double-spending of an owned asset does not need consensus at all ([Guerraoui et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05574)). Systems at billions of endpoints take several shapes. One is a federation of autonomous domains joined by a deliberately thin protocol, with delegated hierarchical naming and no global controller: the IPv4 routing table held **1,044,785 entries announced by 78,339 autonomous systems** in April 2026 ([APNIC report](https://seclists.org/nanog/2026/Apr/44)) while serving about 6 billion Internet users ([ITU](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2025-11-17-Facts-and-Figures.aspx)). The largest publicly documented centrally reconciled control domain is a 130,000-node Kubernetes cluster run experimentally ([Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/containers-kubernetes/how-we-built-a-130000-node-gke-cluster/)), five orders of magnitude short of 10^10. The clearest worked example of local rules with a proven global guarantee is interdomain routing: with arbitrary policies stability is NP-complete to decide and can fail ([Griffin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1109/90.993304))†, while under local customer-provider rules and an acyclic hierarchy convergence is guaranteed without global coordination ([Gao & Rexford](https://doi.org/10.1109/90.974523))†. That guarantee has limits: acyclicity is a global property no single network can check, measurements find 14-35% of routing decisions unexplained by the model ([Anwar et al.](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~phillipa/papers/AnwarIMC15.pdf)), and convergence gives no protection against hijacks or route leaks. Another is centrally rooted: single-operator services, the identity and payment systems described below, and Google's Zanzibar authorization service, which keeps access lists externally consistent on a globally replicated database that runs consensus ([Pang et al.](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc19/presentation/pang)). Price-mediated exchange and polycentric legal hierarchy, covered below, are two more, and the shapes combine: a service can have central identity issuance and federated execution.

### Cybernetics has hard limits on any controller and only a heuristic for recursion

The law of requisite variety is an elementary inequality: outcome entropy cannot fall below disturbance entropy minus regulator capacity ([Ashby](http://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/books/IntroCyb.pdf))†. The good regulator theorem is far weaker than its title, and a 2021 critique shows its notion of "model" is nearly vacuous ([Wentworth](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/Dx9LoqsEh3gHNJMDk/fixing-the-good-regulator-theorem)); the versions strong enough to bear weight are recent, proving that agents robust across a large family of distribution shifts have learned an approximate causal model ([Richens & Everitt](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/44a2b9f7bf9aec3f1fa333ad875b0ee0-Abstract-Conference.html)) and that general multi-step agents contain an extractable world model ([Richens et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01622)), under assumptions (a finite, stationary, communicating controlled Markov process and a bounded goal-conditioned agent) that an institution or human team does not automatically satisfy. Control theory adds a minimum feedback rate for stabilisation, the sum of the logarithms of the unstable eigenvalue magnitudes ([Nair et al.](https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/scholarlywork/294877-feedback%20control%20under%20data%20rate%20constraints-%20an%20overview)), and a conservation law on sensitivity ([Stein](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCS.2003.1213600))†.

Stafford Beer's Viable System Model is the most direct prior art for the same structure at every level, and it is a structured heuristic. Its "Recursive System Theorem" is an axiom, and its claim of necessity and sufficiency has no formal definition of viability to be proved against ([Beer](https://doi.org/10.1057/jors.1984.2))†. Its main quantitative support is one survey by a proponent ([Schwaninger & Scheef](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/249134)), with no independent replication found. The free energy principle, the most ambitious scale-free account of agents within agents, slides from a statistical tool to a claimed physical boundary without justification, according to a Behavioral and Brain Sciences target article ([Bruineberg et al.](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/emperors-new-markov-blankets/715C589A73DDF861DCF8997271DE0B8C)), and an analysis of linear systems finds its conditions hold only in a narrow part of parameter space ([Aguilera et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.11203))†. The multiscale extension of Ashby's law is proved only for block-independent systems and does not extend to the complexity profile in general ([Siegenfeld & Bar-Yam](https://mdpi.com/1099-4300/27/8/835)).

The rigorous pieces that do exist fit together. Closure under coupling is proved for DEVS and shown to fail for some natural subclasses ([Zeigler](https://repository.arizona.edu/handle/10150/634636)). Supervisory control theory gives an algebra of permission in which a supervisor only disables controllable events and a maximally permissive supervisor exists as a fixpoint ([Ramadge & Wonham](https://doi.org/10.1137/0325013))†. Near-decomposability has a theorem behind it for linear systems, justifying coarse-graining and time-scale separation ([Simon & Ando](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1909285))†. Viability theory defines "under control" as remaining in the viability kernel of a constraint set ([Aubin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-16684-6))†. Composition of these pieces is conditional. A conjunction of supervisors is valid when their languages are non-conflicting ([Wonham & Ramadge](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/modular-supervisory-control-of-discrete-event-systems/)), and hierarchical supervision needs hierarchical consistency (Zhong & Wonham 1990†) and the observer property (Wong & Wonham 1996†). Assume-guarantee contracts give compositional invariant sets for networked and continuous-time systems, with small-gain theorems as a special case ([Chen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10636); [Saoud, Girard & Fribourg](https://hal.archives-ouvertes.fr/hal-02196511v2)). These results cover particular system classes, and none says that arbitrary locally viable parts stay viable under arbitrary coupling; how far they scale is the open question. The first version of this report wrongly said no such theorems exist.

### Law and organisation theory supply the primitives and the impossibility theorems, without a calculus

Law is the one field that has run a recursive, self-modifying governance system on billions of people, and its structure recurs across traditions. Hohfeld's eight jural positions split into first-order constraints on action and second-order powers to rewrite those constraints ([Hohfeld](https://doi.org/10.2307/785533))†. Hart's secondary rules of recognition, change and adjudication form the reflective layer ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/legal-positivism/))†. Organisational law's essential contribution is asset partitioning, which contract alone cannot replicate ([Hansmann & Kraakman](https://doi.org/10.2307/797521))†, and the legal entity nests: more than 3.1 million entities held active identifiers in mid-2026, though only about 650,000 parent-relationship records exist and most entities file a reporting exception instead ([GLEIF](https://www.gleif.org/en/newsroom/blog/the-lei-in-numbers-q2-2026-signals-growing-regional-momentum-for-the-lei)). Ostrom's institutional grammar, now in a 2.0 version with nested and constitutive statements, is the closest social science comes to a formal rule syntax ([Frantz & Siddiki](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08937)), and her design principles were found well supported across 91 studies ([Cox et al.](https://www.ecologyandsociety.org/vol15/iss4/art38/)). Enterprise ontology contributes a universal transaction pattern of request, promise, state and accept ([Dietz](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-33149-2))†, and accounting contributes the strand's one conservation law: double-entry has an exact algebraic form in which every transaction is a zero-sum vector ([Ellerman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1898))†.

Economics supplies the constraints. With joint output and unobserved effort, no budget-balanced sharing rule yields efficient effort, so efficiency in that model needs a budget breaker ([Holmström](https://doi.org/10.2307/3003457))†. The result depends on its production and observability assumptions, full efficiency is attainable in some settings ([Legros & Matthews](https://ideas.repec.org/p/nwu/cmsems/991r.html)), and a budget breaker is an outside party and not a containing level, so this is a consideration for incentive design and not a proof that nesting is required. In Radner's aggregation model a suitably chosen tree attains the efficient trade-off between processors and delay, and logarithmic delay needs enough parallel processors ([Radner](https://doi.org/10.2307/2951495))†, while fidelity lost at each level compounds and bounds size ([Williamson](https://doi.org/10.1086/259258))†. Serial approval rejects more good projects and parallel approval accepts more bad ones, so architecture is an error-type trade-off ([Sah & Stiglitz](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1806069))†.

The empirical shape of planetary governance is a polycentric overlay of shallow hierarchies. China governs about 1.4 billion people through 34 provincial, about 333 prefecture, 2,843 county and 38,602 township divisions ([HK Education Bureau](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/pshe/national-geography/national-geography-resource-portal/Info_Sheet_01-administrative_division-eng.pdf)), which the researchers compute as spans of roughly 8 to 14 per level. The Chinese Communist Party reports 101.29 million members in 5.43 million primary organisations ([CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-06-30/CPC-membership-reaches-101-286-mln-1OoiX3xQAog/p.html)), about 19 per base unit. Self-management systems keep a containment hierarchy and replace personal authority with rule-based authority plus an explicit rule-change process ([Holacracy Constitution](https://www.holacracy.org/constitution/5-0/)), and the evidence includes reversals: Zappos was reported in 2020 to have quietly moved away from Holacracy ([Quartz](https://qz.com/work/1776841/zappos-has-quietly-backed-away-from-holacracy/)). Dunbar's 150 is not a design constant; a reanalysis found 95% confidence intervals of 4-520 and 2-336 ([Lindenfors et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8103230)). Minimality theorems are rare and local. The competitive price mechanism is the unique informationally decentralised mechanism that achieves efficient, individually rational allocations with a message space of minimal dimension ([Jordan](https://www.gwern.net/doc/economics/mechanism-design/1982-jordan.pdf)), a theorem about exchange economies under regularity conditions, building on Hurwicz and on Mount and Reiter†. It concerns the dimension of the message space and says nothing about incentives or convergence. No tradition proves a primitive set for organisations complete or minimal, and none supplies a composition operator showing that an entity built from entities obeys the same laws, apart from the accounting identity, which consolidates by addition; law and accounting otherwise do that through consolidation and veil rules in practice, not by theorem.

### The 2026 agent frontier has converged on engineering primitives with no composition semantics

The flagship attempt at a compositional language of world models, specifications and proof certificates narrowed during the period studied. ARIA's £59m Safeguarded AI programme halted selection for its machine-learning track in November 2025. Its director judged it "more likely than not we'd select zero successful teams" and said the capabilities the track would have built were now expected from frontier models by default ([ARIA update](https://ariaresearch.substack.com/p/november-update-a-safeguarded-ai)). The programme then dropped the planned cyber-physical applications call, changed programme director in April 2026, and now describes itself as a tooling track plus cybersecurity applications ([ARIA](https://aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces/trust-everything-everywhere/safeguarded-ai)). The categorical theory work continues and ships software. The application focus narrowed to domains where the world model is already formal, while the tooling track was broadened to include "auditable multi-agent systems", with no public deliverable yet.

Practice has converged on identifier, short-lived credential, capability advertisement, task, delegation token or mandate, policy, attestation and log. The IETF working-group draft on agent identity defines no new protocol and declares the translation of a mission into authorisation requirements, the policy model and identifier registries out of scope ([draft-ietf-wimse-aims](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-wimse-aims)). Payment mandates and OAuth token exchange specify scoped, budget-limited delegation from a user to an agent. Multi-hop attenuated delegation is specified in capability-token drafts such as UCAN and is expressible, for information only, in OAuth token exchange ([RFC 8693](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8693.html)); none of the deployed agent protocols mandates it across agent-to-agent hops, and none addresses liability. Measurement shows why that matters. Across 260 configurations, **independent agents amplified errors 17.2 times and centralised coordination contained this to 4.4 times** ([Kim et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08296v3)). The same study reports that every multi-agent variant, centralised ones included, degraded sequential reasoning tasks by 39-70%, so the benefit of hierarchy is task-dependent. A taxonomy built from more than 1,600 annotated traces locates failures in specification, inter-agent misalignment and verification rather than model capability ([Cemri et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13657v3)). In repeated Cournot markets a public, enforced state-transition-sanction manifest reduced LLM collusion from a mean tier of 3.1 to 1.8 where a prompt-only prohibition gave no reliable improvement ([Bracale Syrnikov et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.11369)). Information-flow guarantees are provable at a utility cost: one capability-based design solves 77% of benchmark tasks with provable security against 84% undefended ([Debenedetti et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18813)).

Open populations fail on identity first. The first open million-agent network claimed about 1.5 million agents that traced to roughly 17,000 human owners, with an exposed database key ([SecurityWeek](https://securityweek.com/security-analysis-of-moltbook-agent-network-bot-to-bot-prompt-injection-and-data-leaks/)), and on-chain governance shows voting-power Gini coefficients above 0.94 across examined protocols ([arXiv 2510.05830](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.05830)). The engineered digital systems that govern identity and payment for 10^9 people are public infrastructure with a unique centrally issued identity and a very narrow vocabulary: about 1.45 billion Aadhaar numbers ([UIDAI](https://uidai.gov.in/aadhaar-dashboard/enrollment-dashboard)) and 25.47 billion UPI transactions in October 2026 ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/upi-transactions-hit-2547-billion-in-october-value-soars-to-3153-lakh-crore)). They are existence proofs of scale under strong restriction, not of open-ended compositional governance. The scale figures in this report count different units (users, identifiers, transactions, nodes, access lists), so they show restricted functions running at large scale and do not establish a common capacity limit measured in actors.

## The theorems price what any foundation can promise

### Self-reference is free; self-certification is not

One diagonal scheme underlies Cantor, Russell, Gödel, Tarski and Turing: in a cartesian closed category, a point-surjective map from an object A to the function space Y^A forces every endomorphism of Y to have a fixed point ([Lawvere](http://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/15/tr15abs.html))†, and Yanofsky shows that many paradoxes and incompleteness results "fall out of the same simple scheme" ([Yanofsky](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0305282)). The notes caution that this is a common scheme and not one proof; Gödel's theorems also need arithmetisation and the derivability conditions.

The researchers separate "fractal" into three checkable claims. Closure under composition is cheap and consistent. Self-similarity is an isomorphism between a system type and a functor applied to it, solved canonically by an initial algebra for finite well-founded nesting (induction and terminating audits) or a final coalgebra for open-ended behaviour (bisimulation and safety; termination has to be proved separately); Leinster's general theory of self-similarity realises universal solutions as terminal coalgebras ([Leinster](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.4474)). Reflexivity, a system containing codes for itself or its own transformations, is consistent only under a restriction: partiality, stratified universes, or guarded recursion in which self-consultation consumes a time step ([Birkedal et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.3596))†. Finite syntax and coalgebraic behaviour coexist, and an ongoing service can serve terminating requests, but termination and productivity have to be proved separately for each. A foundation cannot have a type of all systems containing itself at the same level.

The asymmetry is the key result. Kleene's recursion theorem makes self-description always available. Self-verification is blocked: by Löb's theorem a theory asserts the correctness of its own proofs only for statements it already proves ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-provability/))†. Applied to agents, an agent acting on proofs in a theory cannot approve a successor reasoning in the same theory on the grounds that the successor's proofs are sound ([Yudkowsky & Herreshoff](https://intelligence.org/files/TilingAgentsDraft.pdf))†. Two decades of work yield partial results in idealised form, such as a computable but impractical logical inductor ([Garrabrant et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.03543))†, and the probabilistic cooperation lemma that would relax the obstacle exists only as forum posts whose authors flag unconfirmed assumptions ([Alignment Forum](https://alignmentforum.org/posts/ZWhJcHPmRaXAPAK5k/probabilistic-payor-lemma), search summary only). Practice has replaced proof with empirical validation ([Zhang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954))†. The governance consequence the researchers draw is that "self-governing" rigorously means each level is checked by something it cannot fully check itself, with the regress ending in an unverified trust anchor or a cycle of partial mutual checks. Hurwicz reaches the same point from economics: a mechanism is implemented only if its enforcement is itself an equilibrium of the larger game that includes the enforcers ([Hurwicz](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aecrev/v98y2008i3p577-85.html), confirmed from a search summary; the lecture PDF was not readable). Kelsen's basic norm and Hart's rule of recognition as social fact state it from law. A foundation must name its anchors. For organisations this is an argument from regress and not a theorem, and inside formal systems the gap can be narrow: a type checker for Coq's core calculus, without modules or template polymorphism, is verified in Coq assuming strong normalisation ([MetaCoq](https://github.com/Template-Coq/template-coq)), and theories too weak to meet the incompleteness hypotheses can verify their own consistency (Willard†).

### Verification and optimal control fail in general and return under structure

Every non-trivial extensional property of arbitrary programs is undecidable, and the rights-leak question in the general access-matrix model is undecidable ([Harrison, Ruzzo & Ullman](https://doi.org/10.1145/360303.360333))†. Verifying a temporal property for all sizes of a parameterised family of finite-state processes is undecidable ([Apt & Kozen](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(86)90071-2)); cut-offs restore decidability only for restricted topologies and anonymous processes (Bloem et al., "Decidability in Parameterized Verification", SIGACT News 2016†). The decisive variable for control is the information structure rather than the number of agents. Finite-horizon decentralised control with as few as two agents is NEXP-hard ([Bernstein et al.](https://people.cs.umass.edu/%7Eimmerman/pub/bgiz.pdf)), supervisor existence for modular plants is PSPACE-complete ([Rohloff & Lafortune](https://researchwith.njit.edu/en/publications/pspace-completeness-of-modular-supervisory-control-problems/); this is a cost of the modular description and applies to a single supervisor too), and distributed synthesis is undecidable exactly when the architecture contains an information fork, two processes with incomparable knowledge ([Finkbeiner & Schewe, LICS 2005](https://finkbeiner.groups.cispa.de/publications/2005-uniform-distributed-synthesis/)). These are worst-case results in the size of the problem description, some for as few as two agents. They do not say control gets harder as population grows, and mean-field methods make large populations of interchangeable actors tractable (Lasry & Lions; Huang, Malhamé & Caines)†. Runtime monitors that only halt enforce at most safety properties ([Schneider](https://doi.org/10.1145/353323.353382)); monitors that insert and suppress actions enforce more ([Ligatti et al.](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~dpw/papers/nonsafety05.pdf)); liveness is not enforceable by halting alone; inserting actions enforces some of it, and the rest needs deadlines or sanctions.

Tractability returns by giving something up: restricting the language, the topology to tree-like and nested-information shapes, or the property to safety invariants. The researchers note the resulting tension: hierarchy is the tractable shape, and hierarchy is also where control loss, hub fragility and capture concentrate.

### Syntactic composition does not make properties compose

| Composes under stated conditions | Does not compose in general |
|---|---|
| Safety properties of single traces; types | Liveness and progress |
| Capability confinement (authority follows references) | Information-flow security under feedback and refinement |
| Monotone computation (CALM); invariant-confluent transactions | Incentive compatibility; equilibrium efficiency |
| Differential-privacy budgets (additive) | Fairness of individually fair components |
| Passivity; small-gain conditions | Stability under feedback |
| Double-entry conservation across entities | Collective rationality under majority aggregation |
| Linearizability (per object) | Safety of individually "safe" AI models |

Both columns are conditional. The left needs its side conditions, and items on the right compose under stronger assumptions: liveness with fairness or ranking arguments, and cryptographic security within the universal composability framework ([Canetti](https://eprint.iacr.org/2000/067.pdf)). The right column is documented. Possibilistic security properties are preserved by product and cascade and not generally by feedback or refinement (McLean, IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy 1994†), because such policies are sets of sets of traces ([Clarkson & Schneider](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/bitstream/1813/11660/4/hyperproperties-tr.pdf)). Truthfulness is lost when mechanisms are combined, and the composable surrogate, smoothness, preserves only approximate efficiency ([Syrgkanis & Tardos](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.1325))†. **Combining two individually safe models produced vulnerable code 43% of the time where neither alone exceeded 3%** ([Jones, Dragan & Steinhardt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14595)), in an adversarially arranged combination on one task. The researchers found no general theorem that alignment fails to compose; the evidence is empirical and by analogy. Their rule is that whenever a non-compositional property has been made compositional, it was by substituting a stronger or differently shaped property with an explicit quantitative budget, and a composable surrogate for governance will be more conservative than its target. Spectral-gap undecidability ([Cubitt et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04573))† shows macro behaviour is not derivable from local rules in general, but it concerns infinite limits of constructed families, and the notes warn against citing it to claim finite organisations are unpredictable.

### Aggregation, delegation and agreement each force a declared sacrifice

Arrow, Gibbard-Satterthwaite, Sen and the List-Pettit discursive dilemma ([List & Pettit](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266267102001098))† show that small sets of reasonable axioms for aggregation are jointly unsatisfiable, and Myerson-Satterthwaite does the same for bilateral trade. Recursion makes these recur per level, so level-wise aggregation differs from global aggregation and a "fractal democracy" does not inherit the properties of its base rule. Each unit needs its own explicit, imperfect decision procedure and state, which is how corporate law treats boards. The bilateral-trade and strategy-proofness results are sharpest for small numbers. In a double auction with m traders per side, inefficiency falls as 1/m² ([Rustichini, Satterthwaite & Williams](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:ecm:emetrp:v:62:y:1994:i:5:p:1041-63)), and strategy-proofness is approximately recoverable in large markets ([Azevedo & Budish](https://www.nber.org/papers/w23771)), so for interchangeable actors scale helps with those two. Arrow, Sen and the discursive dilemma hold for any finite number of voters.

Agreement has hard costs. Deterministic consensus is impossible under asynchrony with one crash fault ([Fischer, Lynch & Paterson](https://doi.org/10.1145/3149.214121))†, Byzantine agreement without signatures needs more than three times as many parties as faults ([Lamport, Shostak & Pease](https://doi.org/10.1145/357172.357176))†, and deterministic Byzantine agreement needs a number of messages quadratic in the fault count ([Dolev & Reischuk](https://doi.org/10.1145/2455.214112))†, a bound that randomised protocols escape ([King & Saia](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.4561)). Common knowledge is unattainable where delivery is not guaranteed ([Halpern & Moses](https://doi.org/10.1145/79147.79161))†, so a rule change cannot become common knowledge on receipt; it can take effect at a pre-announced time, to within clock skew, and rules must be versioned. One entity cannot be prevented from presenting many identities without a logically central authority or a scarce resource ([Douceur](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-sybil-attack/))†. This is an argument in a specific model, and bounded Sybil resistance is achievable without a central authority under trust-graph assumptions ([Yu et al.](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~yuhf/yuh-sybillimit.pdf)). That bites hardest for AI agents, whose identities are cheap to mint.

### The arithmetic of 10^10 actors

The following figures are the researchers' own arithmetic on the cited bounds. Naming 10^10 actors takes 34 bits; they form about 5 × 10^19 pairs, so any per-pair cost is out of reach, and deterministic Byzantine agreement among all of them, with a constant fraction faulty, would cost on the order of 10^20 messages per decision, which is why agreement is run in small committees. A uniform tree needs 12 levels at span 7, 10 at span 10, 7 at span 30 and 5 at span 150. Depth and diffusion are not the binding constraints: gossip informs all nodes in a number of rounds logarithmic in population ([Karp et al.](https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.2000.892324))†, a few dozen rounds here. The binding constraints are the pairwise coherency term, distortion per level (with an illustrative 90% fidelity per hand-off, 39% survives nine hand-offs along a single chain; redundant paths and coding recover fidelity at a cost), antipodal latency of roughly 130-200 ms per round trip, and human attention. Human behavioural throughput is estimated at about 10 bits per second ([Zheng & Meister](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10234))†; the researchers compute that total human deliberate attention is roughly 1% of the bit rate of a stream in which each actor emits one 100-byte event per second. Exhaustive human oversight is impossible. Sampled audit detects violations of a fixed frequency with a sample size independent of population, but it cannot find a fixed number of violators: one bad actor in 10^10 needs on the order of 10^10 samples, so rare-event safety needs prevention at the boundary and not audit. Fast machine-speed loops must be governed locally under pre-delegated authority, and global governance sets only slowly varying constraints.

History corroborates the theorems. Universal content-bearing foundations, from Carnap's construction of all concepts to Cyc, upper ontologies and the full Semantic Web, stalled on unbounded encoding of tacit local knowledge, no convergence on one ontology, worst-case reasoning cost and inputs from parties with incentives to shade them. Shalizi's analysis of economy-wide planning concludes it is intractable for the foreseeable future on complexity grounds and because participants misreport ([Shalizi](https://bactra.org/weblog/918.html)). The durable successes standardise a thin interface and one invariant and stay agnostic about content: the Hourglass Theorem states the trade-off: a weaker spanning-layer specification admits more supports below and no more applications above, so the layer should be the weakest one that still supports the applications deemed necessary ([Beck](https://web.eecs.utk.edu/~mbeck/OnTheHourglassModel.pdf)). The theorem supports a thin waist only relative to a stated set of necessary applications, which this report has not specified. Double-entry bookkeeping, legal personhood, the relational model and the shipping container have that shape. None of them delivers liveness, fairness, optimality or good incentives.

## The prior art suggests nine recurring elements: a checklist, not a kernel

**Everything in this section is inference from the prior art by the researchers and by this report. No cited source states this list, proves it minimal or complete, or demonstrates it as a working system.** The evidence for it is recurrence across fields, which is weaker than independent convergence: the fields share ancestry (object capabilities descend from actors, enterprise transaction patterns and agent commitments from speech-act theory, Iris from linear logic, Holacracy from sociocracy and cybernetics), and the researchers were looking for common elements.

Both reviewers judged the list a useful architectural checklist and not a fundamental kernel, for four reasons. The elements are not independent: a boundary with a controller is a unit plus a control loop, a commitment is a stateful contract over a resource, and all nine can be encoded in an open transition system with a composition operator and a refinement logic. The list omits what the body of this report treats as decisive: identity, time, who observes and knows what, goals and preferences, and probability and learning. The cross-field identifications blur real differences, noted under the third and fifth elements below. And "smallest" has no meaning until a metric charges for encodings, proof rules and trusted assumptions.

The first element is the unit behind an interface: an actor, process, component, polynomial interface, role-holder or legal person, with private state and a declared boundary of interaction. The requirement on it is closure. A composite presents the same type as its parts, stated as an operad-algebra law or a closure-under-coupling proof. Substitutivity of equivalent parts is a separate requirement, met by congruence results under side conditions. Several formalisms provide this by construction, which settles closure and not whether governance properties survive it.

The second is the boundary with its own controller. The Erlang supervisor, the object-capability membrane, the Fractal component membrane, RINA's layer management, the cell in cloud architecture, the Holacracy circle and the legal entity are one design element seen from seven communities: every composite carries a control interface that mediates what crosses its boundary and manages what is inside, and that controller is an ordinary unit one level up. Milner's separation of place graph from link graph recurs as supervision tree against message graph and as address hierarchy against traffic graph, and the organisational evidence (people sit in state, employer, party and family at once) says containment must be a lattice or graph of entities, not one tree.

The third is authority as an unforgeable reference that attenuates under delegation. Pi-calculus restriction, actor locality and the object-capability rule are closely related. Hohfeldian power, "speaks for" and delegable commitments are neighbours and not the same thing: delegating a capability creates no obligation, and delegating a commitment grants no access. Attenuation is also not the whole story. Capability systems have rights amplification, where two references combined yield authority neither carries alone, and political authority aggregates upward, so a legislature holds powers no individual delegator held. Where authority originates is left to the ninth element. Attenuation-only delegation is monotone, so chains verify offline given trusted root credentials and a shared policy language; revocation is the non-monotone operation and needs an online check, short expiry or eventual propagation. Unrestricted creation and transfer of rights makes safety undecidable, so deployed systems restrict delegation to monotone, Datalog-like forms.

The fourth is the contract ordered by refinement, with parallel composition, conjunction and quotient. This is the composition discipline between units at every level, and its independent-implementability theorem is the same kind of result as the separation-logic frame rule: a local proof stays valid in any larger context that respects the declared footprint.

The fifth is a conserved resource ledger. Double-entry bookkeeping, resource-event-agent duality and linear logic treat budgets, tokens and delegated shares as resources that are neither copied nor discarded. The agreement is partial: Iris resource algebras deliberately include duplicable, persistent resources ([Jung et al.](https://cs.au.dk/~birke/papers/iris-journal.pdf)), so knowledge and shareable permissions need a different resource type from conserved tokens, and a balanced ledger is a bookkeeping identity that does not conserve value or capacity. Consolidation of sub-entity ledgers is addition with elimination of internal balances, which is why legal-entity recursion is tractable in practice. Holmström's result suggests, under its assumptions, naming who bears the residual at each level.

The sixth is the commitment with a lifecycle. The request-promise-state-accept transaction, the social commitment with delegate and assign operations, the joint commitment and the contract of economics are one object: a directed, public promise that can be discharged, cancelled, delegated or violated. Norms attach as typed data with a monitor semantics and a sanction clause, in the manner of the institutional grammar and Catala, and not as a primitive obligation modality. Promise theory's axiom that an agent can promise only its own behaviour matches how actors that cannot be commanded cooperate.

The seventh is monotone shared state. Only lattice-structured, mergeable state is shared without coordination; every non-monotone invariant (uniqueness, exclusion, non-negative balance, immediate revocation) is assigned to a single owning boundary, split into local quotas, or decided by a small consensus group whose outputs are then disseminated monotonically.

The eighth is the control loop by constraint. Higher levels restrict, by disabling actions and setting references and budgets, and lower levels choose within what remains. Maximal permissiveness in supervisory control, Beer's resource bargain, perceptual control theory's reference signals, subsidiarity in EU law and management by exception in knowledge hierarchies are the same prescription. Its necessary conditions are theorem-grade within their models (requisite variety and information rate, an adequate model, sensitivity trade-offs for applicable feedback systems), and extending them to organisations is a heuristic; its accompaniments, time-scale separation between levels and a fast alarm channel that bypasses intermediate levels, are convergent heuristics. A coarse-graining counts as a legitimate higher level only when it is dynamically closed, which the researchers identify as the shared content of Simon-Ando aggregation, the observer property in hierarchical supervision and computational closure.

The ninth is the stratified rule-change rule: recognition of what counts as a valid rule or status, a procedure for change, and adjudication of disputes, with a root of acceptance that is assumed rather than derived. Hart's secondary rules, Ostrom's constitutional level, the Holacracy governance process and the meta-object protocol are instances. The safe engineering form is bounded reflection: the meta-level is a separate, smaller system with a narrow declared protocol, the base keeps running without it, and untrusted producers, including AI agents, propose artefacts that a small trusted checker verifies.

Compressed, the list is close to what one researcher summarised as "legal person + double-entry + capabilities + types": a narrow waist with closure under composition and a short list of invariants that provably compose, and everything else left to plural layers above. The category-theory notes rank the mathematical packaging options: Poly alone is the most minimal host for interfaces, wiring, state and self-rewiring; the double-categorical module shape is the most general and the direction institutional funding follows; "symmetric monoidal category plus lens plus operad algebra" is their common denominator and not a third option. The logic notes give the complementary packaging: a dependent type theory as trusted meta-level, a resource layer, the contract algebra as composition discipline, modalities as libraries, and a designated decidable fragment for anything checked at population scale. A further packaging is proof-carrying admission: a small universal semantic substrate, compositional interfaces and a small checker, with evidence required only for designated invariants ([Necula](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~mernst/teaching/6.893/readings/necula-popl97.pdf)). Its formal development is proof-carrying data, in which each message carries a short proof that its whole history satisfies a compliance predicate ([Chiesa & Tromer](https://projects.csail.mit.edu/pcd)). It covers safety-shaped predicates only, rests on cryptographic setup assumptions, and cannot cover humans, whose conformance is established after the fact by audit and adjudication. The packagings are compatible, and none has been built for this purpose.

### What any candidate must satisfy

The limits translate into requirements of two kinds, and neither is a published standard. Some follow from the cited theorems. A candidate states closure as a theorem of its syntax and, separately for each property, the composition operators and side conditions under which that property is preserved. It says which inductive structures, operational coalgebras and behavioural equivalences it uses, and proves termination or liveness separately where it claims them. It stratifies or time-guards self-evaluation and names its trust anchors along with how they are contested and replaced. It states whether each scaling claim is proved for all sizes, proved up to a stated bound under a stated workload and fault model, or only measured. It keeps a monotone core and pays for coordination knowingly. It declares which social-choice axiom is relaxed at each aggregation point. It assumes an identity primitive and states per-committee fault thresholds and the consequence of capture. Others are design heuristics that this report infers: restrict what is verified, using correctness by construction, actor-supplied certificates, capability confinement and safety monitors; let self-modification preserve a fixed set of invariants or pass through an earlier or stronger checker, since unrestricted reflection can trivialise equational reasoning, as Wand showed for one calculus with fexprs ([Fexpr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fexpr))†; design the information structure so that optimal coordination across information forks is not needed; give each non-compositional property a conservative surrogate with a stated budget; absorb variety locally and move summaries upward; expect any control metric to be gamed; and keep shared interfaces as thin as the required applications allow.

The same theorems yield red flags, and their scope matters. The flags are claims of a sound, complete and automatic solution to an unrestricted problem: verifying arbitrary agents or contracts; synthesising optimal decentralised policies for any architecture; one universal type that contains everything including itself; a theory that satisfies the incompleteness hypotheses proving its own consistency; a mechanism that is at once efficient, incentive-compatible, individually rational and budget-balanced; a network that is permissionless and strictly Sybil-proof with no identity authority or scarce resource; and guarantees that are the same at every level because the structure is self-similar. Particular successes are not excluded: a specific safety invariant can be proved, an optimal policy can be computed for a particular finite instance, and restricted classes are decidable by construction. The mirror-image claim, that Gödel or undecidability shows nothing can be verified, is equally a red flag.

## Formal coverage is strong for specified components and thin for autonomous principals and constituted authority

The question names ten levels. The table below walks them once, drawing on the sections above and the researchers' notes. It is this report's analysis, it is not a test of any single candidate formalism, and no source cited here makes this comparison.

| Level | What it adds that a foundation must represent | Strongest existing formal treatment | Main gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resource | Scarcity; some things cannot be copied | Linear and separation logic; resource algebras; double-entry ledgers | Bookkeeping balance does not conserve value or capacity |
| Program | Behaviour and state | Transition systems, type theory, refinement; verified kernels and compilers | Proof cost; correctness of the specification |
| Service | Long-running interaction, concurrency, partial failure | Process calculi, actors with supervision, session types, contracts | Liveness under failure; protocols that are not agreed in advance |
| Human | Goals; limited attention; no complete predictive model | Game theory and mechanism design; strategic-ability and epistemic logics; conservative behavioural bounds; treated as environment in assume-guarantee reasoning | Model validity and enforcement coverage; no comprehensive behavioural guarantee |
| AI agent | Learned, stochastic policy; identity that is cheap to mint | Runtime monitors, capability confinement, probabilistic assume-guarantee | Conditional composition results exist for runtime-enforced safety (halting monitors by conjunction; action-substituting shields when non-conflicting) and for directly verified learned controllers with compatible mode contracts ([Ivanov et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3477023)); none for general safety or alignment of open-ended, self-modifying agent organisations; Sybil-resistant identity |
| Team | Joint commitments and coordination under partial information | Commitment protocols, organisational multi-agent models, alternating-time and coalition logics, decentralised decision models, supervision | Worst-case hardness of decentralised control; small empirical base |
| Organisation | Legal personhood, roles, asset partitioning, internal rule change | Enterprise ontology, electronic institutions, law-governed conformance hierarchies | No theorem, beyond the accounting identity, that an entity built from entities obeys the same laws |
| Enterprise | Nested entities and delegation across them | Accounting consolidation; multi-level norm compliance checking | Propagation of liability |
| Government | Authority constituted by recognition and aggregated from the governed; adjudication | Secondary rules, constitutive ("counts as") rules, social choice theory | Aggregation impossibilities; legitimacy is not a formal property |
| Nation | Sovereignty: a root of authority in some domains, constrained by constitutional and supranational law in others | Basic norm or rule of recognition; enforcement as an equilibrium among enforcers | Recognition and enforcement anchors are domain-dependent and assumed, not derived |

Within a chosen formalism the interface and containment structure is the same at every level, which is what closure provides. Two distinctions cut across the levels. The first is between components whose behaviour is specified and principals whose behaviour is not (humans, learned agents, third-party services). For the second group, conditional guarantees remain possible for actions mediated at an enforceable interface and for interactions covered by conservative behavioural bounds; everything outside those needs monitoring, incentives and accountability after the fact. The second is between authority that is delegated and attenuated and authority that is constituted by recognition. Constitution occurs wherever an institution is founded, governments and states can be subject to constitutional and supranational constraints while being roots of authority in other domains, and the ten levels are an overlay and not a single tree. The normative multi-agent literature represents both distinctions (constitutive rules and institutional power; regimentation versus sanction), at the scale of research prototypes and without composition theorems across levels. This survey found no construction that integrates them with attenuating delegation, resources and cross-level liability.

## Closure is available, guarantees are conditional, and integration is the open problem

| Requirement in the question | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Same structure at every level | Closure holds by construction, under side conditions; faithful cross-scale governance is unproven | Operad algebras; DEVS closure; congruence results |
| Composition of control (supervisors, invariant sets) | Conditional theorems for restricted system classes | Modular and hierarchical supervisory control; invariance contracts |
| Mathematically rigorous composition of behaviour | Theorem per doctrine, sometimes only lax | Structured cospans, categorical systems theory; a restricted stochastic case since 2026 |
| Local reasoning that survives context | Proven theorem | Frame rule; independent implementability; capability safety |
| Coordination-free consistency | Theorem within stated models | CALM; invariant confluence |
| Model-dependent controller limits | Proven within stated plant, channel and feedback assumptions | Requisite-variety bounds; data-rate theorems; Bode sensitivity integrals |
| Cost of decentralising control | Proven worst-case hardness | NEXP for decentralised control; undecidability with information forks |
| Cost of modular description | Proven worst-case hardness, for a central supervisor too | PSPACE for supervisor existence over modular plants |
| Recursive viable structure (five functions, algedonics) | Heuristic | Viable System Model; case studies and one proponent survey |
| Time-scale separation, near-decomposability beyond linear systems | Heuristic with a linear theorem | Simon-Ando; architecture principles |
| Self-reflection | Self-description proven; self-certification impossible at full strength | Kleene; Gödel II, Löb, Tarski |
| Successor and self-modification trust | Open problem | Tiling agents; practice uses empirical validation |
| Minimality or completeness of a primitive set | Proven only locally; open for organisations | Price-mechanism message space (Mount & Reiter; Jordan 1982); sequential abstract state machines (Gurevich 2000); glue expressiveness in BIP ([Bliudze & Sifakis](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/record/170493)) |
| Control at 10^10 actors | Not found by this survey for the full combination; not an established capacity limit | Verified components run at about 10^9 decisions per second in single-operator authorization; nothing formally founded spans heterogeneous actors across levels |
| The nine-element list | Inference; a checklist, neither minimal nor complete | This report |

What remains unsolved is specific. First, the glue: no published formalism integrates interface, boundary, authority, resource, commitment and monotone state with a proved congruence or compositionality result. Partial integrations exist, including the 2006 logic of resources, authority and knowledge, capability-based financial instruments ([Miller, Morningstar & Frantz](https://papers.agoric.com/papers/capability-based-financial-instruments/abstract)) and law-governed interaction; none spans human, organisational and AI principals. Bigraphs have structure and congruence without authority or data consistency; psi-calculi have parametric data without nesting; object-capability actor systems have authority with limited metatheory; categorical systems theory composes behaviour and has nothing yet for authority, obligation, budget or liability. Second, a calculus of delegation: proof-carrying authorization formalises delegation of authority, and formal definitions of accountability exist for security protocols ([Künnemann, Esiyok & Backes](https://publications.cispa.de/articles/conference_contribution/Automated_Verification_of_Accountability_in_Security_Protocols/24612942/1)), but no formal semantics covers how accountability and liability propagate along chains that cross human, organisational and AI principals, and the agent protocols explicitly leave it out. Third, norms exist in three untranslated forms (natural-language constitutions inside models, analysable policy code at the tool boundary, and institutional state machines and grammars) with no refinement or soundness relation spanning all three. Sound and complete compliance checking between abstract and concrete norms exists for formal institutional models; the missing bridges are to natural-language constitutions and to deployed policy code. Fourth, dynamic structure: formal accounts of changing interfaces exist, but none proves safety or accountability for self-rewiring organisations, and the one attempt on hierarchical delegation was withdrawn. Fifth, quantitative emergence: no general bound on how far a lax semantics departs from strict composition, and compositional viability results cover only restricted system classes. Sixth, probabilistic assume-guarantee reasoning exists ([Kwiatkowska et al.](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/39607/1/tacas10.pdf)) but is less developed than the Boolean theory, which matters because LLM agents are stochastic components. Seventh, parametric verification does not cover heterogeneous, hierarchical, self-modifying populations with identities, which is the actual target. Eighth, composable approximate-efficiency guarantees exist for restricted mechanism classes (smooth mechanisms), but there is no general compositional guarantee of truthfulness or alignment for heterogeneous, learning, self-modifying organisations. Ninth, Sybil-resistant identity for AI agents without a single global authority is unsolved, and the first open agent networks failed on exactly this. Tenth, the empirical base stops early: controlled evidence on LLM-agent organisations ends at a few dozen cooperating agents and two or three levels, simulations at 10^3 to 10^6 agents are unvalidated as predictive models, and the formally verified systems at large scale cover one property, authorization decisions, under one operator; this survey found no formally founded system governing anything near 10^10 heterogeneous actors across organisational levels. Finally, the comparison this report set out to make is incomplete: the walk through the ten levels above is this report's own analysis, and no single candidate formalism has been tested against each level on a common worked example.

Several findings rest on weaker evidence than their prominence suggests, and readers should weight them accordingly. Most classical theorems and their citations across all seven notes were stated from memory, with DOIs not resolved this session. The reported change of plan at ARIA was hearsay in one note and confirmed from ARIA's own pages in another; this report relies on the confirmed account. Vendor and analyst figures on agent populations are definitionally unstable and came from secondary sources, so they are omitted here. The absence of prior work on a single calculus spanning resource through nation is an absence after limited searches in each strand, not an exhaustive review.

## Conclusion

Smaller rigorous substrates exist, and each is closed under its own composition. Partial integrations exist, but the substrates have not been composed into the complete foundation the question asks for. Guarantees over them are conditional and have to be established property by property. The price of guarantees over an unrestricted language is proof effort, conservatism, runtime enforcement, a guarantee that holds only with stated probability or under computational assumptions, or accountability after the fact, which deters and does not guarantee; the price is not loss of universality. The existence question stays open, and two lists bound it. Excluded, under the hypotheses of the cited theorems, none of which the question strictly requires: unconditional self-certification by a sufficiently strong theory; a sound, complete, terminating verifier for arbitrary programs or for the general parameterised problem; and aggregation or bilateral trade that satisfies all the classical axioms at once. Strict Sybil-proofness in a permissionless population without an identity authority or scarce resource is excluded by an argument in a specific model. Run at large scale, each for a restricted function and measured in different units: delegated hierarchical naming and federated routing (about 6 billion users, about 78,000 autonomous systems); centrally evaluated policy-based authorization (access control lists for billions of users in Zanzibar; about a billion decisions per second at AWS, with an engine proved against a formal specification); unique centrally issued identity (about 1.45 billion identifiers); and interbank payment routing and settlement through one central switch under one rulebook, with accounts held by the participating banks (about 25 billion transactions a month). Some of these are recursive for one function: naming authority and Internet number-resource authority are delegated by the same rule at every depth ([RFC 1034](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1034.html); [RFC 6487](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6487.txt)), supervision trees nest, and legal entities nest. Attenuating delegation is deployed in narrow vocabularies, in number-resource certificates and in caveated bearer tokens ([Birgisson et al.](https://research.google/pubs/macaroons-cookies-with-contextual-caveats-for-decentralized-authorization-in-the-cloud/)); object-capability systems with general authority have kernel-scale evidence, and the open multi-hop agent standards are drafts. None of these carries authority, obligation and liability together through the levels the question names. That is the open part: doing so for heterogeneous and learning actors, with a stated composition law. This report neither constructs that nor shows it impossible.

What the evidence supports for a design is a mixture: capability confinement and runtime monitors for safety invariants; restricted languages wherever decisions must be made at population scale; certificates where a producer can supply them; and accountability after the fact for everything else. Of the invariants a foundation might enforce, bounded authority (absent revocation), contract refinement and history compliance can be checked locally, while uniqueness of identity, absence of forks, revocation and non-negative shared balances need an authority, an owner or agreement. Trust anchors (identity issuance, root rules, root enforcers) have to be named and made contestable, because no one has shown how to derive them from inside. Coordination at 10^9 has taken at least four shapes, and a design will mix them. Measured behaviour of AI-agent teams is task-dependent, so organisation theory is a source of hypotheses and not of settled design rules. The most useful next step is a common worked example: take a transition-system baseline, an institutional compliance model and a recursive enforcement scheme, build one case with explicit authority, resource, norm, observation and rule-change semantics, and compare polynomial functors, double-categorical systems theory, bigraphs, proof assistants with a resource layer, reflective rewriting logic and Mesarović's coordination theory against it on encoding size, trusted assumptions and preservation proofs.
