## B. Design objections and evidence limits

1. **Timer arithmetic is still causally essential.** In `attacks`, the round-9 case returns loss 2, prohibition loss 1, u0 share 1. Replacing `timers` with the actual timetable returns loss 2, prohibition loss 2, u0 share 0. This does not establish that the arrival trigger is outside InstAL: it is now an actual institutional rule. It establishes that the numerical counterfactual schedule remains an external premise, exactly as the new text acknowledges. Removing the trigger rules fails the maintained test. Arithmetic alone can describe this restricted family; that is not a refutation of an implementation which actually delegates refusal decisions to InstAL.
2. **Evidence checking is not full event-history reproduction.** `PRE_READY` accepts u1's refusal at 1 when its readiness is 2 and its receipt is 3. InstAL also refuses it because its duty permission is not yet issued. The reader ignores it when inferring work. That is not an invalid norm trace and does not falsely excuse the unit. A refusal with no cause after readiness (`NO_REASON`) and disconnected readiness (`BAD_READY`) are rejected with `ValueError`. Repeated attempts while forbidden (`RETRY`, refusals 1 and 2, receipt 3 under [0,3)) are accepted, correctly; their timings are not all replayed. The document promises matching actual refusals and receipt, not reproduction of every retry on every coalition.
3. **Malformed quantitative inputs need better diagnostics.** A negative budget reaches an assertion about a request at -1 instead of the older adapter's clean rejection. A zero-length immediate standing ban sorts its lapse before its arrival and rejects the proposed later receipt; a delayed zero-length ban yields an empty interval and succeeds. The older K8 interface explicitly rejects empty intervals and negative budgets. I do not count this boundary inconsistency as an in-scope wrong outcome without silently extending that domain. The new interface should state and validate the same domain, or deliberately support empty bans with arrival-before-lapse ordering.
4. **Cache trust is unchanged.** Ordinary calls changing a standing length 2 → 4 → 2 return receipts 2 → 4 → 2 and require 2 → 2 → 0 additional solver runs. Keys include standing and fixed schedules as well as the ordered entries. Cached traces are mutable and source/compiler changes are not in the key; mutation tests clear the cache. No stale trace under an ordinary unmodified call was found.
5. **Stop condition 3 is conditional, not a general impossibility theorem.** Deadline bounds, standing lengths and ledger caps are outside this particular institution. That supports the implementation inventory. It does not prove that every conceivable norm containing a number must be outside every InstAL encoding. The vendored grammar recognizes numerical terms and comparisons, but my attempted constant-threshold rule was rejected by its type checker (`2 in conditional but not bound`), so that attempt is not a counterexample and is not list A. A second experimental institution with an Amount type and bound comparison variables (`numeric_bound`) also failed: the solver reported zero answer sets with numerical domain literals. This was an altered institution/domain outside the supplied adapter scenarios, not a zero-trace history of the contract model; it is not promoted to stop condition 1. The report does not claim arithmetic or a clock was successfully added to InstAL.
6. **Coverage remains finite.** Repeated arrival after a completed duty is not covered by the maintained standing examples. A mutation adding `not done(O)` to the immediate trigger passes the entire maintained contingent test. Phase `survivor` supplies a continuing explicit permission, completes the duty, then logs a second arrival: the original emits `occurred(refuse(h_u0),u0)` on the final request, the mutant `occurred(emit(h_u0),u0)`. This tests the omission without counting an arbitrary repeated-input policy as supported K8 behavior. An undetected mutant is a coverage objection, not an extra wrong outcome in the unmodified model. The previously admitted route, adapter, two-way exemption and deadline-scheduling gaps remain.
