# A narrow waist beats a universal foundation

Yes, a smaller and more rigorous foundation exists, but only in a restricted sense, and the restriction is fixed by theorems rather than by missing effort. Seven independent bodies of prior art (applied category theory, logic and formal methods, concurrency and distributed systems, cybernetics and control, organisation and institutional theory, the mathematics of self-reference, and the 2022-2026 AI-agent governance frontier) converge on the same small set of primitives: an autonomous unit behind an interface, a nested boundary with its own controller, authority carried as unforgeable and attenuating references, contracts ordered by refinement, a conserved resource ledger, commitments with a lifecycle, monotone shared state, and a stratified rule-change rule that ends in a named trust anchor. The fractal requirement is the one that is fully met: "a system of systems is a system" is a proven law in operad algebras, in DEVS closure under coupling, and in process-calculus congruence. What cannot be had is the stronger thing the question hopes for, a foundation from which everything can be constructed and also governed with guarantees: Gödel and Löb block self-certification, Rice and Apt-Kozen block general verification of arbitrary or unboundedly many actors, decentralised optimal control is NEXP-complete or undecidable, and Arrow, Holmström and Myerson-Satterthwaite block ideal aggregation and delegation. A foundation is therefore universal in what it can express or strong in what it can guarantee, and the theorems set the exchange rate between the two. Control at 10^10 actors is achievable only for safety-type, monotone, locally enforceable invariants inside a recursive, nearly decomposable structure; liveness, incentives, fairness and alignment need budgets, statistics and re-verification. No published formalism integrates the convergent primitives, none has been exercised above a few hundred hand-wired components or a few dozen cooperating AI agents, and the kernel described in this report is an inference from the prior art, not an established result.

*Provenance convention.* The researchers' notes distinguish sources checked during this research session (November 2026, mostly at abstract or search-excerpt level) from sources cited from the researchers' memory of the primary literature. This report preserves that distinction: a citation followed by **†** was cited from memory and its bibliographic details, exact figures and wording were not re-fetched, so it should be spot-checked before being quoted. Unmarked citations were checked this session. Arithmetic for the 10^10 scale and all cross-field mappings are the researchers' own inferences and are labelled as such.

## Seven strands each solve one piece, and none solves the whole

The comparison below comes before any synthesis, because the central finding is negative: every strand has a theorem-grade core, each core covers a different part of the problem, and no strand's own experts claim sufficiency.

| Candidate foundation | Primitives | What it proves about composition | What it does not express | Largest evidence of use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poly, operads, double-categorical systems theory | Interfaces, dependent lenses, wiring, modules of systems | Nesting is an exact operad law; behaviour composes strictly or laxly per doctrine | Goals, norms, authority, identity; no theorem for self-rewiring systems | Hundreds of hand-wired components (epidemiology, ODE models) |
| Contract algebra (assume-guarantee, hypercontracts) | Contract = (environments, implementations); refinement, composition, conjunction, quotient | Independent implementability; quotient is right adjoint to composition | Authority, obligation with violation, knowledge | Automotive and aerospace design flows |
| Dependent type theory with a resource layer (Lean, Rocq, Iris) | Types, proofs, resource algebras, invariants | Small trusted checker; frame rule for local reasoning | Norms and time only by encoding; proof cost is high | seL4, CompCert, Cedar's verified model |
| Process calculi, bigraphs, actors with supervision | Units, asynchronous messages, names, nested places | Bisimilarity is a congruence; supervision recursion in production | Authority (except via names), obligation, data consistency | 10^6 connections per node, 10^8-10^9 users per service |
| Object capabilities and authorization logics | Unforgeable references; says/speaks-for; relation tuples | Capability safety implies authority safety; Datalog-decidable policy | Obligation, liveness, accountability | Verified kernels; planetary-scale authorization |
| Lattices, CRDTs, monotone logic | Join-semilattice state, merge | CALM; invariant confluence | Structure, authority, non-monotone decisions | Dynamo-class stores |
| Control theory and cybernetics | Control loop, constraint set, supervisor, coarse-graining | Requisite variety, data-rate, supervisory controllability, Simon-Ando aggregation | No compositional viability theorem; VSM recursion is an axiom | Engineering control; VSM by case study |
| Law, institutional economics, enterprise ontology | Role, Hohfeldian position, commitment, entity, secondary rules | Double-entry conservation; impossibility theorems as constraints | No composition calculus, no minimality proof | 8.3 billion people, roughly 200 states |

### Category theory proves the fractal law and admits it lacks agency, norms and scale

Applied category theory supplies the cleanest statement of recursion. An operad of wiring diagrams gives the syntax of boxes nested in boxes ([Spivak 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0297))†, and open dynamical systems form an algebra over it, so wiring open systems yields an open system coherently at every depth ([Vagner, Spivak & Lerman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1598))†. The most compact single candidate is the category of polynomial functors: one category whose objects are interfaces and whose morphisms are bidirectional interaction patterns, with book-length treatment of its monoidal structures, comonoids and bicomodules ([Niu & Spivak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00990)). It reaches self-rewiring structure through the double category Org of "dynamic organizations", whose worked examples are prediction markets and deep learning ([Shapiro & Spivak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03906)), and since February 2026 through interfaces that themselves change, in a single-author preprint ([Spivak 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17917)). The most general candidate is the double operadic theory of systems, which defines a systems theory as a module of systems over a double category of interfaces and interactions and recovers Petri nets over undirected wiring and Moore machines over lenses as doctrines ([Libkind & Myers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329)).

The field's own record marks the limits. **The paper claiming task delegation for hierarchical agents on Org was withdrawn** after its authors found the relevant Kleisli category was pre-monoidal rather than monoidal ([arXiv 2410.08373](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08373)). The double operadic paper is titled "Towards", and its authors state that deriving a composite's behaviour from its parts "isn't always possible" ([Libkind & Myers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329)). Whether stochastic and non-deterministic systems theories admit well-behaved behaviour functors was open until March 2026 ([Lynch, Myers, Rischel & Staton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29573)). The only strand that models agency, categorical cybernetics, composes the definition of equilibrium and of Bayesian update through parametrised optics ([Ghani et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04641); [Capucci et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06332)), yet its originator's own slides say "we can't compute equilibria" and "realising the promised benefits of ACT is still hard" ([Hedges 2020](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/mathematical/ACTUCR/Hedges_Open_Games.pdf)). The proposal for agents managing agents and meta-agents altering their own structure exists only as an extended abstract ([Smithe 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07577)).

The strand's most useful export for governance is a precise, modest definition of emergence. A generative effect is the failure of an observation map to preserve composition ([Fong & Spivak](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05316))†; the reachability semantics of open Petri nets is the standard example of a semantics that is lax rather than strict ([Baez & Master](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05415), publication data checked, the laxness statement cited from memory). The researchers infer the practical rule: treat each property of interest as a semantics functor and ask whether it is strict (verifiable locally, scales to any size) or lax (needs global analysis or a bound on the gap). No framework removes emergence; each locates it. Sheaf theory supplies the complementary audit tool, the formal meaning of "locally consistent, globally inconsistent": under sheaf-Laplacian diffusion the only stable opinion distributions are global sections of the discourse sheaf ([Hansen & Ghrist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12798)).

On adoption, the documented successes are compositional epidemiological modelling and scientific model management ([Baez et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08373)), and the largest institutional bet is the UK ARIA Safeguarded AI programme, which funds double categorical systems theory under David Jaz Myers ([ARIA](https://www.aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces/mathematics-for-safe-ai/safeguarded-ai/meet-the-creators)). The researchers found no category-theoretic framework governing a large organisation or multi-agent deployment, no test above hundreds of components, and no treatment of permissions, obligations, delegation or actor identity; they flag that last absence as the result of a limited search rather than proof.

### Logic supplies the smallest algebra of contracts and the only small trusted checker

Formal methods contribute two kernels at opposite ends. The most minimal is the algebra of contracts. In the meta-theory of Benveniste and colleagues a contract is a pair of sets, admissible environments and admissible implementations, and assume-guarantee contracts, interface automata, modal interfaces and rely-guarantee reasoning are all instances ([Benveniste et al.](https://nowpublishers.com/article/Details/EDA-053)). Hypercontracts extend the algebra to hyperproperties and show the quotient, the most permissive missing component, is the right adjoint of composition ([Incer et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02449)). The recurring signature is a refinement preorder, a monotone parallel product, a meet and a residual. The researchers observe that this is the same residuated shape as linear and separation logic, and that it is recursive by construction: a composed contract is a contract. Its limit is that it is a shell. Circular assume-guarantee reasoning is sound for safety assumptions and not for liveness in general ([Abadi & Lamport](https://doi.org/10.1145/203095.201069))†, so mutual promises compose safely as prohibitions and not as obligations to eventually act.

The most general kernel is dependent type theory used as a meta-language with a small proof checker. It has carried the largest verified artefacts, and the cost is documented: **about 200,000 lines of Isabelle proof for roughly 7,500 lines of C in seL4, about 25 person-years in total and about 11 for the initial functional-correctness proof** ([Trustworthy Systems](https://trustworthy.systems/projects/OLD/l4.verified/numbers)). A study of three verified distributed systems found 16 bugs, none in verified code and all in specifications, unverified shims and tools ([Fonseca et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3064176.3064183))†. Verification relocates risk to the specification and to the boundary with the unverified world, which for organisations, where the specification is contested social intent, is the dominant problem. The architecture that matters for governing untrusted AI agents is the split between arbitrary proof search and a tiny checker: Cedar's authorization semantics is modelled in Lean and tied to production Rust code by differential testing, a process that found 4 soundness bugs by proof and 21 by testing ([Disselkoen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01688v1)).

For resources and interaction, separation logic's frame rule gives local reasoning that survives any larger context ([Reynolds](https://doi.org/10.1109/LICS.2002.1029817))†, and Iris generalises ownership to user-defined resource algebras with invariants inside a proof assistant ([Jung et al.](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-functional-programming/article/iris-from-the-ground-up-a-modular-foundation-for-higherorder-concurrent-separation-logic/26301B518CE2C52796BFA12B8BAB5B5F)). Multiparty session types give correctness by construction for closed, pre-agreed protocols, with a soundness history that needed correction in 2019 and repair in 2024-2026 ([Scalas & Yoshida](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290343); [Yoshida & Hou](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16741v3)).

Normative logic teaches a negative lesson. Standard deontic logic cannot represent contrary-to-duty obligations consistently ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-deontic/))†; the working treatments make norms non-truth-bearing conditional pairs ([Makinson & van der Torre](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/DagSemProc.07122.32)) or prioritised defaults mirroring statutory drafting, as in Catala, which encoded a section of the US tax code and French family benefits and exposed a bug in the official French implementation ([Merigoux et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03198v2)). The researchers' reading is that a norm is typed data with a monitor semantics, not a modal formula. Authority is the one normative area with proven planetary scale, and it reached that scale by being decidable, monotone or stratified, default-deny and locally evaluable; the "says" and "speaks for" calculus makes delegation chains proofs ([Garg & Abadi](https://rd.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-540-78499-9_16.pdf)). Experts expect no single universal object logic: the Stanford Encyclopedia entry on combining logics treats "why are there so many logics instead of just one?" as a central question of the field ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2014/entries/logic-combining)), and institution theory answers structurally by making the logic itself a composable parameter under the slogan that truth is invariant under change of notation ([Goguen & Burstall](https://doi.org/10.1145/147508.147524)). The researchers found no implemented kernel integrating dependent types, a separation-style resource layer and modalities for time, knowledge, obligation and authority.

### Concurrency theory and planetary systems agree on federation, thin interfaces and local coordination

Process calculi share a tiny kernel of parallel composition, name scoping and one interaction rule, and their payoff is a behavioural equivalence that is a congruence, so an equivalent part can replace a part in any context without re-verifying the whole ([Milner](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-10235-3))†. Bigraphs are the smallest framework that makes nesting and linking independent first-class structures, a place graph and a link graph over the same nodes, and recover CCS, the pi-calculus, ambients and Petri nets as instances ([Milner 2009](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511626661))†. The choice of primitives is not neutral: no reasonable encoding takes synchronous mixed choice into the asynchronous pi-calculus, because mixed choice solves leader election in symmetric networks ([Palamidessi](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0960129503004043))†. The researchers conclude that a kernel for very large systems should be asynchronous with receiver-local choice.

The actor model has the strongest production record, and Erlang/OTP adds the one deployed mechanism that is literally recursive governance: the supervision tree, in which each supervisor restarts its children and escalates to its own supervisor when restart intensity is exceeded ([Erlang/OTP](https://www.erlang.org/doc/system/sup_princ.html))†. A 2014 account of WhatsApp reports roughly 465 million monthly users on about 11,000 cores with more than 70 million Erlang messages per second ([High Scalability](https://highscalability.com/how-whatsapp-grew-to-nearly-500-million-users-11000-cores-an/), search excerpt). Authority composes through object capabilities, where a reference is the permission and is acquired only by creation, endowment or introduction ([Miller](http://www.erights.org/talks/thesis/markm-thesis.pdf))†; this is the same rule as the actor locality laws ([Hewitt & Baker](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/41962))†. Open capability standards remain drafts: the UCAN specification lists a 1.0 release candidate with no final version ([ucan-wg](https://github.com/ucan-wg/spec)), and OCapN's documents are not frozen ([OCapN](https://github.com/ocapn/ocapn)).

Distributed computing draws the coordination boundary exactly. A program has a consistent coordination-free implementation if and only if it is monotonic ([Hellerstein & Alvaro](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01930)), and a set of transactions preserves an invariant without coordination if and only if it is invariant-confluent; **10 of 12 TPC-C invariants pass that test** ([Bailis et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2237)). The notes caution that CALM's formal proof lives in the relational-transducer model and should not be quoted as model-independent. Every system that reaches billions of endpoints is a federation of autonomous domains joined by a deliberately thin protocol, with delegated hierarchical naming and no global controller or global consensus: the IPv4 routing table held **1,044,785 entries announced by 78,339 autonomous systems** in April 2026 ([APNIC report](https://seclists.org/nanog/2026/Apr/44)) while serving about 6 billion Internet users ([ITU](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2025-11-17-Facts-and-Figures.aspx)). The largest publicly documented centrally reconciled control domain is a 130,000-node Kubernetes cluster run experimentally ([Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/containers-kubernetes/how-we-built-a-130000-node-gke-cluster/)), five orders of magnitude short of 10^10. The clearest worked example of local rules with a proven global guarantee is interdomain routing: with arbitrary policies stability is NP-complete to decide and can fail ([Griffin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1109/90.993304))†, while under local customer-provider rules and an acyclic hierarchy convergence is guaranteed without global coordination ([Gao & Rexford](https://doi.org/10.1109/90.974523))†.

### Cybernetics has hard limits on any controller and only a heuristic for recursion

The law of requisite variety is an elementary inequality: outcome entropy cannot fall below disturbance entropy minus regulator capacity ([Ashby](http://pespmc1.vub.ac.be/books/IntroCyb.pdf))†. The good regulator theorem is far weaker than its title, and a 2021 critique shows its notion of "model" is nearly vacuous ([Wentworth](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/Dx9LoqsEh3gHNJMDk/fixing-the-good-regulator-theorem)); the versions strong enough to bear weight are recent, proving that agents robust across a large family of distribution shifts have learned an approximate causal model ([Richens & Everitt](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/44a2b9f7bf9aec3f1fa333ad875b0ee0-Abstract-Conference.html)) and that general multi-step agents contain an extractable world model ([Richens et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01622)). Control theory adds a minimum feedback rate for stabilisation, the sum of the logarithms of the unstable eigenvalue magnitudes ([Nair et al.](https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/scholarlywork/294877-feedback%20control%20under%20data%20rate%20constraints-%20an%20overview)), and a conservation law on sensitivity ([Stein](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCS.2003.1213600))†.

Stafford Beer's Viable System Model is the most direct prior art for the same structure at every level, and it is a structured heuristic. Its "Recursive System Theorem" is an axiom, and its claim of necessity and sufficiency has no formal definition of viability to be proved against ([Beer](https://doi.org/10.1057/jors.1984.2))†. Its main quantitative support is one survey by a proponent ([Schwaninger & Scheef](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/249134)), with no independent replication found. The free energy principle, the most ambitious scale-free account of agents within agents, slides from a statistical tool to a claimed physical boundary without justification, according to a Behavioral and Brain Sciences target article ([Bruineberg et al.](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/emperors-new-markov-blankets/715C589A73DDF861DCF8997271DE0B8C)), and an analysis of linear systems finds its conditions hold only in a narrow part of parameter space ([Aguilera et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.11203))†. The multiscale extension of Ashby's law is proved only for block-independent systems and does not extend to the complexity profile in general ([Siegenfeld & Bar-Yam](https://mdpi.com/1099-4300/27/8/835)).

The rigorous pieces that do exist fit together. Closure under coupling is proved for DEVS and shown to fail for some natural subclasses ([Zeigler](https://repository.arizona.edu/handle/10150/634636)). Supervisory control theory gives an algebra of permission in which a supervisor only disables controllable events and a maximally permissive supervisor exists as a fixpoint ([Ramadge & Wonham](https://doi.org/10.1137/0325013))†. Near-decomposability has a theorem behind it for linear systems, justifying coarse-graining and time-scale separation ([Simon & Ando](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1909285))†. Viability theory defines "under control" as remaining in the viability kernel of a constraint set ([Aubin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-16684-6))†. The researchers found no theorem that viability kernels or supervisors of parts compose into a viable or controllable whole, and they identify that as the main open theoretical question of the strand.

### Law and organisation theory supply the primitives and the impossibility theorems, without a calculus

Law is the one field that has run a recursive, self-modifying governance system on billions of people, and its structure recurs across traditions. Hohfeld's eight jural positions split into first-order constraints on action and second-order powers to rewrite those constraints ([Hohfeld](https://doi.org/10.2307/785533))†. Hart's secondary rules of recognition, change and adjudication form the reflective layer ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/legal-positivism/))†. Organisational law's essential contribution is asset partitioning, which contract alone cannot replicate ([Hansmann & Kraakman](https://doi.org/10.2307/797521))†, and the legal entity nests: more than 3.1 million entities held active identifiers recording parent relationships in mid-2026 ([GLEIF](https://www.gleif.org/en/newsroom/blog/the-lei-in-numbers-q2-2026-signals-growing-regional-momentum-for-the-lei)). Ostrom's institutional grammar, now in a 2.0 version with nested and constitutive statements, is the closest social science comes to a formal rule syntax ([Frantz & Siddiki](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08937)), and her design principles were found well supported across 91 studies ([Cox et al.](https://www.ecologyandsociety.org/vol15/iss4/art38/)). Enterprise ontology contributes a universal transaction pattern of request, promise, state and accept ([Dietz](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-33149-2))†, and accounting contributes the strand's one conservation law: double-entry has an exact algebraic form in which every transaction is a zero-sum vector ([Ellerman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1898))†.

Economics supplies the constraints. With joint output and unobserved effort, no budget-balanced sharing rule yields efficient effort, so efficiency needs an outside residual claimant ([Holmström](https://doi.org/10.2307/3003457))†; the researchers read that as a formal argument for nesting. Hierarchical trees are the efficient networks for aggregating information and delay grows logarithmically ([Radner](https://doi.org/10.2307/2951495))†, while fidelity lost at each level compounds and bounds size ([Williamson](https://doi.org/10.1086/259258))†. Serial approval rejects more good projects and parallel approval accepts more bad ones, so architecture is an error-type trade-off ([Sah & Stiglitz](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1806069))†.

The empirical shape of planetary governance is a polycentric overlay of shallow hierarchies. China governs about 1.4 billion people through 34 provincial, about 333 prefecture, 2,843 county and 38,602 township divisions ([HK Education Bureau](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/pshe/national-geography/national-geography-resource-portal/Info_Sheet_01-administrative_division-eng.pdf)), which the researchers compute as spans of roughly 8 to 14 per level. The Chinese Communist Party reports 101.29 million members in 5.43 million primary organisations ([CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-06-30/CPC-membership-reaches-101-286-mln-1OoiX3xQAog/p.html)), about 19 per base unit. Self-management systems keep a containment hierarchy and replace personal authority with rule-based authority plus an explicit rule-change process ([Holacracy Constitution](https://www.holacracy.org/constitution/5-0/)), and the evidence includes reversals: Zappos was reported in 2020 to have quietly moved away from Holacracy ([Quartz](https://qz.com/work/1776841/zappos-has-quietly-backed-away-from-holacracy/)). Dunbar's 150 is not a design constant; a reanalysis found 95% confidence intervals of 4-520 and 2-336 ([Lindenfors et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8103230)). No tradition proves its primitive set complete or minimal, and none supplies a composition operator showing that an entity built from entities obeys the same laws; law and accounting do that through consolidation and veil rules in practice, not by theorem.

### The 2026 agent frontier has converged on engineering primitives with no composition semantics

The flagship attempt at a compositional language of world models, specifications and proof certificates narrowed during the period studied. ARIA's £59m Safeguarded AI programme aborted its machine-learning track in November 2025, with its director judging it "more likely than not we'd select zero successful teams" ([ARIA update](https://ariaresearch.substack.com/p/november-update-a-safeguarded-ai)), then dropped the planned cyber-physical applications call, changed programme director in April 2026, and now describes itself as a tooling track plus cybersecurity applications ([ARIA](https://aria.org.uk/opportunity-spaces/trust-everything-everywhere/safeguarded-ai)). The categorical theory work continues and ships software, but the retreat is to domains where the world model is already formal, and "auditable multi-agent systems" is named as a target with no public deliverable.

Practice has converged on identifier, short-lived credential, capability advertisement, task, delegation token or mandate, policy, attestation and log. The IETF working-group draft on agent identity defines no new protocol and declares the translation of a mission into authorisation requirements, the policy model and identifier registries out of scope ([draft-ietf-wimse-aims](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-wimse-aims)). None of the interoperability protocols specifies how authority, budget or liability attenuate when an agent delegates onward. Measurement shows why that matters. Across 180 configurations, **independent agents amplified errors 17.2 times and centralised coordination contained this to 4.4 times** ([Kim et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08296v2)), and a taxonomy built from more than 1,600 annotated traces locates failures in specification, inter-agent misalignment and verification rather than model capability ([Cemri et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13657v3)). In repeated Cournot markets a public, enforced state-transition-sanction manifest reduced LLM collusion from a mean tier of 3.1 to 1.8 where a prompt-only prohibition gave no reliable improvement ([Pierucci et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.11369)). Information-flow guarantees are provable at a utility cost: one capability-based design solves 77% of benchmark tasks with provable security against 84% undefended ([Debenedetti et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18813)).

Open populations fail on identity first. The first open million-agent network claimed about 1.5 million agents that traced to roughly 17,000 human owners, with an exposed database key ([SecurityWeek](https://securityweek.com/security-analysis-of-moltbook-agent-network-bot-to-bot-prompt-injection-and-data-leaks/)), and on-chain governance shows voting-power Gini coefficients above 0.94 across examined protocols ([arXiv 2510.05830](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.05830)). The only governed actor systems at 10^9 scale are digital public infrastructure with a unique centrally issued identity and a very narrow vocabulary: about 1.45 billion Aadhaar numbers ([UIDAI](https://uidai.gov.in/aadhaar-dashboard/enrollment-dashboard)) and 25.47 billion UPI transactions in October 2026 ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/upi-transactions-hit-2547-billion-in-october-value-soars-to-3153-lakh-crore)). They are existence proofs of scale under strong restriction, not of open-ended compositional governance.

## The theorems price what any foundation can promise

### Self-reference is free; self-certification is not

One diagonal scheme underlies Cantor, Russell, Gödel, Tarski and Turing: in a cartesian closed category, a point-surjective map from an object onto its own function space forces every endomorphism of the target to have a fixed point ([Lawvere](http://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/15/tr15abs.html))†, and Yanofsky shows that many paradoxes and incompleteness results "fall out of the same simple scheme" ([Yanofsky](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0305282)). The notes caution that this is a common scheme and not one proof; Gödel's theorems also need arithmetisation and the derivability conditions.

The researchers separate "fractal" into three checkable claims. Closure under composition is cheap and consistent. Self-similarity is an isomorphism between a system type and a functor applied to it, solved canonically by an initial algebra for finite well-founded nesting (induction and terminating audits) or a final coalgebra for open-ended behaviour (bisimulation and safety, no termination); Leinster's general theory of self-similarity realises universal solutions as terminal coalgebras ([Leinster](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.4474)). Reflexivity, a system containing codes for itself or its own transformations, is consistent only under a restriction: partiality, stratified universes, or guarded recursion in which self-consultation consumes a time step ([Birkedal et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.3596))†. A foundation cannot claim termination guarantees and unbounded reactive behaviour for the same objects, and it cannot have a type of all systems containing itself at the same level.

The asymmetry is the key result. Kleene's recursion theorem makes self-description always available. Self-verification is blocked: by Löb's theorem a theory asserts the correctness of its own proofs only for statements it already proves ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-provability/))†. Applied to agents, an agent acting on proofs in a theory cannot approve a successor reasoning in the same theory on the grounds that the successor's proofs are sound ([Yudkowsky & Herreshoff](https://intelligence.org/files/TilingAgentsDraft.pdf))†. Two decades of work yield partial results in idealised form, such as a computable but impractical logical inductor ([Garrabrant et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.03543))†, and the probabilistic cooperation lemma that would relax the obstacle exists only as forum posts whose authors flag unconfirmed assumptions ([Alignment Forum](https://alignmentforum.org/posts/ZWhJcHPmRaXAPAK5k/probabilistic-payor-lemma), search summary only). Practice has replaced proof with empirical validation ([Zhang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954))†. The governance consequence the researchers draw is that "self-governing" rigorously means each level is checked by something it cannot fully check itself, with the regress ending in an unverified trust anchor or a cycle of partial mutual checks. Hurwicz reaches the same point from economics: a mechanism is implemented only if its enforcement is itself an equilibrium of the larger game that includes the enforcers ([Hurwicz](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aecrev/v98y2008i3p577-85.html), confirmed from a search summary; the lecture PDF was not readable). Kelsen's basic norm and Hart's rule of recognition as social fact state it from law. A foundation must name its anchors.

### Verification and optimal control fail in general and return under structure

Every non-trivial extensional property of arbitrary programs is undecidable, and the rights-leak question in the general access-matrix model is undecidable ([Harrison, Ruzzo & Ullman](https://doi.org/10.1145/360303.360333))†. Verifying a temporal property for all sizes of a parameterised family of finite-state processes is undecidable ([Apt & Kozen](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(86)90071-2)); cut-offs restore decidability only for restricted topologies and anonymous processes ([Bloem et al.](https://finkbeiner.groups.cispa.de/publications/BJKKRVW16.pdf)). The decisive variable for control is the information structure rather than the number of agents. Finite-horizon decentralised control with as few as two agents is NEXP-hard ([Bernstein et al.](https://people.cs.umass.edu/%7Eimmerman/pub/bgiz.pdf)), supervisor existence for modular plants is PSPACE-complete ([Rohloff & Lafortune](https://researchwith.njit.edu/en/publications/pspace-completeness-of-modular-supervisory-control-problems/)), and distributed synthesis is undecidable exactly when the architecture contains an information fork, two processes with incomparable knowledge ([Finkbeiner & Schewe](https://finkbeiner.groups.cispa.de/publications/atva07.pdf)). Runtime monitors that only halt enforce at most safety properties ([Schneider](https://doi.org/10.1145/353323.353382)); monitors that insert and suppress actions enforce more ([Ligatti et al.](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~dpw/papers/nonsafety05.pdf)); pure liveness is never enforceable by prevention and needs deadlines or sanctions.

Tractability returns by giving something up: restricting the language, the topology to tree-like and nested-information shapes, or the property to safety invariants. The researchers note the resulting tension: hierarchy is the tractable shape, and hierarchy is also where control loss, hub fragility and capture concentrate.

### Syntactic composition does not make properties compose

| Composes under stated conditions | Does not compose in general |
|---|---|
| Safety properties of single traces; types | Liveness and progress |
| Capability confinement (authority follows references) | Information-flow security under feedback and refinement |
| Monotone computation (CALM); invariant-confluent transactions | Incentive compatibility; equilibrium efficiency |
| Differential-privacy budgets (additive) | Fairness of individually fair components |
| Passivity; small-gain conditions | Stability under feedback |
| Double-entry conservation across entities | Collective rationality under majority aggregation |
| Linearizability (per object) | Safety of individually "safe" AI models |

The right column is documented. Possibilistic security properties are preserved by product and cascade and not generally by feedback or refinement ([McLean](https://web.mst.edu/~ff/classes/6600/mclean-sp94.pdf)), because such policies are sets of sets of traces ([Clarkson & Schneider](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/bitstream/1813/11660/4/hyperproperties-tr.pdf)). Truthfulness is lost when mechanisms are combined, and the composable surrogate, smoothness, preserves only approximate efficiency ([Syrgkanis & Tardos](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.1325))†. **Combining two individually safe models produced vulnerable code 43% of the time where neither alone exceeded 3%** ([Jones, Dragan & Steinhardt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14595)). The researchers found no general theorem that alignment fails to compose; the evidence is empirical and by analogy. Their rule is that whenever a non-compositional property has been made compositional, it was by substituting a stronger or differently shaped property with an explicit quantitative budget, and a composable surrogate for governance will be more conservative than its target. Spectral-gap undecidability ([Cubitt et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04573))† shows macro behaviour is not derivable from local rules in general, but it concerns infinite limits of constructed families, and the notes warn against citing it to claim finite organisations are unpredictable.

### Aggregation, delegation and agreement each force a declared sacrifice

Arrow, Gibbard-Satterthwaite, Sen and the List-Pettit discursive dilemma ([List & Pettit](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266267102001098))† show that small sets of reasonable axioms for aggregation are jointly unsatisfiable, and Myerson-Satterthwaite does the same for bilateral trade. Recursion makes these recur per level, so level-wise aggregation differs from global aggregation and a "fractal democracy" does not inherit the properties of its base rule. Each unit needs its own explicit, imperfect decision procedure and state, which is how corporate law treats boards.

Agreement has hard costs. Deterministic consensus is impossible under asynchrony with one crash fault ([Fischer, Lynch & Paterson](https://doi.org/10.1145/3149.214121))†, Byzantine agreement without signatures needs more than three times as many parties as faults ([Lamport, Shostak & Pease](https://doi.org/10.1145/357172.357176))†, and deterministic Byzantine agreement needs a number of messages quadratic in the fault count ([Dolev & Reischuk](https://doi.org/10.1145/2455.214112))†. Common knowledge is unattainable where delivery is not guaranteed ([Halpern & Moses](https://doi.org/10.1145/79147.79161))†, so rule changes propagate eventually and never simultaneously, and rules must be versioned. One entity cannot be prevented from presenting many identities without a logically central authority or a scarce resource ([Douceur](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-sybil-attack/))†. That bites hardest for AI agents, whose identities are cheap to mint.

### The arithmetic of 10^10 actors

The following figures are the researchers' own arithmetic on the cited bounds. Naming 10^10 actors takes 34 bits; they form about 5 × 10^19 pairs, so any per-pair cost is out of reach, and flat Byzantine agreement would cost on the order of 10^20 messages per decision. A uniform tree needs 12 levels at span 7, 10 at span 10, 7 at span 30 and 5 at span 150. Depth and diffusion are not the binding constraints: gossip informs all nodes in a number of rounds logarithmic in population ([Karp et al.](https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.2000.892324))†, a few dozen rounds here. The binding constraints are the pairwise coherency term, distortion per level (with an illustrative 90% fidelity per hand-off, 39% survives nine hand-offs, and the data-processing inequality says no higher level recovers what a lower one lost), antipodal latency of roughly 130-200 ms per round trip, and human attention. Human behavioural throughput is estimated at about 10 bits per second ([Zheng & Meister](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10234))†; the researchers compute that total human deliberate attention is roughly 1% of the bit rate of a stream in which each actor emits one 100-byte event per second. Exhaustive oversight is impossible; sampled audit works, because the number of samples needed to detect a violation of given frequency is independent of population. Fast machine-speed loops must be governed locally under pre-delegated authority, and global governance sets only slowly varying constraints.

History corroborates the theorems. Universal content-bearing foundations, from Carnap's construction of all concepts to Cyc, upper ontologies and the full Semantic Web, stalled on unbounded encoding of tacit local knowledge, no convergence on one ontology, worst-case reasoning cost and inputs from parties with incentives to shade them. Shalizi's analysis of economy-wide planning concludes it is intractable for the foreseeable future on complexity grounds and because participants misreport ([Shalizi](https://bactra.org/weblog/918.html)). The durable successes standardise a thin interface and one invariant and stay agnostic about content: the hourglass result formalises that a weaker spanning-layer specification admits more supports below and applications above ([Beck](https://doi.org/10.1145/3274770))†. Double-entry bookkeeping, legal personhood, the relational model and the shipping container have that shape. None of them delivers liveness, fairness, optimality or good incentives.

## The prior art converges on nine primitives that collapse to a narrow waist

**Everything in this section is inference from the prior art by the researchers and by this report. No cited source states this kernel, proves it minimal or complete, or demonstrates it as a working system.** The evidence for it is convergence: communities that did not read each other arrived at the same elements.

The first element is the unit behind an interface: an actor, process, component, polynomial interface, role-holder or legal person, with private state and a declared boundary of interaction. The requirement on it is closure. A composite presents the same type as its parts, stated as an operad-algebra law, a closure-under-coupling proof or a congruence result. Three traditions prove this independently, which is why the fractal requirement counts as met.

The second is the boundary with its own controller. The Erlang supervisor, the object-capability membrane, the Fractal component membrane, RINA's layer management, the cell in cloud architecture, the Holacracy circle and the legal entity are one design element seen from seven communities: every composite carries a control interface that mediates what crosses its boundary and manages what is inside, and that controller is an ordinary unit one level up. Milner's separation of place graph from link graph recurs as supervision tree against message graph and as address hierarchy against traffic graph, and the organisational evidence (people sit in state, employer, party and family at once) says containment must be a lattice or graph of entities, not one tree.

The third is authority as an unforgeable reference that only attenuates under delegation. Pi-calculus restriction, actor locality, the object-capability rule, Hohfeldian power, "speaks for" and Singh's delegable commitment describe the same thing. Attenuation-only delegation is monotone, so chains verify offline with no coordination; revocation is the non-monotone operation and needs an online check, short expiry or eventual propagation. Unrestricted creation and transfer of rights makes safety undecidable, so deployed systems restrict delegation to monotone, Datalog-like forms.

The fourth is the contract ordered by refinement, with parallel composition, conjunction and quotient. This is the composition discipline between units at every level, and its independent-implementability theorem is the same kind of result as the separation-logic frame rule: a local proof stays valid in any larger context that respects the declared footprint.

The fifth is a conserved resource ledger. Double-entry bookkeeping, resource-event-agent duality, Iris resource algebras and linear logic agree that budgets, tokens, permissions and delegated shares are resources with a conservation law. Consolidation of sub-entity ledgers is addition with elimination of internal balances, which is why legal-entity recursion is tractable in practice. Holmström's theorem adds that each level needs a named residual claimant and residual controller.

The sixth is the commitment with a lifecycle. The request-promise-state-accept transaction, the social commitment with delegate and assign operations, the joint commitment and the contract of economics are one object: a directed, public promise that can be discharged, cancelled, delegated or violated. Norms attach as typed data with a monitor semantics and a sanction clause, in the manner of the institutional grammar and Catala, and not as a primitive obligation modality. Promise theory's axiom that an agent can promise only its own behaviour matches how actors that cannot be commanded cooperate.

The seventh is monotone shared state. Only lattice-structured, mergeable state is shared without coordination; every non-monotone invariant (uniqueness, exclusion, non-negative balance, immediate revocation) is assigned to a single owning boundary, split into local quotas, or decided by a small consensus group whose outputs are then disseminated monotonically.

The eighth is the control loop by constraint. Higher levels restrict, by disabling actions and setting references and budgets, and lower levels choose within what remains. Maximal permissiveness in supervisory control, Beer's resource bargain, perceptual control theory's reference signals, subsidiarity in EU law and management by exception in knowledge hierarchies are the same prescription. Its necessary conditions are theorem-grade (requisite variety and information rate, an adequate model, conserved robustness); its accompaniments, time-scale separation between levels and a fast alarm channel that bypasses intermediate levels, are convergent heuristics. A coarse-graining counts as a legitimate higher level only when it is dynamically closed, which the researchers identify as the shared content of Simon-Ando aggregation, the observer property in hierarchical supervision and computational closure.

The ninth is the stratified rule-change rule: recognition of what counts as a valid rule or status, a procedure for change, and adjudication of disputes, with a root of acceptance that is assumed rather than derived. Hart's secondary rules, Ostrom's constitutional level, the Holacracy governance process and the meta-object protocol are instances. The safe engineering form is bounded reflection: the meta-level is a separate, smaller system with a narrow declared protocol, the base keeps running without it, and untrusted producers, including AI agents, propose artefacts that a small trusted checker verifies.

Compressed, the kernel is close to what one researcher summarised as "legal person + double-entry + capabilities + types": a narrow waist with closure under composition and a short list of invariants that provably compose, and everything else left to plural layers above. The category-theory notes rank the mathematical packaging options: Poly alone is the most minimal host for interfaces, wiring, state and self-rewiring; the double-categorical module shape is the most general and the direction institutional funding follows; "symmetric monoidal category plus lens plus operad algebra" is their common denominator and not a third option. The logic notes give the complementary packaging: a dependent type theory as trusted meta-level, a resource layer, the contract algebra as composition discipline, modalities as libraries, and a designated decidable fragment for anything checked at population scale.

### What any candidate must satisfy

The limits translate into acceptance tests, and the researchers present them as consequences of the cited theorems rather than as a published standard. A candidate states closure as a theorem of its syntax and, separately for each property, the composition operators and side conditions under which that property is preserved. It declares whether its systems are an initial algebra or a final coalgebra. It stratifies or time-guards self-evaluation and names its trust anchors along with how they are contested and replaced. Its self-modification preserves a fixed kernel of invariants or passes through an earlier or stronger checker, since fully reflective languages lose all modular reasoning ([Wand's fexpr result](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fexpr))†. It restricts what is verified, using correctness by construction, actor-supplied certificates, capability confinement and safety monitors. Its guarantees hold for all sizes by a parametric argument, through structural induction, symmetry or cut-off, because checked instances do not generalise. It designs the information structure to avoid optimal coordination across information forks. It keeps a monotone core and pays for coordination knowingly. It gives each non-compositional property a conservative surrogate with a stated budget. It declares which social-choice axiom is relaxed at each aggregation point. It assumes an identity primitive and states per-committee fault thresholds and the consequence of capture. It absorbs variety locally, moves summaries upward, audits by sampling and anticipates that any control metric will be gamed. It keeps the waist content-agnostic.

The same theorems yield red flags. Claims that a system proves its own consistency, safety or alignment; that one universal type contains everything including itself; that compositional structure implies safety at every scale without per-property theorems; that arbitrary agents or contracts are verified automatically; that an optimal decentralised policy or global plan is computed; that a mechanism is fair, efficient, strategy-proof and budget-balanced; that a network is permissionless and Sybil-proof with no identity authority or scarce resource; or that guarantees are the same at every level because the structure is self-similar, each contradict a specific result. The mirror-image claim, that Gödel or undecidability shows nothing can be verified, is equally a red flag: these are worst-case and arbitrary-program results, and restricted classes are decidable by construction.

## Fractality is proven, control is conditional, and integration is the open problem

| Requirement in the question | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Same structure at every level (closure) | Proven theorem | Operad algebras; DEVS closure; bisimulation congruence |
| Mathematically rigorous composition of behaviour | Theorem per doctrine, sometimes only lax | Structured cospans, categorical systems theory; stochastic case since 2026 |
| Local reasoning that survives context | Proven theorem | Frame rule; independent implementability; capability safety |
| Coordination-free consistency | Theorem within stated models | CALM; invariant confluence |
| Limits on any central controller | Proven theorems | Requisite variety; data-rate; Bode; NEXP, PSPACE and undecidability results |
| Recursive viable structure (five functions, algedonics) | Heuristic | Viable System Model; case studies and one proponent survey |
| Time-scale separation, near-decomposability beyond linear systems | Heuristic with a linear theorem | Simon-Ando; architecture principles |
| Self-reflection | Self-description proven; self-certification impossible at full strength | Kleene; Gödel II, Löb, Tarski |
| Successor and self-modification trust | Open problem | Tiling agents; practice uses empirical validation |
| Minimality or completeness of any primitive set | Open; no proof in any tradition | Stated as a gap in every strand |
| Control at 10^10 actors | Not demonstrated; scaling argument is theoretical | Largest formally founded artefacts are single kernels |
| The nine-element kernel | Inference from convergence | This report |

What remains unsolved is specific. First, the glue: no published formalism integrates interface, boundary, authority, resource, commitment and monotone state with a proved congruence or compositionality result. Bigraphs have structure and congruence without authority or data consistency; psi-calculi have parametric data without nesting; object-capability actor systems have authority with limited metatheory; categorical systems theory composes behaviour and has nothing yet for authority, obligation, budget or liability. Second, a calculus of delegation: no formal semantics exists for how authority attenuates and accountability propagates along chains that cross human, organisational and AI principals, and the agent protocols explicitly leave it out. Third, norms exist in three untranslated forms (natural-language constitutions inside models, analysable policy code at the tool boundary, and institutional state machines and grammars) with no refinement or soundness relation among them. Fourth, dynamic structure: no theorem says what self-rewiring organisations do, and the one attempt on hierarchical delegation was withdrawn. Fifth, quantitative emergence: no general bound on how far a lax semantics departs from strict composition, and no compositional viability theorem. Sixth, probabilistic assume-guarantee reasoning is far less developed than the Boolean theory, which matters because LLM agents are stochastic components. Seventh, parametric verification does not cover heterogeneous, hierarchical, self-modifying populations with identities, which is the actual target. Eighth, composable surrogates for alignment and incentive compatibility do not exist. Ninth, Sybil-resistant identity for AI agents without a single global authority is unsolved, and the first open agent networks failed on exactly this. Tenth, the empirical base stops early: controlled evidence on LLM-agent organisations ends at a few dozen cooperating agents and two or three levels, simulations at 10^3 to 10^6 agents are unvalidated as predictive models, and no formally founded kernel operates anywhere near 10^10 actors, while the systems that do operate at that scale are not formally founded.

Several findings rest on weaker evidence than their prominence suggests, and readers should weight them accordingly. Most classical theorems and their citations across all seven notes were stated from memory, with DOIs not resolved this session. The reported change of plan at ARIA was hearsay in one note and confirmed from ARIA's own pages in another; this report relies on the confirmed account. Vendor and analyst figures on agent populations are definitionally unstable and came from secondary sources, so they are omitted here. The absence of prior work on a single calculus spanning resource through nation is an absence after limited searches in each strand, not an exhaustive review.

## Conclusion

The question assumed that smallness, universality and control travel together. The prior art says smallness and closure travel together, and control has to be bought separately, property by property, at a price the theorems publish in advance. That reframes the research programme. The valuable next result is not a larger unifying framework but a short list of invariants (conservation, attenuating authority, attributable identity, append-only provenance, contract refinement) each shipped with its own composition theorem and its own decidable fragment, plus an honest ledger of the properties that are budgeted and sampled rather than proved. The measured behaviour of AI-agent teams already tracks classical organisation theory: verification hierarchies contain error, flat swarms amplify it, and enforced state machines succeed where instructions fail. That suggests the old results will transfer and the scarce ingredient is the composition law for authority and obligation, not new primitives.

Two implications follow for anyone building toward 10^10 mixed human and machine actors. The trust anchor is a design decision and not a defect: identity issuance, root rules and root enforcers must be named, kept few, and made contestable, because mathematics forbids deriving them from inside. And the recursion that scales is the one law, accounting and the Internet already run: a polycentric overlay of shallow hierarchies whose units are opaque to one another except through a thin interface, with decisions held at the lowest level that owns the state. A formal foundation earns its place by proving once, in the Gao-Rexford manner, that local rules of that kind yield a stated global guarantee, and by saying plainly which guarantees it does not offer.
